# Faith-reason integration lesson-quality rubric

**Audience: teachers/administrators, not a student faith score. October 2026.**
Preserves the earlier focus on faith/reason, Catholic teaching, wonder,
heritage and service while avoiding a compulsory saint/Scripture/prayer formula
for every lesson. C shapes the purpose and choices; R is explicit faith
teaching/reflection. Scientific inquiry remains evidence-based.

## Local planning criteria

| Lesson-quality criterion | 1 Beginning | 2 Developing | 3 Meets Expectation | 4 Extends Quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Faith and empirical reasoning distinguished | Religious claim presented as experimental proof, or science misstated | Connection named but distinction unclear | Scientifically accurate evidence and Catholic reflection distinguished | Teacher background anticipates a misconception and models a careful source/evidence response |
| Catholic purpose affects work | No connection or prayer alone | General value named without a classroom decision | Students consider a specific dignity, stewardship, truth or service choice | Choice is revisited using results, user needs or a tradeoff |
| Source accuracy | Invented/misattributed standard, quotation or Church claim | Source question marked but not yet resolved | Primary source checked, quoted/paraphrased accurately and linked | Appropriate context and limits are explained in teacher-friendly language |
| Inquiry and wonder | Questions suppressed or inquiry replaced by a craft | Wonder question named but no student investigation | Students observe/ask/test and reflect without forced theological inference | New student questions guide a feasible next investigation |
| Access and evidence | Participation/prayer behavior graded as belief | Flexible modes named but criteria unclear | Named objective assessed with individual oral/drawn/model/written evidence | Results inform specific access support and next teaching |

Mark optional heritage/service extensions **N/A** if not relevant to that lesson;
do not add points for more quotations, longer prayers or named scientists.
Record dimension evidence, not a /20 total suggesting official certification.
Unresolved source accuracy is a readiness blocker, not a minor score offset.

**Source pathway:** [Local Standards](../Review/Local_Standards.md) and the shared
review's authoritative source record. A primary reference for faith/reason is
[Fides et Ratio](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_jp-ii_enc_14091998_fides-et-ratio.html).
**VERIFICATION REQUIRED** for exact passage/quotation and any claimed diocesan
or state requirement. This rubric is local, not an official Church standard.

Useful prompts: "Which design choice respects the person using this?"
"What did our test show?" "How can we avoid waste?" "What remains unknown?"
Avoid "God is an engineer" as an explanatory scientific analogy.
Historical biographies, devotional comparisons and scientific evidence require
different source handling; consult background guides critically, not as proof.

If assessing a student Catholic objective, use the
[student project rubric](./Student_Project_Rubric.md) for taught reasoning.
Never grade interior belief, awe, prayer frequency or family religious practice.
