# Thinking, communication and collaboration assessment

The earlier question banks on reasoning, communication, collaboration,
creativity, curiosity and ethics remain useful purposes. Select an explicitly
taught objective, not a universal character score. **October 2026.**

| Taught objective | 1 Beginning | 2 Developing | 3 Meets Expectation | 4 Extends Learning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Justify a design choice with evidence | Choice contradicts observed evidence | Gives a relevant choice but evidence link is incomplete | Explains the choice with the specified observed result | Compares an alternative using another result or tradeoff |
| Communicate a result | Response does not yet convey the result | Conveys part of the result | Shows/tells/draws the result and relevant evidence clearly enough to interpret | Adapts explanation after a clarifying question without changing the evidence |
| Combine two ideas in a plan (only if an objective) | Plan does not yet include either discussed idea | Uses one idea, or includes both without explaining their use | Identifies each idea and shows how they contribute to the plan | Tests the combined plan and explains an improvement based on feedback |
| Revise an artistic/design idea | Change is unrelated to the purpose | Relevant revision with an incomplete explanation | Revises a taught visual/musical/design choice and explains its purpose | Compares versions and explains an intentional effect or tradeoff |
| Frame an investigable question | Question cannot yet be investigated with the available evidence | Question is relevant but too broad | States a question answerable by the planned observation/test | Refines it using results and identifies what to test next |
| Explain a privacy/stewardship choice | Does not yet recognize the taught concern | Names the concern without linking the action | Explains how a concrete choice addresses the taught concern | Compares two actions and explains consequences for people/resources |

**NE** is not yet evidenced. Teacher read-aloud, bilingual response, gesture,
scribe and motor assistance are access supports, not evidence of a lower level.
Do not score smiles, spontaneous awe, public speaking confidence, written
fluency, leadership, speed or active participation as subject mastery.
Behavior/engagement can be noted separately for support planning without
converting it into the objective's grade.

## Student/peer reflection (not a mastery grade)

Read aloud if needed: "Show one idea you changed. What result made you change
it?" / "Which idea did your partner contribute?" / "What will you test next?"
Peers provide a specific appreciative statement and question, not star ratings
of a child's personality. Teacher checks individual concept evidence separately.
