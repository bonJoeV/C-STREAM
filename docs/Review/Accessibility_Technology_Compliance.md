# Accessibility and technology compliance

## Print and digital artifact check

Before release, print every student-facing page at actual scale and verify:

- readable grade-appropriate type, spacing, contrast, and response area;
- plain language and uncluttered steps;
- directions read aloud and modeled for emerging readers;
- no meaning conveyed by color alone;
- meaningful images have alt text in digital copies and useful labels in print;
- black-and-white reproduction remains understandable; and
- show/tell/draw/build/scribe options preserve the objective.

Decorative images must not compete with directions. A support may change how a
student responds, not the scientific, design, artistic, mathematical, or ethical
idea being assessed.

## Device/external-service release record

Complete one row for every app, website, video host, account system, or connected
device. Blank or unknown fields mean **HOLD**.

The [external-service approval register](External_Service_Approval_Register.csv)
contains one controlled row for every currently named digital platform or
robot-control service. All rows remain `HOLD` until school staff enter current
terms, access, privacy, device, pretest, and approval evidence. Manufacturer
manual links without a student-facing service are still checked through the
physical kit and teacher pretest.

| Check | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Instructional value | Objective strengthened beyond the unplugged route; screen time is not the purpose |
| Accounts and age | Account owner, minimum age, consent and school-managed login |
| Data/privacy | Exact personal data, retention/deletion, sharing, advertising and tracking |
| Terms and approval | Terms version/date, school approver and approval date |
| Access | Keyboard, switch, captions, transcript/audio alternative, visual/motor supports |
| Operation | Offline capability, supported device/version, setup/login time, technical contact |
| Burden | Number of devices, charging, updates, student turns and teacher pretest |
| Equivalent fallback | Same concept/objective where possible; any deferred execution evidence named |

No required participation may depend on home internet, a personal device,
family subscription, home printing, or parent technical support. A paper
algorithm can evidence sequencing/debugging, but it cannot be scored as actual
software execution, robot operation, sensing, or digital file management.

## Current program boundary

Every device lesson must identify `None`, `Optional`, `Recommended`, or
`Required` in the lesson audit. External tools remain conditional until the
school completes the record above. Use the named low-tech route when approval or
equipment is absent and explicitly defer hardware/software-only evidence.

The [technology plan](Technology_Plan.md), [teacher artifact audit](Teacher_Artifact_Audit.csv),
and [school release checklist](School_Release_Checklist.md) are the operational
records. Actual individual accommodations and institutional approval remain
local responsibilities.
