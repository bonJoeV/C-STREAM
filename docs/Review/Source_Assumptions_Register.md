# Source and assumptions register

The authoritative source catalog, classification, target versions, effective
dates, URLs, access dates, and verification limits are in
[Standards Sources](Standards_Sources.md). Its 2026-27 Minnesota baseline was
rechecked October 5, 2026 against official MDE subject portals: 2019 science,
2007 mathematics (2022 edition begins 2027-28), 2018 arts, 2020 ELA, and 2021
social studies. CSTA 2026 remains a national framework, not a Minnesota or
Archdiocesan standard.

Public Archdiocesan mission and policy pages do not provide enough public detail
to certify this local curriculum, safety procedures, external services, or
lesson-level approval. Authorized school staff must obtain current internal
guidance. No local `CST-*` code is represented as official.

The machine-readable [assumptions register](Assumptions_Register.csv) records
each unresolved point, source/office checked, decision used for drafting, staff
question, owner, and status. Every row remains **VERIFICATION REQUIRED** until
the named evidence is entered. Do not delete a row to make the program appear
verified; close it with source, reviewer, and date.

When a standards edition changes, record the version replaced, phase-in/sunset,
reason for the target version, affected lessons, and revision risk in
[Standards Sources](Standards_Sources.md) and the [change log](Change_Log.md).
Benchmark codes must be copied exactly from the directly inspected official
edition. If no code exists, say so.
