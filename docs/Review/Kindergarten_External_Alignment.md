# Kindergarten external standards: source-verified candidates

**Checked October 4, 2026 for 2026-27.** This is progress beyond topic-level
alignment, not a school approval, observed mastery or complete subject crosswalk.

[Download the twelve candidate links](Kindergarten_External_Alignment.csv).

## What was actually verified

The workbook was downloaded directly from [Perpich, Minnesota's state arts
education agency](https://perpich.mn.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Minnesota-2018-K-12-Academic-Standards-in-the-Arts.xlsx).
Its key identifies grade **0 as Kindergarten** and the identifier positions as
arts area, grade, strand, anchor standard and benchmark. Visual arts is area
5; music is area 3. The `VisualArts` sheet's Kindergarten rows 7-19 and the
`Music` sheet's Kindergarten rows were inspected directly.

| Exact identifier | Worksheet / row | Concise paraphrase of the inspected requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 5.0.2.3.1 | MN2018ArtsEdStandardsVisualArts / 9 | Make art that communicates an idea through artistic foundations. |
| 5.0.2.4.1 | MN2018ArtsEdStandardsVisualArts / 11 | Identify choices in one's own original artwork. |
| 5.0.4.8.1 | MN2018ArtsEdStandardsVisualArts / 16 | Describe distinctions between images and real objects. |
| 3.0.2.3.1 | MN2018ArtsEdStandardsMusic / 8 | Choose a musical sound to share. |

These identifiers and their Kindergarten placement were **seen in the source**,
not manufactured from another grade or inferred from a topic.
Use the [source register](Standards_Sources.md) for the verified 2018 edition's
implementation status and source classification.

## What remains conditional

The workbook itself says the MDE-hosted Excel is the official version.
The directly inspected state-agency copy establishes its displayed codes and
content; a final **MDE-copy corroboration and school curriculum review** remain
required before a formal program-wide official-alignment claim.

Each proposed link was matched to the rewritten lesson's actual directions
and evidence check. It is not a claim that pupils already demonstrated the
benchmark. A table row must meet its stated scope:

- Original art requires a child-created or child-directed representation.
  Merely selecting a supplied picture does not prove art creation.
- A stated choice must concern the child's own artwork. Counting points or
  watching a teacher is not sufficient.
- Image/object distinctions are visual-arts evidence, not proof of full
  science-standard coverage.
- The music link requires a student-directed choice and sharing of an actual
  musical sound. Silent gestures or only rests can satisfy the local pattern
  route but do not establish that external sound-sharing benchmark.

The CSV status deliberately separates **source verification** from
institutional review and classroom outcomes. The generated local-standard
map retains its general external-alignment verification hold and links here
for these narrowly checked candidates.
