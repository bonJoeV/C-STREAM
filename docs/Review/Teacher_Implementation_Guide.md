# Teacher implementation guide

## 1. Select a year that fits

Use the [instructional time baseline](Instructional_Time_Baseline.md): **28
weekly meetings (24 core + 4 flex)** or **15 bi-weekly meetings (13 core + 2
flex)** until the school enters its actual calendar. Select one A/B rotation;
do not combine schedules. Use the [core unit plans](Core_Unit_Plans.md) and
[lesson map](Lesson_Map.csv) to choose lessons whose objectives fit the unit.

## 2. Find the authoritative materials

1. [Review index](README.md): all program decisions and release status.
2. [Grade reviews](README.md#program-decisions-and-evidence): baseline concerns
   and revised lessons.
3. [Teacher artifact audit](Teacher_Artifact_Audit.csv): repository artifact
   evidence and applicability decisions.
4. Actual lesson: timing, supplies, sequence, evidence, fallback, cleanup.
5. [School release checklist](School_Release_Checklist.md): physical/policy gate.

Generated `site/` pages are publication output; edit `docs/`, not generated HTML.

## 3. Build and label kits

Default teams are at most three unless a lesson specifies pairs. Label every bin:

```text
C-STREAM | grade/rotation | unit | lesson
Enrollment / active teams / spare kits:
Issue: item and count
Return: item and count
Consumables to replace:
Teacher-only items:
Dry/charge/repair before closing:
Checked by / date:
```

Keep batteries, magnets, sharp tools, chemicals, SDS records, and restricted
equipment in teacher-only storage. Provide drying and charging space only where
the selected lesson requires it. Count and pretest; a catalog row is not stock.

## 4. Print and prepare

- Confirm the selected lesson still passes the freshly generated
  [artifact audit](Teacher_Artifact_Audit.csv).
- Print student pages at actual scale and test black-and-white legibility,
  contrast, response space, and non-color directions.
- Prepare the board/chart exactly; attach expected responses and misconceptions
  to the teacher copy.
- Build/test the worked and finished example where applicable.
- Put unfinished-work folders and return/count cards in the kit.
- Prepare the indoor, no-device, and missing-supply route before class.
- Complete safety/SDS, accessibility, service/account, and local approval checks.

## 5. First week

1. Teach stop, tool-passing, material-return, and show/try/tell routines.
2. Use Unit 1 observation/evidence work; do not begin with account setup.
3. Explain that engineering failure is information, not failure of the child.
4. Collect one brief individual demonstration in an accessible mode.
5. Time setup, transitions, evidence, and cleanup; record one revision.
6. Send the family objective/evidence summary; assign no purchase or routine
   homework.

## 6. Record progress with low burden

Use one roster row per student and objective:

```text
Date | lesson/objective/local code | actual evidence | 1/2/3/4 or NE |
access support | feedback/next check
```

Shared levels are **1 Beginning, 2 Developing, 3 Meets Expectation, 4 Extends
Learning, NE Not Yet Evidenced**. Do not score handwriting, spelling, artistic
talent, reading speed, public-speaking confidence, participation, or faith
practice unless that exact skill is the objective. Expected mastery requires two
dated observations in changed contexts, not one team product.

## 7. After class and annually

Count, dry, disconnect, recharge, repair, replace, and store exactly as the kit
label requires. File or recycle unfinished work according to the lesson. Record
consumption, actual timing, missing evidence, access/safety issues, and defects.
Use the [change log](Change_Log.md) and annual procedure; never store
student-identifying data in this public repository.
