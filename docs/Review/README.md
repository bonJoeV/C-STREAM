## Before scheduling

1. Choose **one schedule** and **one rotation**, not all available tracks.
2. Check `revision`, technology, materials and open concerns in the lesson audit.
3. Use the local progression to select the younger/older grade expectation.
4. Prepare the actual kit and verify accommodations and school safety procedures.
5. Collect one individual piece of evidence, even when students work in teams.
6. Record what students demonstrated; do not infer mastery from participation.

**Hold any unresolved P0 activity.** Substitute a documented safe lesson that
meets a stated objective rather than improvising with unknown materials,
unavailable equipment or unsuitable weather.
