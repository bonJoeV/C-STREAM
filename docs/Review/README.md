# 2026-27 curriculum review

**Reviewed beginning October 4, 2026.** This is a local curriculum-development
review, not an Archdiocesan approval or certification of official standards
coverage.

[Current generated program totals](Program_Summary.csv) are regenerated with
the lesson/material maps rather than inferred from filenames or old report
snapshots. They describe documents and planned evidence/use—not observed
student outcomes or physical school holdings.

Start with the [executive review](Executive_Review.md), then choose the relevant
grade review and read the actual lesson before teaching. The audits distinguish
baseline concerns from revised lessons; complete written instructions do not
replace a counted kit, local safety/access checks or an actual classroom trial.

All **251 K-6 documents** have revised lesson pathways, but the expanded
[teacher-artifact audit](Teacher_Artifact_Readiness.md) holds every lesson until
missing physical/written supports are resolved. Do not interpret a revised
lesson, generated map, or passing schema as classroom/substitute release.

## Before scheduling

1. Choose **one schedule** and **one rotation**, not all available tracks.
2. Check `revision`, technology, materials and open concerns in the lesson audit.
3. Confirm the lesson still passes the generated artifact audit.
4. Use the local progression to select the younger/older grade expectation.
5. Prepare the actual kit and verify accommodations and school safety procedures.
6. Collect one individual piece of evidence, even when students work in teams.
7. Record what students demonstrated; do not infer mastery from participation.

**Hold any unresolved P0 activity.** Substitute a documented safe lesson that
meets a stated objective rather than improvising with unknown materials,
unavailable equipment or unsuitable weather.
