## Annual review procedure

| When | Responsible role | Required action and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| May-June | Curriculum lead + at least one teacher who taught the material | Review teacher notes, evidence trends, failed/held pathways and family/access concerns |
| June | Curriculum lead | Recheck MDE implementation pages, CSTA edition, public OMCE/Archdiocesan guidance and all exact benchmark mappings |
| June-July | Materials coordinator/business office | Count/inspect stock; update prices, pack sizes, lead times, loss/wear, SDS and recurring budget |
| August | Grade teachers + safety/technology leads | Select schedule, confirm core artifact coverage, resolve school release holds, pretest kits/services and approve accommodations |
| Each term | Teacher | Record actual prep/cleanup, individual evidence, defects, consumption and needed revisions |
| After revision | Second teacher/substitute reviewer | Re-run affected artifact, safety, accessibility and simulation checks |
| Before publication | Curriculum lead/principal | Approve changes, accepted exceptions and the dated release status |

Store school pilot records with the locally controlled curriculum evidence; do
not place student-identifying information in this public repository.
