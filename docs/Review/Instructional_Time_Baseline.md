# Instructional time baseline

**Target school year: 2026-27.** This baseline controls scope until the school
replaces each assumption with its calendar and staffing facts. It is deliberately
more conservative than counting every numbered week.

## Known repository evidence

| Band | Native period | Existing alternatives |
|---|---:|---|
| K | 25 minutes | Weekly single track or 17-session bi-weekly track |
| 1-2 | 30 minutes | Weekly or bi-weekly, Year A or B |
| 3-4 | 40 minutes | Weekly or bi-weekly, Year A or B |
| 5-6 | 45 minutes | Weekly or bi-weekly, Year A or B |

The weekly planner has 34 numbered slots, two of which are already non-teaching
breaks. The repository does not establish the school's actual bell/calendar,
sections, staffing model, Mass/testing/field-trip dates, or snow-day policy.

## Controlling assumptions

Every row below is **ASSUMPTION - VERIFY LATER** and **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.

| Question | Drafting decision | Staff question |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | One native period weekly, or one every other week; never both | Which schedule and A/B rotation will each grade use? |
| Instructional weeks | 34 possible weekly slots | How many C-STREAM dates appear on the adopted calendar? |
| Holiday loss | Two weekly slots already reserved as breaks; one bi-weekly meeting may be displaced | Which holidays close school or interrupt this special? |
| Mass loss | Reserve one weekly meeting | Does C-STREAM move, shorten, or cancel on school Mass days? |
| Testing loss | Reserve one weekly meeting | Which grades and dates displace specials for testing? |
| Field-trip loss | Reserve one weekly meeting | Which trips affect each section? |
| Minnesota snow loss | Reserve one weekly meeting; one combined interruption reserve in a bi-weekly schedule | Is e-learning used, and is this special rescheduled? |
| Grade sections | One section per grade/band for planning; not established | How many sections and pupils will be served? |
| Delivery model | Standalone special with optional classroom connections | Is it a special, integrated classroom time, or both? |
| Instructor | One school-designated adult | Who teaches, prepares kits, records evidence, and approves substitutes? |

The weekly calculation is `34 slots - 2 planned breaks - 1 Mass - 1 testing -
1 field trip - 1 snow/closure = 28 meetings`. Holiday loss is represented by
the two planned breaks. The bi-weekly calculation is `17 scheduled meetings -
1 holiday - 1 combined Mass/testing/trip/snow reserve = 15 meetings`.

## Annual capacity

| Band | Weekly realistic meetings | Weekly annual minutes | Bi-weekly realistic meetings | Bi-weekly annual minutes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| K | 28 | **700** | 15 | **375** |
| 1-2 | 28 | **840** | 15 | **450** |
| 3-4 | 28 | **1,120** | 15 | **600** |
| 5-6 | 28 | **1,260** | 15 | **675** |

These minutes include setup directions, transitions, individual evidence, and
cleanup. They do not permit 32 or 17 core meetings plus extra projects.

## Scope rule

Plan **24 core + 4 flex/recovery meetings** for a weekly year, or **13 core + 2
flex/recovery meetings** for a bi-weekly year. Flex meetings absorb lost dates,
retesting, reteaching, exhibitions, and unfinished multi-meeting work. If the
school verifies more time, add recommended lessons only after core evidence is
secure. If less time is available, reduce product complexity; never remove
safety, individual assessment, or cleanup.

Update this page, the [scope and sequence](Scope_and_Sequence.md), and the
[teacher implementation guide](Teacher_Implementation_Guide.md) together when
the school supplies actual calendar facts.
