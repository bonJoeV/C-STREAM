# Safety risk protocol

This protocol applies to every hands-on pathway. It does not replace school,
Archdiocesan, insurer, facility, manufacturer, or emergency policy. Those local
requirements are **VERIFICATION REQUIRED** before release.

## Risk classification

**Severity:** Low (minor reversible discomfort), Moderate (first aid or material
damage), Serious (professional medical evaluation or significant injury), Severe
(life-threatening or permanent harm).

**Likelihood:** Unlikely, Possible, Likely, Frequent.

| Initial risk | Minimum response |
|---|---|
| Low + Unlikely/Possible | Written handling and cleanup directions |
| Moderate or Likely | Adult pretest, active supervision, specific prevention and response |
| Serious at any likelihood | Eliminate/substitute where possible; named teacher-only controls and school safety approval |
| Severe, or Serious that remains Possible/Likely | Prohibit the pathway and use a safer objective-equivalent alternative |

For each hazard record: activity and stage; who may be harmed; severity;
likelihood; prevention; adult ratio; teacher-only steps; PPE; storage; spill,
breakage, burn, cut, allergy, ingestion, and disposal response; residual risk;
approver/date. “Be careful” is not a control.

## Non-negotiable controls

- Use safety glasses where debris, splash, liquid, stored force, or breakage
  could reach eyes. Verify fit and school cleaning practice.
- Use only protected low-voltage classroom power approved for the actual kit.
  No mains wiring, exposed hot parts, damaged leads, or improvised chargers.
- Store batteries and magnets under adult control. A suspected swallowed button
  cell or magnet requires immediate emergency escalation under school procedure.
- Do not eat experimental materials. Check food/allergy and mouthing risks.
- Teachers perform cutting, heating, piercing, chemical mixing, and other
  restricted steps unless written school policy explicitly permits the age/tool.
- Stop and isolate spills, breakage, overheating, smoke, damaged power, or an
  unaccounted battery/magnet. Contact the school's designated responsible adult.

## Safety Data Sheets

Any commercial chemical product—including adhesive, paint, cleaner, spray,
solvent, lab material, or mixture—requires the current manufacturer Safety Data
Sheet, exact product identity, review date, storage location, and school approval
in the [SDS register](Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Generic names do not
establish equivalence. **No registered SDS means the product pathway is held.**

## Prohibited K-6 core materials and tools

| Item | Why prohibited | Grades | Safer alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Button/coin cells | Ingestion can cause severe internal injury | K-6 | Protected AA classroom pack with adult-controlled cells |
| Loose neodymium magnets | Multiple-magnet ingestion and pinch risk | K-6 core; especially K-2 | Large encapsulated ferrite magnets after age/policy review |
| Latex balloons | Allergy, choking, fragments, unpredictable breakage | K-6 | Paper membrane, sealed bag, or teacher-controlled commercial apparatus |
| Open flame, hot plate, hot glue, soldering iron | Burn/fire/fume burden | K-6 student use | Cold fasteners/tape; teacher-prepared component if policy permits |
| Utility/craft knives, razor blades, needles | Laceration/puncture | K-6 student use | Blunt scissors, pre-cut parts, hole punch used by adult |
| Projectile launchers or compressed-air builds | Eye/impact injury and space burden | K-6 | Seated rolling, drop, or low-force enclosed test |
| Unknown powders/liquids or real forensic samples | Ingestion, allergy, accusation and contamination | K-6 | Labeled inert teacher-prepared fictional evidence cards |
| Mains electricity or stripped wire on mains circuits | Shock/fire | K-6 | Enclosed low-voltage classroom modules |
| Unlabeled cleaners, paints, adhesives or sprays | Unknown ingredients/fumes; no SDS | K-6 | School-approved exact product with current SDS |
| Food tasting as investigation | Allergy, hygiene and coercion | K-6 | Visual/model data or sealed teacher demonstration |

## Local compliance gate

For every scheduled lesson, the school must confirm policy, insurance, room
capacity, storage, cleaning/disposal, emergency response, accommodations, and
the supervision ratio. Until confirmed, record **VERIFICATION REQUIRED** and
hold that pathway. Replace any activity whose residual risk cannot be reduced
to the school's approved level.
