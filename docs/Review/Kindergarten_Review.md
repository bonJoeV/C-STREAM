  Withdrawn high-shelf/pet/infant challenges, height races, unsafe sound options,

  absent from the rewritten minimum pathways.
- Supplied each reference's objective, purpose, quantities, setup, background,
  misconception/answer, timed sequence, individual evidence, support/challenge,
  safety, troubleshooting, indoor route, cleanup and family copy.

  school display, Thanksgiving, school-community art and weather.

  not another unrestricted helper craft.

  school-community lessons. No real personal data are collected.
- Made practice, service algorithms and privacy/community lessons CORE.
  Current scope is **30 CORE, 6 RECOMMENDED, 1 OPTIONAL** across alternatives.
- Normalized every K primary-material term to an explicit catalog entry;
  neither a name match nor a zero additional assembly price proves ownership.

### End-of-K evidence and handoff

| Competency | Evidence sought, not automatic mastery |
|---|---|

| Art | Represent a detail/idea; identify a purposeful visual/rhythm choice and clarify communication |

Use the [local progression](Learning_Progression.md) and individual evidence

supports are allowed. Participation, portfolios and certificates alone are
not Grade 1 readiness. Missing evidence remains unobserved/not yet.

## 5. Materials, grouping and reported holdings

Use [K reference routines](../Resources/Kindergarten_Reference_Routines.md).
10/15/20/25 pupils form **5/7/10/12 active pair/trio teams**; budget
**5/8/10/13 kits**, including one reserve at odd enrollment.
Use no singleton "pair." Limited-kit groups <=3 require 4/5/7/9 kits with
every child receiving a full decision/test turn.

Exact quantities live in each lesson; do not issue nine generic kits to all
pair-based lessons. Examples: 260 blocks for a full structure allocation;
15 germination cups/36 seeds; 4.2 L contained water; 104 child/teacher life-cycle
cards. Timings include individual checks and cleanup, but freezing, charging,
ongoing plant care and actual school hygiene rules require advance arrangements.

The [expanded catalog](Materials_Inventory.csv) and
[material-usage index](Material_Usage.csv) now identify every K primary term.
Optional robots/hosts remain explicit in the lessons and technology plan, not
mandatory primary-material purchases. Paper resources are assembled from
separately priced stock; zero additional item-purchase cost is not free paper
or zero teacher preparation. Budgets do not guarantee every K pair-based kit
or all annual consumables for a 32-meeting year.
