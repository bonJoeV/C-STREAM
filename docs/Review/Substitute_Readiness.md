# Substitute readiness review

> expanded [teacher-artifact audit](Teacher_Artifact_Readiness.md). All 251
> lessons remain on substitute-release hold until missing artifacts and the

**October 2026.** A revised template is not certification of every lesson.
This workstream owns shared operations, not the four band lesson audits.

named local contact and successful prep test. Classroom outcomes, inventory
condition, staff availability and school policies remain unverified.

of documents and assumptions. No substitute-arrival trial, pupil classroom test,
observed teacher-prep timing or actual kit-capacity trial was performed.
Future READY status requires the named local checks; it is not an outcome
already established by this review.

conditional references. The remaining release gate concerns actual kits,
permissions, accommodations and trials, not a queue of missing outlines.

## What changed in shared operations

Baseline problems: the old substitute guide assumed nonexistent quick-reference
cards and "likely" equipment locations, treated paper as an equivalent for
programming, and had no complete classroom cleanup/evidence allocation.
The updated [substitute guide](../Resources/Substitute_Teacher_Guide.md) supplies
the actual 20-minute gate, native-period fallback plans and safety escalation.
The [handoff template](../Templates/Substitute_Lesson_Template.md) attaches a
complete [lesson plan](../Templates/Lesson_Plan_Template.md), not a second format.
Shared kits and actual stock assumptions live in [Materials Plan](./Materials_Plan.md).

## Grade 3, twenty minutes before class

Use this actual target, maintained by the band owner:
[Week 10: Bridge Engineering](../Lessons/Grades_3-4_YearA/Week10_Bridge_Engineering.md).
Use the rebuilt lesson's **exact** span, loads, issue quantities and individual
check; the generic structures kit is not permission to change those silently.
Do not read its old title as proof of readiness.

| Preparation window | Substitute action | Evidence of readiness |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 minutes | Read the linked lesson's at-a-glance objective, background, misconception and 40-minute sequence | Can state the objective and individual success condition |
| 4-8 | Locate the labeled kit and count actual enrollment; this bridge uses 12 active pair/trio teams at 25, with a 13-kit supply budget | 26 books, 28 paper sheets, 260 identical counters, 13 cups/trays/team rulers, 5.2 m tape, 25 evidence slips and the specified teacher reserves are present |
| 8-12 | Review safety/accommodations; confirm office contact/stop procedure; inspect supports and loads | No damaged/sharp/loose unsafe items; no hidden teacher-only task |
| 12-16 | Run the lesson's prep test and prepare trays/roles | Test result is observable; setup can be safely supervised |
| 16-20 | Draw/read aloud steps; rehearse one exit prompt, return counts and fallback | Individual roster and 40-minute cleanup/evidence allocation ready |

If the exact kit/pretest is unavailable, explicitly teach the paper comparison
fallback in the [substitute guide](../Resources/Substitute_Teacher_Guide.md)
instead. Tell the regular teacher which bridge objectives/evidence were deferred.
The nine-team generic structures kit is not the complete pair-based bridge
kit. The fallback uses groups of up to three and its own quantities and
test, so record the changed objective rather than silently changing the
bridge's span, loading method or team allocation.
Do not add unsourced theological metaphors, ratio mathematics or unfamiliar
electric equipment to rescue a lesson.

**Readiness states:** READY after local checks; CONDITIONAL with a named,
tested support/fallback; NOT READY with unresolved safety, material, instruction
or assessment blockers. The link and a document simulation do not establish
school-wide READY status.

## Required local checks before handoff

- Verify school contact/emergency arrangements, accommodations and actual kit
  shelf locations; this review invents none.
- Verify protected electrical ratings, secure covers, age suitability,
  ingredients and counts. No button cells, latex balloons or projectiles;
  no neodymium magnets in K. Suspected battery/magnet ingestion requires
  immediate school emergency escalation.
- Confirm the current rebuilt bridge instructions and physical kit agree.
  Band audits record document-level revision evidence, not classroom testing
  or official certification; this operations review does not replace them.
- Reconcile the four available [audit CSVs](./Materials_Plan.md#legacy-inventory-reconciliation-and-remaining-checks)
  by lesson path; record substitute/safety/technology blockers. The Grade 3
  band owner has documented the rebuilt bridge reference and conservative
  13-kit supply budget; verify the actual local kit rather than using the
  earlier as-read snapshot. The audit is not classroom certification.
  All 251 packages passed the actual written-reference checks; do not infer
  physical classroom release merely because those document checks passed.
- Check [Local Standards](./Local_Standards.md) definitions, faith sources and
  official alignment; unverified Church or supplier claims are
  **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.

The substitute leaves actual individual evidence/NE, accomplished steps,
objective changes, missing/broken supplies, safety issues and follow-up questions.
