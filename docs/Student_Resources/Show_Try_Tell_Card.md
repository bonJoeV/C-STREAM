# Show, Try, Tell

**Teacher/substitute:** This is an adult-led card, not independent reading.
Use it with the actual lesson objective and kit. A printed copy is optional.
Draw the three boxes on the board or place three real objects as visual cues.
Say ONE step, show it, let the child do it, then repeat. No homework.

## Ready-to-use comparison example (K/nonreader)

**Objective:** "I can show which of two objects is longer."
**Materials:** Two ordinary school pencils of visibly different lengths, each
child's familiar drawing tool/paper optional. No small new parts or devices.

1. Hold up the two pencils. Say "Look." Put them side by side with ends aligned.
2. Model pointing along a pencil. Say "Show the longer one." Let each child point.
3. Reverse left/right order, realign ends. Say "Show me again. How do you know?"
   Accept tracing, pointing at the unmatched end, gesture or words; adult scribes.

**Meets:** Child identifies the longer pencil in both placements and indicates
the unmatched end/aligned comparison. If position alone determines the answer,
model aligned ends and recheck later. Do not score reading, handwriting or speech.
This is a local classroom criterion, not a verified official benchmark.

**Support:** Larger objects, extra wait time, adult holds objects steady, child
uses gaze/gesture or home-language response. **Challenge:** Compare a third
object and explain the order. **Catholic connection:** Care for shared tools
and use them fairly; no scientific proof claim or forced comparison of God to
an engineer. **Safety:** No sharpening/swinging; keep tools at desk level.
