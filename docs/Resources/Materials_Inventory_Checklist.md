# Materials inventory checklist

The authoritative October 2026 planning records are
[Materials Plan](../Review/Materials_Plan.md) and
[Materials Inventory CSV](../Review/Materials_Inventory.csv). They include
normalized names/aliases, unit estimates, 10/15/20/25 stock, annual replacement,
storage/safety/batteries, exact kits and $250/$500/$1,000/$2,500 bills of materials.
This checklist does not maintain a contradictory price or loan-stock table.

## Reported school holdings

[Reported OLP holdings](../Review/Materials_Plan.md#reported-olp-holdings)
records **8 individual Snap Circuits STEM Classroom Activity Kits, item OHM-135**,
reported on 2026-10-04. This is a school-reported total, not a completed
physical inspection. Keep actual holdings separate from the CSV's
10/15/20/25-pupil planning quantities; check contents, condition, location,
manufacturer age guidance and battery/power safety before classroom use.
For the four selected Grades 3-6 lessons, use the
[Snap Circuits classroom guide](./Snap_Circuits_Classroom_Guide.md):
label SC01-SC08, record the matching manual/project and fitted cell count,
and release only checked kits. At 25 pupils use nine teams at five stations
in two waves, not nine simultaneous kits or groups larger than three.

## Beginning-of-year stock check

- [ ] Confirm planned schedule/rotation and actual cohorts/enrollment.
- [ ] Count physical stock by normalized ID; record condition/location/date/checker.
      NOT VERIFIED in the CSV is not zero and is not confirmed ownership.
- [ ] Check school basics assumed by budgets: 25 pencils, 25 blunt scissors,
      crayons/markers, board, 18 stable supports, teacher timer, cleaning/handwashing.
- [ ] Inspect every reusable kit; complete counted labels and an actual shelf map.
- [ ] Verify supplier packs, current prices, shipping/tax assumptions and yearly
      consumables. Estimates dated October 2026 are NOT vendor quotations.
- [ ] Verify electrical current protection/ratings, secure battery covers,
      ingredients, age suitability and accessible response tools before purchasing.
- [ ] Choose only confirmed optional devices/loans. No app license or school
      account is assumed. [Technology Plan](../Review/Technology_Plan.md).
- [ ] Schedule one-time kit/card preparation and teacher prep tests before
      substitutes arrive; note shortages explicitly.

## Before each unit / after each lesson

Use [Materials List Template](../Templates/Materials_List_Template.md).
Separate per-student, team, class, teacher-only and optional materials.
Nine complete kits accommodate 25 at teams <=3; if switching to pairs,
recalculate for 13 pairs. The selected Snap lessons instead use their explicit
two-wave allocation. Count fitted/spare batteries separately.
Issue exact amounts, record actual use, count returns, dry wet supplies,
disconnect/off electrical parts and remove cells for long storage.
Mark damaged kits OUT OF SERVICE and notify the responsible adult.

## Safety and optional donations

No button cells, latex balloons or projectile activities in the core;
no neodymium magnets in K. Do not use unverified electrical parts, loose
high-strength magnets, unknown chemicals, sharp metal/glass or food-soiled
recyclables. Donations of screened clean dry cardboard are welcome but the
school provides paper/cardstock alternatives. Never require families to bring
materials or buy equipment.

**Optional request copy:** "If convenient, you may donate clean, dry flat
cardboard after checking with the teacher. Please send no glass, metal, batteries,
magnets, chemical containers or food-soiled items. Donations are optional:
every child will have school-provided materials."

## Loan and audit evidence limits

[CSCOE planning](./CSCOE_Library_Planning_Guide.md) requires confirmation; there
is no guaranteed free loan, fixed stock count or two-week entitlement.
Four band audits are available; their as-read counts and specific stock questions
are summarized in [Materials Plan](../Review/Materials_Plan.md). Current
inventory use counts are explicitly estimated core uses; coverage of all
251 legacy lesson documents has not been established.
