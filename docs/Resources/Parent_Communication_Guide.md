# Parent communication guide

Use one [family communication system](../Review/Family_Communication.md)
and [newsletter template](../Templates/Family_Newsletter_Template.md); K-6
unit examples and specific oral questions are supplied there. Retain plain
language, welcoming invitations, positive evidence notes and family feedback.
No routine homework or donation prerequisite. **October 2026.**

## Introduction letter (copy and personalize)

Dear Families,

In C-STREAM, children learn through observing, building, measuring, testing,
improving, composing and explaining. C means Catholic identity; R is
religion/faith integration; A includes arts and music; M means mathematics.
Our Catholic purpose includes dignity, stewardship, truthful reasoning and
using gifts to serve others. Faith reflection does not replace scientific
evidence. Art includes genuine choices about pattern, form, sound and
communication, not just decorating a product.

We use hands-on materials first and devices when they add meaningful learning.
Every child has school-provided supplies and classroom work time. There is
no routine C-STREAM homework, purchase, device or donation requirement.
Newsletters include a specific question you can ask and an optional no-purchase
activity. Your child may show learning by speaking, pointing, drawing, modeling
or recording, with needed access support.

Questions? Contact [actual teacher/school-approved contact].

## Positive evidence note

Dear [Family],
Today [child] demonstrated [objective] by [actual action/explanation].
Next we will practice [specific next step]. Thank you for encouraging curiosity.
[Teacher/contact]

Keep individual notes private using the school's actual approved method.
Do not publicly post names, scores, photographs or identifiable work without
verified school consent/privacy arrangements.

## Showcases and family feedback

Use [Student Project Rubric](../Rubrics/Student_Project_Rubric.md) for selected
objective-linked evidence, not fixed percentages for attractive displays,
confidence, family-funded materials or participation. Work is completed in
class; family attendance is optional. Students may show/tell/draw with support.
Ask families: "Was the learning clear?" "Was the optional activity accessible?"
"What questions remain?" Event planning: [STREAM Night](./STREAM_Night_Guide.md).

Source questions about Church teaching, scientists, official standards or
diocesan policy are **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Local codes link to
[Local Standards](../Review/Local_Standards.md); they are not official accreditation.
