# Weekly parent sheet template

Use the [common newsletter template](../Templates/Family_Newsletter_Template.md)
at unit launch/completion or for a major lesson. The
[family communication system](../Review/Family_Communication.md) supplies
ready-to-adapt examples for **K, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6**.

## Copy for families

**This week/unit in C-STREAM - grade/unit/date:** [ ]
**What we explored:** [ ]
**What students did:** [Actual observation/build/test/measure/compose/program;
say unplugged if no program executed.]
**What they learned:** [Specific concept and evidence.]
**Our Catholic connection:** [Actual dignity/stewardship/service decision.]
**Ask your child:** "[Specific question answerable orally.]"
**Try this at home, optional:** [Short no-purchase safe activity; no device,
account, outdoor exposure, photo, submission or special equipment required.]
**Coming next/contact:** [Confirmed school-approved information.]

C is Catholic identity; R is religion/faith integration; A is arts including
music; M is mathematics. No comparisons that make God an engineer/scientist or
turn a physical result into theological proof. No compulsory scientist spotlight.
Church quotations/biographies require verification before copying.

No routine homework. School supplies all required learning materials;
donations and optional activities never affect participation or grades.
No identifiable student names/images/scores without the verified school
consent/privacy process.
