# C-STREAM FAQ and troubleshooting

**October 2026.** Start with [Quick Start](./Quick_Start_Guide.md) and a completed
[lesson template](../Templates/Lesson_Plan_Template.md).
Generalist teachers can teach a clear lesson; not every legacy file is ready.
Confirm rotation/calendar and the actual lesson rather than promising no
repetition or universal substitute-readiness.

## Time and classroom management

**Running long?** Stop new construction, retain the individual check and cleanup,
store dry labeled work and continue next meeting. Do not delete evidence
collection to add an extra activity or prayer.

**Finished early?** Ask for a repeated fair test, alternative constraint or
explanation of evidence. Faster pupils are not automatically unpaid helpers.

**Students excited/arguing?** Give instructions before materials, use a stop
signal, assign builder/tester/reporter and rotate actual turns. Ask each to show
an idea and test alternatives under the same conditions. Refer persistent
behavior/safety concerns through actual school procedures.

**A child needs more time?** Model one step, reduce choices, pre-cut/larger
materials, allow gesture/voice/scribe/quiet or seated response. Keep the concept
criterion unless documenting an explicit objective change.

## Missing supplies or technology

| Problem | Safe response | Learning limit |
|---|---|---|
| Building blocks absent | Paper/cardstock/sticks with a stated new constraint | Compare evidence; do not silently claim identical load capacity |
| Robot/app/host unavailable | Desk-grid arrow cards and pointer | Sequence/debugging concepts only; execution/robot mastery deferred |
| Internet absent | Teacher-vetted read-aloud source or confirmed offline tool | Actual online/source-navigation objective may change |
| Some devices work | Document turn/reset time, smaller test rotations or more meetings | Each child needs individual real-device evidence if that is O1 |
| Electrical gear damaged | Switch off/disconnect; OUT OF SERVICE; safe paper-path model | Paper path is not practical circuit-building mastery |
| No safe materials or weather | Seated observation fallback | Record actual observation objective, not unperformed investigation |

Do not open personal accounts, install unapproved apps, bypass safeguards or
improvise power supplies. [Technology Plan](../Review/Technology_Plan.md).
Borrowing lead times/fees/stock are **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**, not guaranteed
two-week free access. [Library planning](./CSCOE_Library_Planning_Guide.md).

## Assessment

**How do I grade?** Use the objective -> prompt -> success condition ->
individual evidence chain. [Project rubric](../Rubrics/Student_Project_Rubric.md).
Participation, effort, handwriting, neatness and confidence are not mastery.
An artistic criterion is used only for an explicit arts objective.

**What if the model fails?** A child can still explain valid evidence and redesign.
A successful team model does not prove each member understands. **NE** means
not yet evidenced; unobserved/absent/device-blocked is not a zero.

**How much paperwork?** Short roster evidence during work plus one exit check;
review termly with [Progress Tracker](./Student_Progress_Tracker.md).
Named records/photos follow verified school privacy/consent practice; do not
assume a digital account or public portfolio.

## Faith, arts and grade differences

**Faith connection feels forced?** Use a concrete Catholic decision about dignity,
truth, stewardship or service. R identifies the explicit reflection. Prayer
supports but does not substitute for this purpose. Verify Church quotations,
biographies and standards; never make an experiment theological proof.

**Hard science/faith question?** Acknowledge uncertainty, distinguish observation
from belief/interpretation and consult the verified source record or appropriate
school faith educator. Avoid a simplistic "science only how, faith only why"
rule or "God is an engineer" analogy.

**What does A mean?** Arts including music: observational drawing, composition,
visual communication, form, rhythmic phrases and performance, not just digital
art/decoration. M means mathematics.

**K cannot read?** Use [Show, Try, Tell](../Student_Resources/Show_Try_Tell_Card.md).
An attempt is welcome but does not automatically demonstrate the concept.
Assess pointing/sorting/building/telling with the same explicit success condition.

**Mixed grades / older students need challenge?** Same materials can support
different evidence expectations; use the shared progression and actual
prerequisites. Add repeat trials, constraints, measurement or tradeoffs, not
untaught ratio work or leadership as a substitute for subject learning.

## Safety and families

No button cells, latex balloons or projectiles in the core; no neodymium magnets
in K. Stop unsafe activities, isolate hazards and contact the school's responsible
adult. Possible battery/magnet ingestion needs urgent emergency escalation.
Use indoor alternatives in unsafe Minnesota conditions.

No routine homework, required donations or home purchase/device. Explain
concept + action + evidence + Catholic choice using
[family communication](../Review/Family_Communication.md).
