# CSCOE library planning guide

Preserve the useful browse/reserve/check/return workflow.
[Library entry point](https://cscoe.myturn.com/library/).
**VERIFICATION REQUIRED** for current availability, eligibility, fees, loan
periods, transport, account process, kit contents and support. Earlier counts
such as "98 items," "53 EiE kits" or two seven-robot sets are not verified
October 2026 holdings and must not be used as procurement guarantees.
This resource is not an official inventory or Archdiocesan policy.

## Before selecting a loan-dependent unit

1. Define the objective and select the
   [technology label](../Review/Technology_Plan.md).
2. Check current listings and contact the lender; record confirmed dates and
   complete usable quantities. Check early, but no universal two-week lead time
   or free entitlement is promised.
3. Confirm all included guides, power supplies, chargers, hosts, consumables,
   software, licenses and age ratings. A commercial kit is not automatically
   complete for this class or a 25-minute period.
4. Plan native meetings: K 25, 1-2 30, 3-4 40, 5-6 45 minutes, including
   hands-on turns, individual evidence and cleanup.
5. Have a named school-funded low-tech alternative. If the objective requires
   program/robot execution, explicitly defer that evidence if the loan fails.

At 25, nine teams <=3 need nine complete team kits, or documented rotations
with individual turns and enough meetings. Watching one borrowed robot is not
equivalent to each child's programming evidence.

## When items arrive / return

- Count parts against the lender's actual packing list; report shortages/damage.
- Read the supplied instructions and test the complete activity before class.
- Verify electrical protection and battery restrictions; no button cells,
  latex balloons or projectile accessories in the core; no neodymium magnets in K.
- Store in labeled counted containers; teacher manages batteries/charging.
- On return, count/dry/clean according to confirmed lender directions, note
  damage/missing parts and meet the agreed return date. Do not invent sanitation
  chemicals or attempt unsupported repairs.

## Optional legacy candidates, not a required shopping list

Dash, Sphero, Bee-Bot/Ozobot/EV3-type robotics, Engineering is Elementary,
modular electronics and USB input kits appear in legacy planning. Availability,
product support, proprietary power/hosts, apps and licenses need verification.
Do not assume every EiE unit contains nine 45-minute lessons; inspect the actual
guide and split learning appropriately. VR is not a core need and requires
additional school/manufacturer age/accessibility review.

Seasonal themes remain useful: fall structures, winter indoor light/systems,
spring environmental observations. Confirm schedule/rotation against actual
lessons rather than booking by old week numbers. The
[materials core](../Review/Materials_Plan.md) operates without these loans.
