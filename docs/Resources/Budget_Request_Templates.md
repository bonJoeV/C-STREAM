# Budget and supply request templates

Use the single [Materials Plan](../Review/Materials_Plan.md) and
[Inventory CSV](../Review/Materials_Inventory.csv); do not reuse the earlier
inconsistent annual estimates, fixed loan claims or donor price promises.
All costs are **October 2026 planning estimates, NOT vendor quotations**.

## Grant / donor / optional donation copy

**Grant summary:** "We seek [amount] for [specific materials/objectives/cohorts].
We will document [individual evidence] and inspect/replenish kits annually."
Do not invent baseline results, grant eligibility or official accreditation.

**Optional donation:** "Clean, dry flat cardboard is welcome after teacher
screening. Donating is optional; every child has school-provided supplies.
Please send no batteries, magnets, glass, sharp metal, latex or chemical/food-soiled
containers." No suggested dollar level promises a month/semester of lessons.

**Thank-you:** "Thank you for [confirmed contribution]. It supported [actual
learning/material use]." Photographs require verified consent.
Tax deductibility, school legal status, gift-processing arrangements and monetary
donation instructions are **VERIFICATION REQUIRED** with administration.
