# Grades 1-6 reference routines

Use this guide with the actual lesson. It does not replace that lesson's
supplies, timed sequence, safety rules, objective or individual check.

## Native periods and release gate

Grades 1-2 use **30 minutes**, Grades 3-4 **40**, Grades 5-6 **45**.
Distribution, testing, individual evidence and cleanup fit inside the chosen
period. First-time kits, cards, account approval, charging/freezing and
longitudinal care are advance work, not hidden substitute tasks.

Before handoff, read the lesson, locate/count the kit, check safety/access,
pretest the actual pathway and rehearse the evidence/cleanup prompts. Record
the real school contact and storage location. A complete document does not
approve unfamiliar equipment or supply missing materials.

## Balanced groups and meaningful turns

| Pupils | Groups <=3 | Size distribution |
|---:|---:|---|
| 10 | 4 | Two trios and two pairs |
| 15 | 5 | Five trios |
| 20 | 7 | Six trios and one pair |
| 25 | 9 | Seven trios and two pairs |

Pair-based lessons use their own allocation, often 5/7/10/12 active teams
and 5/8/10/13 budgeted kits including odd-enrollment reserve. Do not issue a
generic nine-kit set to every pair lesson or leave a lone child as a "pair."

Rotate planner/builder/tester/reporter so each pupil makes a decision,
contributes and explains evidence. Observing another child execute software
or operate hardware does not establish the observer's operation mastery.
Waiting work is meaningful prediction/recording/design, not passive watching.

## Individual assessment

Begin roster checks while pupils investigate/build, then finish within the
lesson's evidence block. Record **not yet / prompted / independent** for each
objective separately, with the actual response/result. Unobserved is not a pass.
At 25 enrollment, avoid serial class presentations or a single exit queue;
use paired exchanges and a teacher sweep.

An oral, drawn, pointed, dictated or assistive response can preserve a target.
Grade 1 directions are modeled/read aloud; handwriting and artistic polish
are not graded unless explicitly the objective. Older-grade challenge adds
evidence, precision, variables, limitations or tradeoffs, not simply more work.
The local progression's mastery checkpoints need two dated individual observations.

## Truthful pathways

- Actual tests compare the stated same conditions and keep failed results.
- Prepared numerical examples and fictional scenarios are labeled **model/
  supplied data**, not pupil-collected observations.
- A model has limits; tabletop structures, assistive-device models and water
  demonstrations are not safe real-person devices or potable-water tests.
- Paper algorithms can support computational reasoning. Actual software
  execution, robot/sensor operation and electrical assembly require actual
  approved equipment and individual turns.
- Preserve relevant source facts without inventing a biography, quotation,
  scientific priority or official benchmark.

## Common teacher kit and cleanup

Teacher timer/clock, paper roster and pencil/approved marker are common tools.
Check the actual selected lesson's measuring, cutting, electrical or wet-work
tools; they are not implied by a generic pupil kit.
Stop and isolate unsafe equipment, apply the school's emergency/hygiene plan,
count issue/return quantities, save dated evidence and clear exits/surfaces.
Do not use surface-cleanup cloths as shared hand towels.

## Family and school follow-up

Copy the lesson's actual concept/action/evidence/Catholic connection and a
child-answerable question. Home activities are optional; devices, transport,
donations, purchases or parent teaching never supply essential assessed evidence.
The [school release checklist](../Review/School_Release_Checklist.md) records
the remaining physical/policy/pilot checks without inventing approval.
