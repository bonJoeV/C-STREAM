## Before class

1. Assign teams of at most three: **4 / 5 / 7 / 9** kits for **10 / 15 / 20 / 25** pupils. Keep work at tables; distribute materials, not roaming station rotations.
2. Ask a classroom helper for two visible groups of three picture cards. Precut cardboard/tape; draw two-group and retrieval criterion icons.
3. Put two plain-paper sheets per child per meeting and crayons at tables. Draw this record on the board: **helper request / two-group allocation / test and change**. Children copy boxes/icons, not paragraphs. Teacher dates and saves each child's record between meetings.
4. Read the SAFETY section, pretest the actual task with one issued kit and put a roster beside the timer. Demonstrate orally with objects; read every direction aloud. No independent reading, Year A completion or family donation is a prerequisite.

## Vocabulary and teacher background

Community = people working together; organizer = helps find items; equal groups = same count in each.

The task models useful organization at school, not a general device for disability. Six cards can be split into two equal groups of three.

**Common misconception:** Service requires litter picking, food delivery or family purchases.

**If asked:** "Can we build a bird feeder?" Not in this package; the requested classroom organizer has a bounded safe test.

## SAFETY

No litter handling, food, outdoor feeders, sharp recyclables or outside deliveries. Keep aisles/exits clear. Stop for damaged materials; do not improvise a hazardous replacement. No student handles loose batteries or teacher cutting tools.

## Meeting 1: Helper organizer - 30 minutes

1. **0-3:** Read the actual helper request and material limit.
2. **3-7:** Model folding two spaces and checking three cards in each plus retrieval without moving the holder.
3. **7-20:** Teams build, distribute six cards into two groups, test retrieval and revise one support/visibility feature. Each child conducts/directs one check and draws the allocation.
4. **20-26:** Each child names the user need, shows the two groups and explains a test-based improvement; giving the model is optional.
5. **26-30:** Return all six cards and save reusable organizer materials. Collect each child's dated evidence, count kit items, return tools and clear tables. Cleanup is part of the period, not time added afterward.

## Questions to ask

- How do the two spaces help the classroom helper?
- How did you split six cards?

## Individual evidence and success

**Grade 1:** Show two groups of three and identify a useful feature/test.

**Grade 2:** Explain 3 + 3 = 6 with a model, a material constraint and a revision linked to retrieval evidence.

Teacher visits three table teams during each of the first three four-minute blocks of practice (7-19), listening to or observing each member briefly; use the remaining practice minute and 20-26 for unfinished checks and comparison. At 25, this is nine table visits, **not 25 presentations**. Where a meeting specifies waves, use its supervised wave/check schedule instead of simultaneous table visits. Children keep separate records and partners may not answer for each other. Score each stated criterion **not yet / prompted / independent**; record absent/unobserved evidence as such. A group product, attendance, attractive art or a certificate is not individual mastery. Use the grade-level criterion, not handwriting, reading speed or religious worth.

## Support and challenge

**Support / nonreader access:** Precreased broad spaces and picture-group icons; child directs placement. Adult scribing, pointing, tactile/large pieces and a seated partner-directed action can show understanding; do not score motor speed.

**Challenge:** Compare a four/two grouping with the requested equal grouping and explain why one does not meet the request. Do not add an unprepared activity or extend the native period.

## If things go wrong / indoor alternative

Cards spill: widen/lower compartments and retest. Helper absent: teacher states the actual school sorting need; no community visit is required.

**Indoor:** The complete primary lesson is indoors at tables; Minnesota weather, internet access and daylight are not prerequisites.

## Family copy - optional, no routine homework

We designed a useful classroom organizer and explained a six-card allocation. Ask: "How do the two spaces help the classroom helper?" Optional: notice how familiar objects are organized. No purchase, donation, device, home teaching or required take-home project.
