# 🔬 Grades 1-2 Year A Bi-Weekly C-STREAM

**17 Sessions | 30 Minutes Each | Every Other Week**

This folder contains the condensed Grades 1-2 Year A C-STREAM curriculum for schools with bi-weekly STREAM classes. Year A focuses on foundational engineering, coding with ScratchJr/Dash Robots, and exploring God's patterns in nature.

## Audit and entry conditions
See the [Grades 1-2 review and downloadable audit](../../../Review/Grades_1-2_Review.md). There are **17 actual meetings in 17 files = 510 minutes**, compared with 32 weekly meetings/960 minutes. This is a reduced-contact alternative, not equal robotics/science mastery in half the time.

Year A need not precede Year B. New Grade 1 students learn through oral directions, pictures, pointing and drawing; returning Grade 2 students add evidence-based explanations and revisions. Before Session 15 recheck Session 04 stored-code start/stop and simple sequence, rather than assuming expertise after one encounter. Revisit skills briefly between biweekly meetings where ordinary class routines permit, but do not count extra C-STREAM sessions as existing inventory.

Bridge Building and Robot Friends are fully rebuilt; the other lessons received targeted corrections but have unresolved P1/P2 readiness work. **VERIFICATION REQUIRED** gates protect uncertain history/doctrine and official benchmarks. No universal substitute-readiness claim is made.

## 🎯 Year A Learning Goals

Target outcomes, demonstrated individually when the relevant primary path is taught:

- ✅ Follow the design process: Ask → Imagine → Plan → Create → Improve

- Run/debug a short stored Dash/Blockly sequence; **manual Go driving is not programming evidence**.

- ✅ Create simple coding projects with ScratchJr

- ✅ Identify patterns in God's creation

- ✅ Connect Catholic faith to scientific discovery

- ✅ Work collaboratively on engineering challenges

## 🛠️ Materials Used Throughout Year

### From CSCOE Library

Possible loans only: verify inventory, booking terms, compatible apps and charging; no item is guaranteed available.

- Dash Robots & Tablets

- KEVA Planks

- Simple Machines Kit

- Pattern Blocks

- Magnifying glasses

### Technology

- ScratchJr on tablets

- Tested Blockly for stored Dash sequences; Go may illustrate manual control only.

### Classroom Supplies

- Craft sticks, cardboard, tape

- Construction paper

- Recording journals

Low-tech supplies support observation, bridges, patterns, weather and artistic communication. When actual robot/ScratchJr execution is assessed, the lesson has a **Required primary path**; cards/token fallbacks assess algorithms, not real robot/app operation. Winter nature/weather routes use screened indoor specimens/window observations. Home activities are optional; no routine homework or required purchases/donations.
