# Session 1: Design Thinking

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly B; Foundations; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** ask a user a bounded question, design a readable desk-label stand
and revise after two tests. **Why:** empathy starts by listening, not assuming
private difficulties. Catholic/CST-C2 dignity/choice; CST-E1 criterion/limits;
CST-E2 actual retest; CST-A2 purposeful form. Official alignment **VERIFICATION
REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; novice, no Year A assumed. Prep 10; cleanup 5.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9), each cardstock sheet, ruler, two markers, **20 cm tape**.
Each pupil paper/pencil. Teacher board/timer; fold demonstration tent from
issued sheet. User brief: classroom visitor wants a readable subject label,
not pupil's name. Ask "Word and picture, or word and arrow?" User may decline;
fictional user prefers word/picture. No interviews about hardship/disability.
Criterion stands ten seconds and user identifies subject at **1 m**.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cards; rulers (1/team each) | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Markers (2/team) | 8 | 10 | 14 | 18 |

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Empathy = listen/consider user; prototype = testable trial; criterion = success;
feedback = user's actual response. **Misconception:** designer liking equals
user need. **If asked "Can I ask why home is hard?"** No; keep interview within
classroom label choice. Human dignity isn't reduced to a successful model.
Paper/tape desk only, no skin tape, sharp tools or private identifying labels.
Stop/report broken supplies. Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "What should we ask before making something for a person?"
2. **4-9:** Model bounded interview/problem statement and ten-second/1 m tests.
3. **9-21:** Every pupil asks/records answer or fictional brief, sketches two
   forms; team builds label stand within card/tape allowance.
4. **21-30:** Test standing/readability twice; revise base or visual layout,
   repeat under same distance/time.
5. **30-35:** Each child states user response/criterion, actual before/after results
   and purposeful form choice. "Which feedback changed your design?"
6. **35-40:** Count/cap tools, save dated sketches/stand, clear.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: user-centered statement, actual retests and justified visual choice.
Grade 3/support: folded starter/choice pictures/scribing. Grade 4/challenge:
stability-versus-space tradeoff. No consent: fictional brief labeled, not
invented interview. Falling stand: widen fold, no extra stock. Missing card:
paper fold, state substitution. Early finish: new reader, same criterion.

**Family:** We listened and tested a user-focused stand. Ask, "What did the user
actually ask for?" Optional: discuss a safe everyday design choice.

## Teacher artifact pack (lesson-specific release checklist)

- **Student material (print/no-print):** Print one half-sheet per pupil titled **Desk-label stand: ask, test, revise**. Include the exact fields `User: classroom visitor / fictional visitor (circle one)`, `Choice asked: Word + picture / Word + arrow`, `User chose: ____`, `Subject word on our stand: ____`, `Sketch version 1; mark fold, base and viewing face`, `Test 1: stood 10 s? Y/N; subject identified at 1 m? Y/N`, `One change and the evidence for it: ____`, and `Test 2: stood 10 s? Y/N; subject identified at 1 m? Y/N`. No-print: pupils divide paper into **ASK / VERSION 1 / TEST 1 / CHANGE / TEST 2** and copy the same prompts; oral response or teacher scribing is valid.
- **Evidence and answer guidance:** Each pupil records the offered choice or labels the fictional brief, contributes a sketch, and reports two actual tests. Accept subject labels such as `MATH` with a number picture or `READING` with an arrow toward books. Accept revisions such as `widened the bottom fold because it fell at 6 s`, `made READING larger because the visitor guessed`, or `increased black/white contrast`; a preferred decoration without test evidence is not a criterion-based revision. A decline is recorded as `fictional visitor used`; do not invent an interview.
- **Worked example and finished example:** On the board complete: `Fictional visitor; asked Word + picture or Word + arrow?; chose Word + picture; subject SCIENCE`. Sketch a tent with **SCIENCE** and a beaker icon. Record `Test 1: stood 10 s Y; identified at 1 m N ("letters crowded")`; revise to one large word and smaller icon; record `Test 2: Y/Y`. This is finished because the user choice, both criteria, evidence-linked change and retest are present. N/A is not justified for the example because a fictional brief makes the full cycle available.
- **Visual example and board setup:** Before pupils enter, mark a viewing line exactly **1 m** from the demo desk and post a ten-second timer. Draw `LISTEN → SKETCH → BUILD → TEST 10 s + 1 m → CHANGE ONE FEATURE → RETEST`; beside it show a tent profile labeled `base`, `fold`, `viewing face`, and the stock limits `1 cardstock / 20 cm tape / 1 ruler / 2 markers per team`.
- **Discussion with acceptable response, and misconception:** Ask `What did the user actually choose?` (word/picture or word/arrow; `fictional brief` is acceptable), `What observation supports your revision?` (a fall time, wrong subject, or readability comment), and `Can a stable stand still fail?` (yes, if the subject is not identified at 1 m). Correct `I like it, so the user will` by requesting the recorded user response; correct `more tape makes it better` by returning to the 20 cm constraint; never invite disclosure about disability or home hardship.
- **Kit contents, kit label, cleanup and reset:** Kit label: **S1 DESK-LABEL—TEAM __**. Prepare one such tray with exactly `1 cardstock sheet, 1 metric ruler, 2 markers, one premeasured 20 cm masking-tape strip`; issue each pupil `1 plain sheet and 1 pencil`. The teacher demo uses its own issued sheet, not team stock. Reset by removing names/private notes, unfolding only reusable demonstration stock, discarding scraps, recapping both markers, coiling any unused tape on release paper, and checking `cardstock 1 / ruler 1 / markers 2` before shelving.
- **Unfinished work:** Staple each pupil record to the team's stand label and box only the next missing action: `user choice`, `Test 1`, `revision`, or `Test 2`. Store an intact stand on a flat shelf in a bag labeled `S1 team __—DO NOT TEST`; if it cannot be stored, record dimensions and dismantle. Resume at the missing action with the same criterion; write **not observed** for any unperformed test rather than awarding success.
- **Annotated exemplar, common error and reteach:** Mark the finished sample with callouts `heard choice`, `readable subject`, `base within stock`, `actual result`, and `change caused by evidence`. A stand that falls before 10 seconds indicates a stability/base problem, not poor artwork: corrective practice a wider crease and hands-free timing. A stand that remains up but is misread at 1 m indicates letter size, contrast or clutter: cover the icon, enlarge the word, and repeat from the marked line.
- **Safety classification:** **Severity: low; likelihood: unlikely; Adult ratio: 1 adult to 24 pupils maximum.** Construction remains seated and cutting-free; prohibit tape on skin, sharp tools, identifying name labels and throwing rulers. Stop for broken rulers, leaking/dried markers or unsafe play. **Commercial-product hold:** Used markers and masking tape. Enter the exact manufacturer/product in [Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Keep each listed product on **HOLD** until school approval and SDS applicability are resolved; do not infer a brand or claim SDS N/A.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Use at least 16-point sans-serif type, bold Y/N boxes, 1.5-line spacing, black outlines and labels in addition to icons or color. Provide an enlarged 11×17 copy, thick pencil/marker, pre-drawn tent outline, read-aloud prompts, oral dictation or scribe; the 1 m identification check may be spoken or indicated by pointing.
- **Technology disposition:** No technology artifact applies; evidence is the listed physical design work and paper records.
