# Session 10: Geology Rocks

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly B; Earth systems; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** record two properties/length of a sample, compare formation
references and explain a tentative identification/model limit.
**Why:** geology distinguishes evidence from confident-looking guesses.
Catholic/CST-C1 honest uncertainty/wonder; CST-S1 properties; CST-S3 formation/
multiple paths; CST-A1 labeled sketch; CST-M1 cm lengths.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**;
introductory. Prep 15; cleanup 5.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9): three teacher-inspected **blunt** samples, lens/ruler, three
formation cards. Per pupil two sheets/pencil; teacher board/timer/demo from kit.
Use school source-labeled sample identities only if provenance known; unknowns
stay tentative. Ready formation cards:
igneous from cooling molten rock; sedimentary from deposited material becoming
rock (not all merely layered); metamorphic from existing rock changed by
heat/pressure without melting. Draw cooling/erosion/deposition/change/melting
arrows with alternate paths, not a compulsory cycle order.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Grain = visible particle; band = stripe; formation = process; tentative =
possible, not certain. Not all metamorphic rocks have bands; appearance alone
doesn't prove origin. **Misconception:** every rock follows identical sequence.
**If asked "Is God literally rock?"** Religious foundation imagery is metaphor,
not mineral classification; no disputed Scripture quotation needed.
No obsidian/sharp fragments, breaking, acids, tasting or unknown outdoor
collecting. Lenses not aimed at Sun. Stop/report rough samples, wash hands.
Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "What does a rock actually let us observe?" Optional wonder prayer.
2. **4-10:** Model two visible properties/length, then compare formation cards.
3. **10-22:** Each handles one inspected sample, draws/labels two properties,
   measures length; rotate through other samples.
4. **22-30:** Match possible type from references/provenance, label uncertainty;
   draw two different formation paths and revise from peer check.
5. **30-35:** Each child gives actual properties/cm, supported tentative label and
   formation/model limit. "What further evidence would establish origin?"
6. **35-40:** Count/store samples/lenses/rulers, save dated records, wash/clear.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: observed detail/measurement, intelligible diagram and justified uncertain
classification. Grade 3/support: pictured property choices/read-aloud/scribing.
Grade 4/challenge: compare lengths and explain why appearance is insufficient.
Unknown identity: record unknown, not force label. No safe samples: teacher
draws three **model** textures with stated hypothetical formation cards; diagram
reasoning only, specimen observation/measurement not observed.
Early finish: additional possible path, no crushing tests.

**Family:** We observed rocks and reported uncertainty. Ask, "Which property
did you see?" Optional: notice a rock picture, no collecting homework.
