# Session 17: Year Celebration

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly B; Portfolio; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** support a learning claim with dated work, read a data/model
record individually and revise a caption from peer feedback.
**Why:** celebrating growth should accurately describe practice/operation/
mastery. Catholic/CST-C3 responsible gifts/work; CST-E3 evidence/limit;
CST-A3 critique/revision; CST-M2 record reading.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**;
developing. Prep 15; cleanup 5. No compulsory summer activity or mastery award.

## Before class and exact supplies

Each pupil portfolio/two sheets/pencil. Teacher board/timer; choose genuine
dated data or model records. Backup **simulated test counts 4 then 7** for new
two-bar reading, labeled practice, not pupil history.
Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9), parallel desk sharing, no live Ozobot/circuit/Makey Makey.
Reuse portfolios, retain dated caption; no printed certificate/photo purchase.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Portfolios; pencils (1/pupil each) | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Paper (2/pupil) | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Artifact = saved work; evidence = real record; growth = supported change;
caption = display explanation. **Misconception:** a group device photo proves
each child operated it. **If asked "Can I say expert?"** Only individual
evidence supports particular skills. Local mastery needs two dated separate
occasions and changed-context explanation, not today's reflection alone.
No live devices, private photos, food/balloons or claims that all pupils completed
all domains. Stop/report missing artifacts. Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Optional gratitude prayer; "Which record shows a changed decision?"
2. **4-9:** Model date/source/result/limit, paper versus actual operation.
3. **9-20:** Choose two genuine artifacts, prepare caption, independently read
   graph/model and explain change. Missing work: backup bars 4/7, difference
   3/total 11, labeled today's simulated practice only.
4. **20-29:** Parallel two-minute pupil turns, demonstrate reasoning and answer
   peer question; receive specific caption critique.
5. **29-35:** Each revises caption, gives supported skill/data explanation/
   stewardship choice, marks historical evidence gaps honestly.
6. **35-40:** Date/file captions, return portfolios/pencils, clear.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: attributable evidence explanation/data reading and deliberate caption
revision. Growth requires actual dated records, not invented improvement.
Grade 3/support: two stems/pointing/scribing. Grade 4/challenge: transfer test
needed or sample limitation. Missing work: assess today's reasoning, record
growth not evidenced. Long speech: task-result-limit within two minutes.
Early finish: answer changed-context question. Enthusiasm/prayer not graded.

**Family:** We celebrated with honest evidence. Ask, "What does your record
show and leave uncertain?" Optional: listen to the explanation.

## Teacher artifact pack (lesson-specific release checklist)

- **Student material (print/no-print):** Print one two-sided **Honest Growth Portfolio Caption** per pupil. Side A asks `Artifact 1 title/date ____`, `Artifact 2 title/date ____`, `What I personally did in each ____`, `Skill claim ____`, `Exact evidence that supports the claim ____`, `What the records do NOT establish ____`, and `Responsible next practice step ____`. Side B provides `Fresh data/model record title ____`, `My independent reading ____`, `Peer question ____`, `Specific caption critique ____`, `Caption before ____`, `Caption after revision ____`, and `Sharing choice: named display / anonymous display / private teacher share`. When genuine records are missing, attach the clearly labeled practice bars `simulated test count: first 4, then 7` and prompts `difference ____`, `combined total ____`, `What this practice does/does not show ____`. No-print pupils divide two plain sheets using these exact headings or dictate to a scribe.
- **Evidence and answer guidance:** A released caption attributes two genuine dated artifacts to the pupil, states one bounded learning claim, accurately reads a fresh data/model record, includes a limit, and visibly revises after peer feedback. Growth requires actual dated records; a group photo/device result does not prove individual operation. For the backup bars, correct answers are `difference 3`, `combined total 11`, and `this shows I can read these simulated counts today; it is not my historical growth`. Accept `My later geology record includes cm and separates observation from inference, so it shows improved reporting in those two records; it does not prove mastery in every measurement setting`. Reserve local mastery language for two dated separate occasions plus changed-context explanation.
- **Worked example and finished example:** Display two fictional attributable records: `March 3--compound-machine drawing correctly labels lever/ramp but has no retest` and `April 14--new setup records four counts and one-variable retest`. Draft caption: `I am an expert engineer`. Evidence check identifies overreach. Fresh record reading: `before counts 4 and after counts 7; after is 3 higher and total is 11`. Peer critique: `name the changed practice and a limit`. Final caption: `Across these dated models, I improved at recording repeated tests and explaining one controlled revision. They do not show that I can design every machine independently; next I will test the same reasoning in a changed setup.` Annotate why the final version celebrates growth without ranking classmates or inventing mastery.
- **Visual example and board setup:** Place each private portfolio and two blank sheets at its owner's desk; no artifacts are pre-displayed. On the board build `DATE + SOURCE → RESULT/FEATURE → SUPPORTED CLAIM → LIMIT → NEXT STEP` and a separate two-bar backup chart labeled **SIMULATED PRACTICE--NOT PUPIL HISTORY**, bars 4 and 7, with axis/count labels. Post the two-minute sharing structure `artifact/task → evidence/result → limit → peer question` and three consent-based sharing choices. Keep all live robots/circuits/Makey Makey hardware out of the room.
- **Discussion with acceptable response, and misconception:** Ask `Why do dates and sources matter?` (they locate evidence and allow honest comparison); `What proves that you operated a device?` (an attributable individual operation/test record, not a group photo); `Can one successful result prove mastery?` (no); `When may growth be claimed?` (when genuine comparable dated evidence shows a supported change); `What may be celebrated when artifacts are missing?` (today's reasoning/practice, with the historical gap stated); `Why revise a caption?` (to make it clearer, more accurate, and appropriately limited); and `Must anyone display publicly?` (no--private sharing is valid). Correct the misconceptions that celebration requires universal success, enthusiasm is assessed evidence, or `expert` is a participation label.
- **Kit contents, kit label, cleanup and reset:** There is no consumable craft kit. Kit label: **S17 YEAR CELEBRATION--PORTFOLIOS: 1 PER PUPIL / PAPER: 2 PER PUPIL / PENCILS: 1 PER PUPIL**, applied to the secure class crate and load only those quantities from the allocation table (10/15/20/25 portfolios and pencils; 20/30/40/50 sheets). The teacher separately holds the timer and backup simulated bar record. Reset by matching every artifact/caption to the correct portfolio, checking sharing-choice status, counting all portfolios and pencils, removing anonymous-display copies from public view after the event, and returning originals to secure teacher-controlled storage. No sticky notes, rulers, gallery adhesives, certificates, purchased photos, food, or balloons belong in this pack.
- **Unfinished work:** Use status tabs `ONE ARTIFACT ONLY`, `ATTRIBUTION UNCLEAR`, `FRESH RECORD READ PENDING`, `PEER CRITIQUE PENDING`, or `REVISION PENDING`. Never fabricate a second date, fill a claim from memory as fact, or relabel the simulated 4/7 bars as pupil progress. A pupil with no portfolio evidence completes the fresh reading and a caption `Today I practiced...`; the teacher records `historical growth not evidenced`. Respect private-sharing choice and resume only the missing step.
- **Annotated exemplar, common error and reteach:** Mark the finished example at `two dates`, `personal attribution`, `exact feature/result`, `claim`, `limit`, `fresh reading`, `critique`, and `substantive revision`. `I got better` with no cited change signals unsupported inference--draw arrows from words in the claim to exact artifact details. A difference of 11 or total of 3 shows operation confusion--model 7 - 4 and 7 + 4 with counters or number lines. A photo-based operation claim signals attribution error--replace with a dated individual record or state the gap. A punctuation-only edit does not satisfy critique-backed revision; prompt a clarity/accuracy change in evidence or limit language.
- **Safety classification:** **Severity: low; likelihood: unlikely** because the session uses seated paper portfolios only. Adult ratio: **1 adult to 25 pupils**, with a second authorized adult only if school supervision/privacy rules require separate sharing areas. Keep aisles clear, displays at child height without adhesives, and all food, balloons, live devices, batteries, and loose construction models absent. Emotional/privacy control is essential: no forced public speaking, ranking, private photos, or disclosure of grades/family information. SDS review is not applicable to portfolios, paper, and pencils; no chemical products are released.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Use 16-18-point sans-serif prompts, high contrast, wide writing spaces, plain-language stems (`This shows...`, `It does not show...`, `Next I will...`), and a chart with labeled axes and values printed on bars so color is unnecessary. Offer read-aloud, oral/audio-free teacher scribing, pointing to evidence, number line/counters for 4 and 7, shortened/private two-minute sharing, communication supports, and anonymous or non-display participation. Preserve rigor by assessing the evidence link, not handwriting, speech fluency, or public performance.
- **Technology disposition:** No technology artifact applies; portfolios and paper records are the evidence.
