# Session 9: Faith & Science

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly B; Faith and evidence; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** cite a supported priest/scientist fact, measure two scale drawings
and distinguish model, history and religious reflection.
**Why:** faith/reason value truthful claims rather than an invented cosmology
"proof" of Genesis. CST-C1 distinction; CST-S3 limited model; CST-T3 source
credit; CST-A1 annotated drawings; CST-M1 whole-cm comparison.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; introductory.
Prep 15 minutes first use, 10 with ready cards; cleanup 5.
No Einstein quotation or sole-discovery chronology.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9): ruler/two source cards. Each pupil two sheets/pencil.
Teacher board/timer; make two half-sheet cards from one ordinary sheet per team.
Reuse the Lemaitre card from
[verified Catholic scientific heritage](../../../Resources/Catholic_Scientists_Heritage.md#georges-lemaitre-religious-and-scientific-roles):
"Lemaitre was a Catholic priest and a university scholar whose field included
studying the universe. We can learn about a person's scientific and religious
roles without pretending that a classroom model proves a belief."
This is original teacher text, not an attributed Lemaitre quotation.
Copy ready cards:
1. [Pontifical Academy of Sciences: Georges Lemaitre](https://www.pas.va/en/academicians/deceased/lemaitre.html)
   lists Rev. Msgr., Cosmology, professor of Mechanics and Mathematical
   Methodology, Louvain Catholic university (retrieved October 4, 2026).
2. [Fides et Ratio opening](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_jp-ii_enc_14091998_fides-et-ratio.html),
   John Paul II: paraphrase, faith/reason support seeking truth.
Demo from issued paper: **simulated scale drawing** A/B/C at 0/2/4 cm then
0/4/8 cm. It represents increasing separations, not actual universe data,
an explosion into surrounding space or a model of the entire Big Bang.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Cosmology = study of universe; scale drawing = representation; source = claim's
origin; reflection = application. **Misconception:** drawing expansion proves
creation from nothing. **If asked "Did faith cause his scientific work?"**
Affiliation/field alone don't prove motivation; this source doesn't establish it.
Paper/ruler desk work, no balloons, exploding demos or Sun observation.
Stop/report damaged supplies. Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "What kinds of evidence support history, models and reflection?"
2. **4-10:** Read cards, model cited fact versus faith application.
3. **10-22:** Each makes/measures two drawings; record A-B 2/4 cm and A-C
   4/8 cm, label simulation/not-to-universe-scale.
4. **22-30:** Peer checks annotations; revise a misleading "proof" caption;
   link sourced field/role to a question, not unsupported discovery priority.
5. **30-35:** Each child cites one fact, explains measured separation/model limit and
   separate faith reflection. "What would real cosmology need beyond paper?"
6. **35-40:** Count cards/rulers, file dated drawings, clear.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: accurate source/claim, four readings/annotations and explicit model/
belief distinction. Grade 3/support: marked positions/read-aloud/scribing.
Grade 4/challenge: compare whole-number changes and explain projection limits,
no forced ratios. Wrong measurement: zero/spacing check, record correction.
Missing cards: copy the complete facts and teacher model above to the board,
then read aloud; no guessed biography. Early finish: formulate
an evidence question without unsupported theological/scientific conclusions.

**Family:** We joined sourced history and limited models. Ask, "Which claim
did paper measurements NOT prove?" Optional discussion only.

## Teacher artifact pack (lesson-specific release checklist)

- **Student material (print/no-print):** Print a two-sided **Source, simulated expansion and reflection record**. Side A: `Source used: 1 PAS / 2 Fides et Ratio`, `Copy/paraphrase one supported fact: ____`, `Words on the card that support it: ____`, `This is history / scientific model / faith reflection (circle)`. Side B: `Drawing 1 positions A=0, B=2, C=4 cm; measured A–B ____ cm, A–C ____ cm`, `Drawing 2 positions A=0, B=4, C=8 cm; measured A–B ____ cm, A–C ____ cm`, `What changed: ____`, `This model represents ____`, `It does NOT prove/show ____`, and `Separate faith reflection: ____`. No-print: pupils copy the two source prompts and four-reading table onto their two sheets.
- **Evidence and answer guidance:** Accept the PAS-supported fact `Georges Lemaître was a Catholic priest/Rev. Msgr., cosmology scholar and professor at Louvain Catholic university` without extending it to sole discovery or motivation. Accept Card 2's paraphrase that faith and reason support seeking truth, clearly labeled reflection. Measurements must be `2 cm, 4 cm, 4 cm, 8 cm`; acceptable change: `both separations doubled/increased by 2 and 4 cm`. Limits include `paper points are simulated, not universe data`, `not to universe scale`, `not an explosion into surrounding space`, and `does not prove creation from nothing or Genesis`.
- **Worked example and finished example:** Complete a sample: `Source 1 PAS`; claim `Lemaître was listed as Rev. Msgr. and his field as Cosmology`; supporting words `Rev. Msgr.; Cosmology`. Draw A/B/C at `0/2/4 cm`, then `0/4/8 cm`; record `A–B 2→4 cm, A–C 4→8 cm`. Finish `represents increasing separation in a scale drawing; does not reproduce the universe or prove a belief` and a separate reflection `Faith and reason can both value truthful inquiry`. No invented quotation or star-size conversion is used.
- **Visual example and board setup:** Divide the board into `SOURCE-SUPPORTED HISTORY | MEASURED SIMULATION | RELIGIOUS REFLECTION | LIMIT`. Post the two prepared cards with full titles/URLs, then two horizontal ruler lines with A/B/C at the exact coordinates. Draw arrows from each statement to its evidence column and box the caption **SIMULATED SCALE DRAWING—NOT ACTUAL UNIVERSE DATA**.
- **Discussion with acceptable response, and misconception:** Ask `Which words support the biographical fact?` (the PAS card's titles/field/position), `What changed between drawings?` (separations increased from 2/4 to 4/8 cm), `What would real cosmology need beyond this paper?` (observations, measurements, mathematics and expert analysis), and `Did this drawing prove a religious belief or Lemaître's motivation?` (no). Correct `faith caused his scientific work`, `Lemaître alone discovered everything`, `explosion from one point into empty surrounding space`, and fabricated Einstein/Lemaître quotations by returning to the cards.
- **Kit contents, kit label, cleanup and reset:** Kit label: **S9 SOURCE + SCALE—TEAM __**. Each tray contains exactly `1 metric ruler and 2 prepared half-sheet source cards: Card 1 PAS Lemaître; Card 2 Fides et Ratio paraphrase`; each pupil receives `2 plain sheets and 1 pencil`. The teacher kit has `board copies of both cards, demo ruler, and the two coordinate models`. Reset by returning cards in pockets `1 HISTORY` and `2 REFLECTION`, checking ruler edge/zero, erasing only reusable board marks and filing dated pupil drawings; no substitute biography cards are added without verification.
- **Unfinished work:** On each sheet box the last verified element: `source words`, `Drawing 1`, `Drawing 2`, `limit`, or `reflection`; store both sheets with the selected card number. An oral citation may be teacher-scribed, but missing measurements remain incomplete. Resume with the supplied card/ruler; do not let pupils fill gaps through unsupervised searching or copy another pupil's claim.
- **Annotated exemplar, common error and reteach:** Call out `source title`, `supporting words`, `ruler zero`, `four cm units`, `simulation label`, `limit`, and `separate reflection`. A correct number without `cm` needs unit fresh demonstration; `2/4/4/8` in wrong cells needs endpoint tracing. `The model proves God/Genesis` conflates categories: move the sentence to `unsupported`, reread the limit and rewrite `My faith reflection is __; the drawing measured __`.
- **Safety classification:** **Severity: low; likelihood: unlikely; Adult ratio: 1 adult to 24 pupils maximum.** Paper, pencils and metric rulers remain on desks; no balloons, exploding demonstrations, Sun observation, lab substances or sharp tools. Stop for broken rulers or unsafe play. **SDS: not applicable** because no chemical or laboratory material is used.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Use at least 16-point text, one claim per source-card paragraph, high-contrast A/B/C marks, bold centimeter ticks, uncluttered four-cell measurement table and the categories in words plus symbols. Offer enlarged cards, marked ruler starts, tactile dots, read-aloud, oral citation, calculator-free scribing or partner measuring while preserving individual interpretation.
- **Technology disposition:** No technology artifact applies; evidence is the physical model and paper records.
