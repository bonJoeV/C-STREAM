# Session 3: Tower Challenge

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly B; Structures; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** measure two tower versions after ten-second standing tests and
justify a base revision. **Why:** structural evidence and responsible stock
use matter more than winning tallest.
Catholic/CST-C3 count-back/reuse; CST-E1 criteria/limits; CST-E2 retest;
CST-M1 heights/comparison. Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.
Technology **None**; developing. Prep 15 with stock/loan extra; cleanup 5.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9): **30 inspected KEVA planks**, ruler.
Each pupil two paper sheets/pencil. Teacher board/timer; mark 20 cm square
desk base; **40 cm cap**, criterion >=15 cm/ten seconds hands-free.
The teacher demo uses a separate counted 30-plank set and metric ruler. Same
rectangular blocks may replace all KEVA; label the substitution.
Reuse planks/tools and save records; no added weights are used.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rulers (1/team) | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Base = contact footprint; balance = supported arrangement; criterion = success;
constraint = limit. Wide/symmetric base may help but doesn't guarantee stable
construction; loose triangles aren't rigid trusses.
**Misconception:** exceeding cap is achievement. **If asked "Can we climb to
measure?"** No; lower model only. Religious building stories aren't structural
evidence. No climbing/throwing/heavy loads/people below structures.
Stop/report splinters/sliding setup. Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "How does responsible building serve others?" State criteria/cap.
2. **4-9:** Model base/hands-free timer/ruler zero.
3. **9-21:** Each sketches; team builds with 30-plank allowance, rotates roles.
4. **21-30:** Standing/height test, record; change base only, rebuild/retest
   with same allowance/base area; record failed result honestly.
5. **30-35:** Each child gives two heights/pass results, whole-number difference,
   test-backed revision and reuse choice.
6. **35-40:** Dismantle top-down/count 30, return rulers, file dated evidence.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: criterion/constraint, two tests, justified revision/quantity comparison
and responsible return. Grade 3/support: starter base/tally/scribing.
Grade 4/challenge: repeat same test, explain height/stability tradeoff, no ratios.
Immediate fall: record zero, widen/shorten; never add loading challenge.
Less stock: equal reduced allowance, explicitly adjust plan/criterion.
Early finish: new repeat, not higher cap. All nine desks work simultaneously.

**Family:** We redesigned stable towers and counted materials back. Ask,
"Which base change came from evidence?" Optional: notice a building footprint.

## Teacher artifact pack (lesson-specific release checklist)

- **Student material (print/no-print):** Print a **30-plank tower evidence sheet** per pupil with `Team ____`, `Constraint check: 30 planks / inside 20 cm square / no higher than 40 cm`, `Sketch A; circle the base`, `A height ____ cm; stood hands-free 10 s? Y/N; if no, fell at ____ s`, `Base-only change for B: ____`, `B height ____ cm; stood 10 s? Y/N`, `Difference |B−A| = ____ cm`, and `Which version better met the criteria? ____ because ____`. No-print: pupils copy the constraint line and the two-row `A/B—height—seconds—pass` table onto one of their two sheets.
- **Evidence and answer guidance:** Require two whole-centimeter readings from ruler zero, two honest hands-free results, the absolute height difference, and a conclusion tied to `≥15 cm and 10 s`, while staying under the 40 cm cap and 30-plank allowance. A collapse may be recorded as `0 cm / 0 s` or the last independently standing height/time. Accept `A 27 cm, pass; B 25 cm, pass—B was steadier but A was taller` or `A failed, B passed after widening the base`; do not reward height that violates the cap or needs a hand.
- **Worked example and finished example:** Build/draw from the issued planks: `A = 24 cm, fell at 4 s, fail`; move only the bottom six planks into a wider alternating base; `B = 20 cm, stood 10 s, pass`; `difference = 4 cm`; conclusion `B met both criteria even though it was 4 cm shorter`. Finished evidence names the base revision and both results. No tape, paper legs or added weights appear in the example.
- **Visual example and board setup:** Tape a **20 cm × 20 cm** boundary on the demo desk, place a metric ruler with zero at desk level, and mark a visible **40 cm STOP** line. Post `Version / height cm / hands-free seconds / pass?` and the criteria `30 inspected planks; ≥15 cm; 10 s; ≤40 cm`. Demonstrate starting/stopping the timer without touching the tower.
- **Discussion with acceptable response, and misconception:** Ask `Is the tallest tower automatically successful?` (no; it must satisfy every criterion/constraint), `Why change only the base?` (to connect the retest to that variable), `What does a fall tell us?` (that arrangement did not remain balanced under this test, not that the team failed), and `Why count back 30?` (responsible reuse and equal stock). Correct `triangles are always strong` by noting loose plank triangles are not rigid trusses and `above 40 cm wins` by applying the safety cap.
- **Kit contents, kit label, cleanup and reset:** Kit label: **S3 KEVA—TEAM __—COUNT 30**. Each tray holds exactly `30 inspected KEVA planks and 1 metric ruler`; each pupil receives `2 plain sheets and 1 pencil`. The teacher station adds `20 cm square, 40 cm height marker, timer and demo planks drawn from a separate counted set`. Reset top-down: pupils dismantle, teacher removes splintered/damaged pieces, team sorts and counts five stacks of six, returns ruler at zero edge, and signs the tray count `30`. Replacement rectangular blocks must be uniform and the tray relabeled with the substitution.
- **Unfinished work:** Do not leave an untested tower standing in an occupied room. Record current height as `not independently tested`, sketch the bottom three layers, dismantle top-down and bag the 30 planks with the team record. Resume by reconstructing Version A or B from the sketch and performing the missing test; never infer a pass from a momentary standing position or an unverified record.
- **Annotated exemplar, common error and reteach:** Label the sample `footprint`, `alternating base`, `ruler zero`, `hands off`, `10 s`, and `30 returned`. A missing unit indicates measurement-recording support: re-place zero and say `__ centimetres`. A high tower that falls indicates an unsupported/narrow arrangement; rebuild only the bottom layer. A low stable tower under 15 cm is safe but misses the height criterion; add supported layers without narrowing the base.
- **Safety classification:** **Severity: moderate; likelihood: unlikely; Adult ratio: 1 adult to 24 pupils maximum.** KEVA planks can fall, pinch or splinter: build only on desks/floor work squares below 40 cm, keep faces/feet clear, never climb, load, throw or stand beneath a structure, and dismantle top-down. Stop for sliding tables or damaged planks. **Commercial-product hold:** Tape used to mark the 20 cm × 20 cm demo boundary. Enter the exact manufacturer/product in [Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Keep that product on **HOLD** until school approval and SDS applicability are resolved; do not infer a brand or claim SDS N/A. Any coated or chemically cleaned substitute blocks require the same product review before use.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Use 18-point headings, a spacious A/B table, bold `cm` and `seconds` labels, tactile or high-contrast 20 cm boundary, and a large-face timer. Offer pre-drawn base grids, partner ruler positioning, oral results/scribing and adapted grasping; maintain the same two measured tests.
- **Technology disposition:** No technology artifact applies; evidence is the physical tower test and paper records.
