# Session 6: Thanksgiving Design

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly B; Gratitude design; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** design a pull-tab thank-you card, test two cycles, revise and
explain one purposeful visual choice. **Why:** making can express specific
appreciation with useful communication, not required private emotion.
Catholic/CST-C2 respectful audience; CST-E1 reveal criterion; CST-E2 retest;
CST-A2 purposeful form. Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.
Technology **None**; introductory. Prep 10; cleanup 5.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9): cardstock base, two paper sheets (slider/message),
ruler/two markers, **40 cm tape**. Per pupil evidence paper/pencil.
Adult precuts any slot or uses two taped folded-paper guides.
Teacher board/timer and a prepared pull-tab demo use the listed cardstock,
paper, ruler, markers and masking tape. Sample "Thank you, classroom helpers /
You prepare our shared tools." Fictional recipient allowed.
Criterion message fully reveals twice without detached parts and peer names
helpful action. Paper gift only, adult consent for delivery.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Slider = guided moving paper; mechanism = parts doing a job; audience = reader;
contrast = visible difference. **Misconception:** decoration proves function.
**If asked "Must I list my blessings?"** No; assess communication/design, not
feelings or private life. Catholic gratitude is application, no unverified
Paul/saint quotation needed.
No blades, springs, bands, food or personal media; adult cuts only.
Stop/report torn guides. Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "How can thanks name an actual helpful action?" Optional prayer.
2. **4-9:** Demo slider/reveal/two-cycle criterion and stock limit.
3. **9-21:** Each sketches; team builds card, every pupil authors visual feature.
4. **21-30:** Two pull/return tests and reader response; revise guide/layout,
   repeat twice under same conditions.
5. **30-35:** Each child records before/after, explains criterion/user need and
   purposeful visual change. "What did sticking tell you?"
6. **35-40:** Count/cap tools, save dated evidence/card, clear scraps.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: function/reader results and deliberate form revision, not emotional
enthusiasm. Grade 3/support: prepared guide/large message/scribing.
Grade 4/challenge: hidden-area/readability tradeoff. Jam: widen guide one change,
retest; no sharp-tool shortcut. No cardstock: folded paper base, label limit.
Early finish: different reader, same cycles.

**Family:** We tested moving thank-you cards. Ask, "What changed after the
user test?" Optional: thank a helper if comfortable.

## Teacher artifact pack (lesson-specific release checklist)

- **Student material (print/no-print):** Print one **Pull-tab gratitude design record** per pupil with `Recipient role or fictional recipient: ____`, `Specific helpful action named: ____`, `My message (copy exact words): ____`, `Sketch; label base, two guides, slider, hidden/reveal area and pull arrow`, `Cycle 1—fully revealed? Y/N; stayed attached? Y/N; reader named the helpful action? Y/N`, `What stuck/was unclear: ____`, `One guide/layout change: ____`, `Cycle 2—fully revealed? Y/N; stayed attached? Y/N; reader named action? Y/N`, and `My purposeful visual choice: ____ because ____`. No-print: copy the same headings as a two-cycle table; private emotion or a real recipient name is never required.
- **Evidence and answer guidance:** The mechanism must pull out and return twice without a detached part, and a reader must identify the named action. Accept `Thank you, classroom helpers. You prepare our shared tools`, `Thank you, bus driver. You help riders travel safely`, or a respectful fictional role. Valid changes include widening/re-aligning the folded-paper guides, shortening the slider, moving the message into the reveal window, increasing contrast or enlarging type; `pulled harder` is not a design revision. Assess communication/function, not enthusiasm or disclosure.
- **Worked example and finished example:** Use the prepared sample: cardstock base, paper slider between two taped folded-paper guides, message `Thank you, classroom helpers / You prepare our shared tools`. Record `Cycle 1: reveal N, attached Y, reader action N—last words hidden`; move the message 2 cm toward the exposed end, then `Cycle 2: Y/Y/Y`. Add `blue frame around white message because contrast guides the reader`. Finished work has both actual cycles, reader response and evidence-linked change; decoration alone is unfinished.
- **Visual example and board setup:** Display front and side views labeled `cardstock base / taped folded guide / slider / stop end / reveal area / pull-return arrow`. Write stock limits `1 base + 2 paper sheets + 40 cm tape + ruler + 2 markers per team` and the loop `PULL OUT → READ → RETURN → RECORD → CHANGE ONE THING → REPEAT`. Place three checkboxes under each cycle: **reveal / attached / action understood**.
- **Discussion with acceptable response, and misconception:** Ask `What makes the thank-you specific?` (it names a role/person and an actual helpful action), `Why test pull and return?` (the complete mechanism must work both ways), `What did the reader—not the maker—understand?` (the helpful action), and `May a pupil use a fictional recipient?` (yes). Correct `more decoration proves gratitude` with the communication criterion and `pull harder` with guide alignment/clearance. Do not ask pupils to list blessings or disclose family circumstances.
- **Kit contents, kit label, cleanup and reset:** Kit label: **S6 PULL-TAB—TEAM __**. Each tray contains exactly `1 cardstock base, 2 plain paper sheets (slider/message and folded guides), 1 metric ruler, 2 markers, and one premeasured 40 cm masking-tape strip`; each pupil gets `1 evidence sheet and 1 pencil`. Adult-precut slots are stored separately and issued only if that approved method is used; no pupil cutting tool enters the tray. Reset by collecting message/record together, removing scraps, recapping two markers, returning ruler, placing unused tape on release paper and checking all six team items.
- **Unfinished work:** Paperclip the individual record to the team card and mark the precise next action: `guides not attached`, `Cycle 1 due`, `revision due`, or `Cycle 2 due`. Store the slider seated in its guides in a labeled flat envelope; if it must be dismantled, trace guide locations first. On return, perform the missing cycle before adding decoration; mark any unrun cycle **not observed**.
- **Annotated exemplar, common error and reteach:** Add callouts `specific action`, `reveal boundary`, `parallel guides`, `pull direction`, `reader result`, and `revision`. A snag with wrinkled edges indicates guide clearance/alignment: loosen one guide without using more than 40 cm tape. A detached slider indicates the guide or stop failed: fold/position a paper stop. Smooth motion but hidden words indicates layout, not mechanism; mark the visible window and reposition text.
- **Safety classification:** **Severity: low; likelihood: unlikely; Adult ratio: 1 adult to 24 pupils maximum.** Adult performs any slot precut; pupils use paper, ruler, pencil, markers and masking tape while seated. No blades, springs, rubber bands, food, tape on skin or personal media; stop for torn/sharp cardstock or damaged tools. **Commercial-product hold:** Used markers and masking tape. Enter the exact manufacturer/product in [Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Keep each listed product on **HOLD** until school approval and SDS applicability are resolved; do not infer a brand or claim SDS N/A.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Use 18-point prompts, wide-lined message fields, black-on-white reveal boxes and words plus arrows. Offer a preassembled guide, larger slider/tab, grip adaptation, oral dictation, role-based fictional prompt, scribe or alternate high-contrast visual choice. A pupil may direct a partner's pull while still interpreting both cycle results.
- **Technology disposition:** No technology artifact applies; evidence is the physical card tests and paper records.
