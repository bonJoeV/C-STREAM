# Session 4: Ecosystems

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly B; Life systems; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** build a four-role feeding model, direct food-to-eater arrows and
record which modeled links change when one card is removed.
**Why:** ecological care needs system reasoning, not body-yarn games or
guaranteed catastrophe claims.
Catholic/CST-C3 stewardship choice; CST-S3 roles/model limit;
CST-A1 explanatory drawing; CST-M2 before/after link table.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; introductory.
Prep 10; cleanup 5.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9): four large **hypothetical** organism cards, five short
20 cm string pieces, two markers. Per pupil two sheets/pencil.
Teacher board/timer; copy stipulated model key:
P photosynthetic producer; Q consumes P; R consumes Q; D decomposes remains
from P/Q/R. Five represented links: P->Q, Q->R, P->D, Q->D, R->D.
These relationships are a prepared model, not unsupported real species diets.
Add sunlight as nonliving energy input, not counted organism/link here.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Ecosystem = organisms/environment relationships; producer = makes food;
consumer = eats organisms; decomposer = breaks down remains.
**Misconception:** arrows point from predator to prey. **If asked "Does
removing Q prove R dies?"** It removes represented food links; real effects
depend on other food sources/conditions, not shown here.
No yarn on bodies/fingers, tugging, knots, unknown specimens or live animals.
Short desk strings only; report damaged supplies. Indoor primary, no Kateri
ancestry/biography claims needed.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "How does caring for habitat require knowing relationships?"
2. **4-10:** Read key; model food-to-eater arrow, sunlight distinction.
3. **10-22:** Each places a card/link, draws five labeled arrows/roles; team
   checks from key and records original five links.
4. **22-30:** Remove Q and attached three links; two remain. Record table
   5 -> 2, discuss uncertain ecological effects, revise a reversed arrow.
5. **30-35:** Each child explains one arrow, roles, link-count change, model limit and
   habitat-care choice without claiming a measured cascade.
6. **35-40:** Count four cards/five strings, cap markers, save dated diagrams.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: accurate roles/arrows, 5/2 table and honest system limitation.
Grade 3/support: pictured arrows/read-aloud/scribing. Grade 4/challenge:
explain omitted alternate foods, no universal percentages.
Reversed link: follow source-food into eater, compare key. Missing string:
draw arrows, same model objective. Early finish: remove a different card and
count represented links, not predict certain deaths.

**Family:** We modeled ecological links. Ask, "Which way did your arrows point?"
Optional: discuss caring for a habitat from a picture.
