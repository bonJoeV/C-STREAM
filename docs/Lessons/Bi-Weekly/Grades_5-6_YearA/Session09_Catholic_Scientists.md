## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Provenance:** origin. **Paraphrase:** own wording preserving meaning.
**Claim:** statement needing support. **Limit:** what source does not establish.
**Common misconception:** religious identity verifies every scientific claim.
**If asked, "Did faith cause his discovery?"** These cards show his role/work,
not establish a causal account of his private motives.

**Card A - verified heritage/history:** Adapted from the original
[Mendel heritage card](../../../Resources/Catholic_Scientists_Heritage.md#gregor-johann-mendel-observation-and-inquiry).
[Masaryk University's Mendel Museum](https://mendelmuseum.muni.cz/en/about-the-museum/mendel-museum)
identifies Mendel as a member and abbot of the Augustinian order, his pea
experiments, and preserved original objects/work on plant hybrids. A religious
role and scientific work can coexist; this record does not establish private
motives or prove a faith claim. Its historical facts are separate from our
invented classroom data.
**Card B - archive:** [Villanova Library, Rediscovering Gregor Mendel](https://sciencefromthestacks.library.villanova.edu/rediscovering-gregor-mendel-os)
lists holdings of Mendel's plant-hybridization paper and monastery archive
materials. A holdings list establishes preserved sources, not every quoted belief.
**Card C - faith:** [Fides et Ratio, opening](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_jp-ii_enc_14091998_fides-et-ratio.html)
affirms faith/reason in pursuit of truth; this is Church teaching, not pea data.
All cards are paraphrases, not attributed quotations.

### Complete model dataset and inquiry key

**Invented classroom labels, not Mendel's measurements or observations of real
plants.** R means round and W wrinkled in a paper pea-trait model:

```text
Sample A: R R R R R R R R W W
Sample B: R R R R R R R W W W
```

Question: "Do these two equal-sized samples have identical category counts?"
Each student tallies directly from the labels: A has **8 R / 2 W / 10 total**;
B has **7 R / 3 W / 10 total**. A has one more R and one fewer W.
Combined counts **15 R / 5 W / 20 total** give a **3:1** count ratio
(divide both counts by 5). This arithmetic describes only the invented dataset;
it is not an inheritance experiment, a guaranteed offspring ratio or evidence
about human traits. The individual poster distinguishes museum fact, classroom
model-data result and an unanswered question about real plant observations.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "Which source supports which kind of claim?"
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Read the short heritage card and source cards; model history versus invented data, one tally and the sample-comparison question.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each counts both rows, records R/W/total and compares equal sample sizes; records one museum fact/source and one limit. Make a poster with original pea symbols and separate source/model-result panels.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Partners check counts against labels, not memory; each explains 15/5 and its 3:1 ratio. Ask "Where did that result come from?" Revise a misleading caption while retaining the original; name a question the model cannot answer.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual A/B counts, one numerical comparison, supported historical claim/source/date/limit and revised caption; distinguish model arithmetic from historical or biological evidence.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Save posters/notes, cap/count tools, reuse scraps and tidy.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"Which result did you count yourself? What real records would compare plants
honestly? Does our model prove inheritance? Does a catalog prove a quotation?"
Each has both correct 10-item tallies, a supported A/B comparison, a sourced
historical claim, provenance/date, model/history limit and reader-driven revision.
The combined count ratio must be checked against 15/5, not recalled as biography.
1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent; 4 distinguishes source types and
explains a further observation needed; NE if missing.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

Source page offline -> supplied verified packet; do not invent fresh claims.
Count mismatch -> mark each R/W once and compare the printed labels; keep the
correction. Too much text -> one historical fact and one model-data result.
Missing markers -> pencil. No plants, food, biological sampling, personal-trait
or belief interviews, copied images/quotes, public media or browsing without
school policy. Never infer a pupil's genes or Mendel's private faith motives.
Stop/report unsafe data or device use.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: read cards aloud, point/tally each of ten labels, use a table
and comparison stem; scribing permitted. Grade 6/challenge: check 15/5 as
75%/25% of the 20 labels and explain why model proportions do not establish
real inheritance; distinguish a museum record from a catalog referring to
the same work. No simplistic "always supported" history. Indoor source study.
