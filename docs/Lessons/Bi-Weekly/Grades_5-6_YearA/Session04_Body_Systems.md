## BEFORE CLASS / MATERIALS

Review contact/accommodations; do not ask health/disability stories. Seat
4/5/7/9 teams. Copy complete cards below; draw simple lung/heart/body boxes.
Prepare paper arrows, word labels and four-chamber heart inset; no tubes,
shared breathing, fluids or specimen sourcing. Adult checks arrow direction.

| Item | Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Paper model/evidence; pencil | 1 each/student | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Cardstock arrow sheet; fact sheet; ruler; red/blue marker set | 1 each/team | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Board; clock; roster | 1 each/teacher | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Paper consumable; tools reusable. Use words/patterns with colors.

## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Respiration:** processes involving gas exchange/energy use; distinguish
breathing movement from cellular respiration. **Circulation:** blood transport.
**Alveoli:** small lung air sacs. **System:** interacting parts.
**Common misconception:** blue diagram means real blue human blood.
Blood is red; color/pattern indicates relatively more/less oxygen, both streams
carry gases. **If asked, "Does the brain start every heartbeat?"** Specialized
pacemaker cells initiate beats; nerves help regulate rate.

**Complete cards/key:** Air moves through nose/mouth, trachea, branching
airways to alveoli. Oxygen moves into nearby blood; carbon dioxide moves toward
air and is exhaled. Heart's right side pumps toward lungs, left toward body;
vessels return blood. Cells use oxygen/nutrients and produce carbon dioxide.
Digestive system supplies absorbed nutrients; muscles help breathing/movement;
nervous system helps regulation. Model arrows: BODY -> right HEART -> LUNGS
-> left HEART -> BODY. Four chambers (two atria/two ventricles); boxes omit
capillaries/valves/relative size/time. No clinical numbers measured.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "How does oxygen get from air to cells?" State dignity boundary.
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Read cards, model full loop and correct red/blue misconception.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each draws lungs/heart/body and labeled directed arrows, heart inset and one other-system connection; teams check cards.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Move a paper oxygen label along path, carbon dioxide back; every pupil traces without pretending to be a patient. Peer asks one model question; revise drawing.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual function/connection, five-part path, blood-color/model limit and dignity independent of health explanation.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Save diagrams, cap/count tools, sort scraps and tidy.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"Where do gases exchange? What does a blue arrow mean? What did boxes omit?"
Each has correct directed loop, functions, four chambers/model limit and
dignity statement. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent; 4 adds accurate
system connection; NE if missing. Not a clinical/anatomy certification.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

Arrow backwards -> reread cards, retain correction. Confusing detail -> simplify
to loop before inset. Missing colors -> words/hatching preserve objective.
No pulse/breath counts, exercise challenges, breath holding, body samples,
disease diagnosis, shared mouthpieces or private health discussion. Stop/report
distress or unsafe condition; do not diagnose in class.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: labeled boxes, tactile arrows, oral/scribed explanation.
Grade 6/challenge: explain nutrient/gas connection and why model is not to scale.
Seated indoor model; no outdoor/exercise alternative.

## CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER

Keep each model/correction, count tools, note unanswered science questions.
**Explored:** body cooperation. **Did:** traced gas/transport model.
**Learned:** functions and limits. **Catholic connection:** equal dignity.
**Ask:** "What did blue mean in your diagram?" No routine homework or health measurement.
