# Grades 5-6 Year A: Bi-Weekly C-STREAM 🚀

## Overview
**Grade Band:** 5-6 | **Duration:** 45 minutes per session | **Sessions:** 17 over the school year | **Rotation Year:** A

This bi-weekly schedule delivers advanced C-STREAM experiences for intermediate students with Catholic integration throughout.

## October 2026 review and teaching conditions

**17 lesson documents, 17 planned 45-minute meetings (765 minutes)**. This is an
alternative to weekly, not an additional full curriculum. Service implementation,
extended programming, and invited exhibitions require separately approved time;
they are not silently included in the 17 meetings.

Read the [Grades 5-6 review](../../../Review/Grades_5-6_Review.md) and
[69-lesson audit](../../../Review/Grades_5-6_Audit.csv). Scratch Advanced and
Christmas Electronics are fully rebuilt. Other lessons have targeted fixes and
individual local checks but **are not fully substitute-certified**. Shortened
research/app/invention sessions need tighter scope and prepared materials.

Check measurement, sequencing, comparison, and diagram-reading prerequisites.
Grade 5 uses reference cards/numbered steps; Grade 6 adds independent tracing,
uncertainty, and tradeoff justification. Neither A nor B assumes the other has
already been taken. A 45-minute introduction is not platform mastery.

Sphero/Little Bits loans, compatible devices, and accounts are not confirmed.
Protected-AA circuit kits are the inexpensive primary electronics path; no
button cells. Paper coding/app routes teach algorithms/interfaces, not actual
device programming. Biology uses prepared images/models, not body samples,
cultures, or alcohol extraction. Outdoor work has an indoor alternative.

Unsourced Scripture translations, saint sayings/biographies, historical
superlatives, current roles, and numerical statistics are **VERIFICATION REQUIRED
before teaching**; prefer verified paraphrase. Acutis and Frassati were canonized
September 7, 2025; older resources may still have outdated "Blessed" labels.
Local CST codes are not official benchmarks; official alignment **VERIFICATION
REQUIRED**. No routine homework or required home device/donation.

## Year A vs Year B
Year A focuses on:

- Human body systems (Year B: Ecosystems & Environment)

- Sphero navigation/algorithm modeling (Year B: stationary sensor-threshold modeling, not drones)

- App Inventor introduction/interface modeling (Year B: user-centered app modeling, not Python)

Students in combined 5-6 classrooms alternate between Year A and Year B content.
