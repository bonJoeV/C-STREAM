# Session 6: Gratitude Tech

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly A; Gratitude communication; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** create/run two digital slides naming a helpful action and revise
their readability for a peer. **Why:** presentation software supports editable
visual communication, not merely screen consumption.
Catholic/CST-C2 respectful thanks; CST-T1 actual edit/present/save;
CST-T3 private sharing decision; CST-A3 critique/revision.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **Required**;
novice. Prep 20 after approved presentation software works; installation extra;
cleanup 5. No podcast/QR/photo workflow or compulsory personal disclosure.

## Before class and exact supplies

Use approved offline **LibreOffice Impress**: blank presentation, `Slide > New
Slide`, `Insert > Text Box`, `Slide Show > Start from First Slide`, Escape,
`File > Save As` local `.odp`; test/reopen two-slide sample. Leave sample/save
location. If this app unavailable, teacher pretests equivalent school software
and leaves exact commands; otherwise paper pathway.
Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) computer/team; paper/pencil per child. Board/timer, demo on
issued device. Software is access resource, not physical supply or price.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Computers (1/team) | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Paper; pencils (1/pupil each) | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Slide = display page; audience = reader; contrast = visible difference;
consent = permission. Slide 1 "Thank you, classroom helpers"; slide 2 "You
prepare our shared tools." Original fictional/classroom message, no quoted
biography. **Misconception:** digitally made means publicly shared.
**If asked "Can we send it?"** Adult checks recipient consent/school approval
first; this lesson assesses local display, not delivery.
Dry devices, no names/photos/voices/passwords/cloud upload; stop/report damage.
Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "What helpful action can thanks name?" Optional gratitude prayer.
2. **4-10:** Demo blank/two slides/text boxes/present/Escape and privacy rule.
3. **10-21:** Every pupil edits/presents for three minutes, peers plan/read;
   each records one actual command/action.
4. **21-29:** Reader states helpful action/message at normal viewing distance;
   record feedback, revise font/wording, present again.
5. **29-35:** Each child explains before/after choice and private delivery plan;
   roster marks actual edit/present/save separately.
6. **35-40:** Save/reopen locally, exit, return device, collect dated evidence.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: specific respectful message, observed digital actions, reader-backed
revision and safe sharing choice. Grade 3/support: copied sample/motor assistance/
dictation. Grade 4/challenge: contrast/space tradeoff. Wrong text box: select
slide/box then edit; no audio workaround. No app/device: two paper slides and
same reader test, communication/privacy only, **digital operation not observed**.
Half devices allow two waves of turns within work windows; below that reschedule.
Early finish: new reader, not more untested formats.

**Family:** We made private thank-you slides and revised for a reader. Ask,
"What made the message clearer?" Optional thanks, no home app.
## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

Prepare the two-slide template and evidence slip after the approved offline
presentation program has been opened, saved, closed, and reopened successfully.

- **Student material (creator/reader evidence slip):** Print these exact prompts: `The classroom
  helper/action I am thanking is ___`; `Slide 1 exact text: ___`; `Slide 2
  exact text: ___`; `I personally used this command/action: ___`; `At normal
  viewing distance my reader could state the helpful action: YES / NOT YET`;
  `Reader's exact feedback: ___`; `Before, ___`; `I revised ___`; `After, ___`;
  `I presented again: YES / NO`; `I saved and reopened locally: YES / NO /
  PAPER PATH`; `Safe delivery choice: keep local / adult checks consent and
  school approval`. With no printer, pupils fold paper into `MESSAGE`,
  `READER TEST`, `REVISION`, and `PRIVACY` quarters and copy the twelve prompts.
- **Evidence and answer guidance:** The message must name a specific,
  respectful action such as preparing shared tools, shelving books, cleaning
  tables, or helping classmates--not disclose a private story or identify a
  child. Accept an observed New Slide, Text Box, text edit, Slide Show, Escape,
  or Save As action for the personal command field; separately roster actual
  edit, present, and save. Reader feedback may identify small text, low
  contrast, crowded wording, unclear action, or excess words. Valid revisions
  include larger type, shorter wording, more spacing, or stronger approved
  foreground/background contrast. `Send/post it` is not an acceptable delivery
  choice without adult consent and school approval.
- **Worked example:** Build Slide 1 `Thank you,
  classroom helpers.` and Slide 2 `You prepare our shared tools.` Present at
  normal viewing distance. Record reader feedback `Slide 2 is too small`;
  enlarge that line, present again, then save/reopen a local `.odp`.
  The finished example is exactly two readable slides plus a slip tying exact feedback to
  a before/after change and a private delivery decision. On the paper pathway,
  save/reopen and software-operation entries are `N/A--paper pathway`; the
  two paper slides, reader test, revision, and privacy choice remain required.
- **Board setup and visual example:** Keep a side-by-side paper or screen image
  of `BEFORE: small/crowded` and `AFTER: larger/shorter`, viewed from the same
  marked distance. That paired image is the visual example pupils compare their
  own slides against. Show a local-folder icon crossed with a cloud/upload icon.
  Board workflow: `NAME ACTION -> MAKE 2 SLIDES -> PRESENT -> READER REPEATS
  ACTION -> RECORD FEEDBACK -> REVISE -> PRESENT AGAIN -> SAVE/REOPEN LOCAL`.
  Keep a roster beside it with separate `edit / present / save` columns.
- **Discussion answers and misconceptions:** Ask `How do we know the revision
  helped?` The same reader can identify the action at normal distance after the
  change, or gives clearer follow-up feedback. `Is "Thank you for everything"
  specific enough?` It is respectful but should name the helpful action.
  `Does making it digitally mean we may send it?` No; digital creation and
  public/directed sharing are separate, and recipient consent plus school
  approval come first. `Is decoration evidence of readability?` No; font size,
  contrast, spacing, wording, and reader response are evidence. Correct the
  misconception that more colors/animations automatically improve clarity.
- **Station package and reset:** Each team station contains one compatible
  school computer, tested offline LibreOffice Impress or pretested equivalent,
  blank two-slide starter, known local save folder, and one `NO NAMES / NO
  UPLOAD` card; each pupil gets paper/pencil. Kit label: `G3-4 YA S06 | GRATITUDE
  SLIDES STATION | LOCAL ODP | NO ACCOUNT/UPLOAD`. Cleanup and reset by confirming only
  two slides, saving/reopening the local file, closing the slide show with
  Escape, exiting the application according to school procedure, clearing no
  files except through the school's routine, returning the computer, and
  logging whose edit/present/save actions were observed and any reopen failure.
- **If a product is unfinished:** On the evidence slip circle `message / Slide
  1 / Slide 2 / present / reader feedback / revision / present again / save /
  reopen` and note the last completed slide plus local filename/station.
  Resume on the assigned school device; do not email a file, use a home account,
  collect a home recipient response, or mark a partner's operation as complete.
- **Annotated exemplar and response to errors:** Underline the named helpful
  action, box the reader's exact words, bracket the before/after entries, circle
  the matching revision, and star the private delivery choice. If feedback says
  `too small` but only color changes, the revision does not address the evidence.
  If the reader can say `thank you` but not identify the action, Slide 2 needs
  clearer wording. If a pupil lists `Save As` with no roster observation, mark
  operation unverified rather than inferred. Reteach by modeling New Slide,
  Text Box, Slide Show, Escape, and local Save As once, then use the frame
  `My reader noticed ___, so I changed ___; on the second reading ___`.
- **Safety classification:** Severity **low** and likelihood **low**
  with dry, seated equipment. Adult ratio is **1:25 maximum**, with
  teams of three or fewer and a timed individual device turn. Keep liquids and
  cords away, stop for damaged equipment, and collect no student/helper names,
  photos, voices, passwords, email addresses, or personal stories. No cleaner
  is used in the lesson; if an adult later uses a cleaner, that separate
  product must be school approved and handled under its SDS.
- **Accessible paper/screen design:** Evidence slips use 14-point minimum black
  type, bold section headings, wide writing lines, and labeled checkboxes.
  Slides use large sans-serif text, strong contrast, plain backgrounds, and no
  meaning conveyed by color alone; avoid flashing animation. Read prompts
  aloud and permit dictation, scribing, oral reader feedback, paper slides,
  keyboard/mouse assistance, or an approved alternative input. Paper fallback
  preserves communication evidence but not digital-operation credit.
- **Technology/external-service compliance:** Use approved offline LibreOffice
  Impress or an equivalent pretested school application and local `.odp` or
  approved local format. No login, cloud sync, upload, QR code, email, public
  link, image search, camera, microphone, personal data, or actual delivery
  occurs in this lesson. Any later sending requires separate adult consent,
  recipient consent, and school policy approval. Paper or unplugged work is not
  executed-device evidence; Impress operation remains deferred/not observed
  until the individual edit, present, save, and reopen turn is witnessed.
