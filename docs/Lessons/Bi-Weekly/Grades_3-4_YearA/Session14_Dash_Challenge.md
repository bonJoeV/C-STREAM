# Session 14: Dash Challenge

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly A; Computing; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** edit/run/stop one L-route, measure endpoint error and retest a
deliberate correction. **Why:** real robotics reveals traction/start errors
that paper cannot. Catholic/CST-C2 meaningful individual turns; CST-T1 operation/
troubleshooting; CST-T2 sequence/debug; CST-M1 cm comparison.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **Required**;
developing with novice rehearsal. Prep 20 after charging/pairing, installation/
loan extra; cleanup 5. No five missions or imaginary tape sensing.

## Before class and exact supplies

Follow model/Blockly verification in [Session 2](Session02_Dash_Code.md), unchanged.
Two charged Dash/tablet pairs; teacher handles chargers. Verify exact labels for
`forward 20 cm; right 90 degrees; forward 20 cm`, low speed, start/stop twice.
Two clear 1 m square areas with soft cardboard boundaries, no doors/stairs.
Class **400 cm floor-safe tape** marks starts/targets, not full perimeter.
Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) each ruler/two route sheets; each pupil evidence sheet/pencil.
Demo uses station kit. Post two-minute slots, five rounds maximum/two stations.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Relative turn = from current facing; input = command; output = movement;
endpoint error = distance from intended endpoint.
**Misconception:** Dash senses floor tape. **If asked "Why miss?"** Check start/
facing, units and surface before one distance change. No sensor claims.
Low speed, dry lanes; hands off moving robot, stop before retrieval; teacher
controls power/charging. Stop/report unsafe lane or pairing failure. Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "What could cause a correct paper route to miss physically?"
2. **4-10:** Demo verified L/start/stop; each rehearses three paper commands.
3. **10-22:** Two stations/five two-minute rounds. Every pupil edits one setting,
   runs/stops, peers measure error; restore agreed baseline between turns.
   Waiting teams trace/debug an incorrect left turn on paper.
4. **22-32:** Same slots for chosen one-setting correction/retest. Record actual
   baseline/revised error and fixed start/surface; individual explanation.
5. **32-35:** Each trace, cm results and correction reason; roster logs actual
   edit/run/stop, not only team success.
6. **35-40:** Stop/off/store by adult, return rulers/cards, restore lanes/collect.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: correct route/debug reasoning and observed operation/measurement.
Grade 3/support: three cards/ruler help/scribing. Grade 4/challenge: repeated
whole-cm errors and traction limitation, no ratios. Pairing fails after two
minutes: paper path/report. One robot cannot cover all turns in these windows:
record actual completed turns and schedule missing operations, don't claim all.
No devices: same windows token route, wrong turn/change/retest; CT only,
**robot operation/measurement not observed**. Early finish: repeat same safe route.

**Family:** We tested a precision route. Ask, "What caused the mismatch?"
Say paper debugging if hardware pathway was unavailable.
## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

Issue one **Student material (L-route test record) per pupil**, or rule the same record on the
board: `Baseline: forward 20 cm / right 90 degrees / forward 20 cm`; `Draw the
predicted L and mark its endpoint`; `Baseline endpoint error: ____ cm`;
`Direction from target: short / long / left / right`; `I changed only ____
from ____ to ____`; `Start mark, facing and floor stayed the same: yes / no`;
`Retest error: ____ cm`; `The correction helped / did not help because ____`;
`Teacher saw me edit / run / stop: ____`. Include a small blank square for the
route trace and a line for the measurer's initials. A no-print pupil may copy the
table, dictate numbers or point to direction choices, but must take an individual
robot turn for the operation boxes.

- **Evidence and answer guidance:** A complete record shows the verified
  three-command L, an actual whole-centimeter baseline error, one deliberate
  distance change, the unchanged start/facing/surface, a second actual error and
  a comparison such as "12 cm became 5 cm, so the change moved the endpoint
  closer." Also accept an unsuccessful controlled correction when honestly
  reported: "8 cm became 11 cm; I would reverse or reduce my change next time."
  The changed setting may be either forward distance if the observed miss supports
  it; the right turn remains 90 degrees for this precision route. Do not accept a
  guessed measurement, simultaneous speed/turn/distance changes, a different
  route, or another pupil's edit/run/stop.
- **Worked example:** Demonstrate a baseline that ends 6 cm short on
  the second leg. Record `second forward: 20 cm`, change only that value to 26 cm,
  replace Dash exactly on the start mark with the same facing, rerun and record a
  2 cm error. Circle `helped` and write, "The endpoint was 4 cm closer, although
  wheel traction still caused error."
  The finished example is that posted route trace with its paired measurements.
  For the paper-token fallback, show the command correction but
  mark physical endpoint measurements and robot edit/run/stop **N/A - not
  observed**.
- **Board setup and visual example:** At each station visibly tape one start line, one
  facing arrow and one intended endpoint inside the clear 1 m square; place the
  metric ruler at the target and cardboard boundary beyond the lane; this taped
  lane is the visual example pupils copy. On the board
  draw the 20 cm by 20 cm L and a table headed `RUN | START/FACING | COMMAND
  CHANGED | ERROR (cm) | CLOSER/FARTHER`, with the reminder `Change ONE setting;
  tape is a marker, not a sensor`.
- **Discussion with acceptable response:** Ask, "Why can correct blocks still miss the target?"
  Accept wheel traction, slight start/facing differences, surface variation or
  measurement limits. Ask, "What makes the retest fair?" Accept the same robot,
  lane, surface, start and facing with only one command setting changed. Ask,
  "What does a smaller endpoint error show?" Accept that this trial ended closer,
  not that every future run will be exact. Ask, "Did Dash follow the floor tape?"
  Accept no; it executed programmed movement and the tape only helped people mark
  positions. Correct the misconceptions that Dash senses tape, that any changed
  route is a controlled test and that one successful run proves perfect accuracy.
- **Kit contents, kit label and reset:** Each of the two stations contains one charged
  Dash, one paired compatible tablet with the verified low-speed project, one
  metric ruler, route/evidence sheets, pencils, start/target tape and a soft
  cardboard boundary. Kit label on both tubs: `S14 DASH L-ROUTE - LOW SPEED - HANDS OFF
  WHILE MOVING - ADULT CHARGING`. Between pupils, stop the robot and restore the
  20-right-20 baseline unless the teacher has recorded the deliberate change.
  Final cleanup and reset: adult powers down and stores Dash/tablet/charger, pupils return
  rulers/cards, remove only the temporary lane tape, stack boundaries and submit
  a roster of completed and deferred individual turns.
- **Unfinished work:** Check the last valid stage on the record: `paper rehearsal /
  baseline run / first measure / one-setting edit / retest / explanation`. Write
  the actual last error and reserve the next school station slot; do not copy a
  teammate's number or mark the whole team complete. Pairing failure after two
  minutes moves the pupil to paper debugging and leaves robot operation and
  physical measurement unobserved.
- **Annotated exemplar, common error reading and reteach:** On the exemplar, underline the one
  changed block, box both centimeter errors, draw identical start arrows on both
  trials and add a margin note `6 - 2 = 4 cm closer`. A moved start/facing or
  several changed values is a common error that makes the retest
  `uncontrolled - no comparison`; retest only when a safe slot exists. Reteach
  confused left/right by physically turning
  a paper arrow from its current facing. If measurement begins at the robot rather
  than endpoint-to-target gap, model ruler zero at one point and read the other.
  Use the stem `Baseline error __ cm; I changed __ only; retest error __ cm, so
  __`.
- **Safety classification:** Severity **moderate** (trip/collision or battery/
  charger damage) and likelihood **low** with the defined lanes. Adult ratio is
  **1:3 maximum**: one adult
  supervises one team of no more than three at a station; the teacher alone handles
  chargers and power concerns. Use low speed on a dry floor, keep hands and feet
  outside the lane, stop before retrieval and stop/report pairing damage or an
  unsafe boundary. The used commercial product category is floor-safe tape.
  Enter the exact manufacturer/product in the [Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv),
  record whether an SDS applies and the school approval decision, and keep the
  tape on **HOLD** until that review is resolved. Also follow manufacturer
  battery/charger guidance and school damaged-device procedures.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Print the route at least 10 cm wide with
  thick high-contrast black-and-white arrows, 16-point labels, uncluttered centimeter boxes and
  text labels in addition to direction/color cues, so no answer depends on color alone. Read blocks aloud; offer
  command cards, a paper robot, ruler alignment help, scribing and a seated
  programmer/measurer role without substituting for the pupil's edit/run/stop.
- **Device compliance:** Use only the preapproved school Dash/tablet and verified
  local Blockly workflow. No pupil account, cloud project, camera, microphone,
  location data or upload is needed. Required technology evidence is an observed
  individual edit, run and stop; paper or unplugged work is not executed-device
  evidence, and operation remains deferred/not observed until that individual
  turn is witnessed. The token route assesses sequencing/debugging only.
