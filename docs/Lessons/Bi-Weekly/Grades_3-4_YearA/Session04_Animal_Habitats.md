# Session 4: Animal Habitats

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly A; Life science; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** model food/water/shelter/space needs, support one organism-specific
relationship and label unknown details honestly. **Why:** habitat care requires
evidence, not pretending animals are perfectly suited to any environment.
Catholic/CST-C3 responsible habitat-care choice; CST-S3 needs/model limit;
CST-A1 explanatory labeled model. Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.
Technology **None**; introductory. Prep 10; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9): cardstock base, two markers, source card.
Per pupil paper/pencil. Teacher board/timer; copy card from retrieved
[Monarch Joint Venture life cycle](https://www.monarchjointventure.org/monarch-biology/life-cycle):
monarch larvae eat milkweed; adults and larvae are different life stages.
Card gives **one food relationship**, not all habitat facts. Water, shelter and
space placements in model are proposed needs diagrams, not approved live-animal
housing. Teacher demo from issued paper; no specimens/natural-material collecting.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Habitat = place providing conditions/resources; need = requirement; adaptation
= inherited feature useful in conditions; model = representation.
**Misconception:** every animal can live in any box with food.
**If asked "Can we put a caterpillar here?"** No; this drawing is not a checked
care plan. Species requirements need further evidence/adult ecological approval.
No animals, unknown plants, mold, sun-focused lenses or classroom releases.
Paper indoors, stop/report damaged materials. Winter fallback is the primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "What would responsible habitat care require?" Optional care prayer.
2. **4-10:** Read source card; model supported food claim versus proposed needs.
3. **10-22:** Each sketches; team builds folded-paper habitat explanation on
   base, labels four needs and larva/milkweed link.
4. **22-30:** Peer checks all needs/labels, questions unsupported claims;
   revise one misleading feature, mark unknown species detail as unknown.
5. **30-35:** Each child names four needs, explains sourced link and model limit,
   draws one labeled relationship and care choice.
6. **35-40:** Cap/count tools/cards, save dated models/evidence, clear.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: four needs, supported relationship, readable model and honest limit.
Grade 3/support: four pictured boxes/read-aloud/scribing. Grade 4/challenge:
compare larval/adult questions needing further sources, no guessed diets.
No cards: copy supplied facts/citation to board. Overclaim: replace "perfectly
designed home" with proposed model and evidence needed. Early finish: identify
safe future observation, not collect wild creatures.

**Family:** We modeled habitat needs responsibly. Ask, "Which relationship
had a source?" Optional: notice an animal picture, no collection.
