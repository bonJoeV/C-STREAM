## Vocabulary, background and safety

**Circuit:** complete conducting path, including return. **Open:** interrupted.
**Closed:** complete. **Switch:** opens/closes the path. **Current:** charge flow.
The battery supplies energy; light symbolism is not an electrical explanation
of faith or human dignity. A lamp project does not establish LED polarity mastery.

**Misconception:** touching any two conductors makes a safe circuit.
**If asked:** "Why is it off?" Trace the approved path while power is isolated;
ask the adult to check contacts/cells. Never short the supply.

**SAFETY:** follow the guide's manufacturer/school gate, adult source isolation
before snap changes and adult inspection before power is restored. No pupil
battery access, coin cells, mains/USB power, extra loads, fan blades, live shorts,
bare LED substitutions or take-home circuits. Warm/smelly/leaking/damaged:
stop/report, adult isolates and marks OUT OF SERVICE.

## Numbered sequence: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4: Purpose/symbol.** Prayer: "Jesus, help us share hope and care for one another." Explain symbol versus physical explanation and the real-turn rule.
2. **4-8: Model safely.** Teacher traces the approved full path, demonstrates OFF/ON and models source isolation/inspection.
3. **8-10: Predict individually.** Each child traces battery -> switch -> light -> return and predicts both states.
4. **10-22: Wave A.** Follow the guide's isolated team build, adult checks, each child's two OFF/ON cycles and one teacher-managed open-path repair/retest. Waiting teams draw diagrams and draft an Advent message with a named reader.
5. **22-23: Reset.** Adult isolates power and transfers/resets station kits.
6. **23-35: Wave B / communicate.** Remaining teams get the identical hardware routine; finished teams peer-check and revise one message detail, record repair evidence and answer individual path/state questions. At smaller enrollment all teams use this window for retests/feedback. Ask: "Where is the gap? What showed the repair worked? What does your message mean?"
7. **35-40: Cleanup.** Isolate/count/return parts and cells, store dry kits and collect sheets. Record any missing physical turns explicitly.

## Success, support and alternatives

Each child traces a full path, predicts and operates both states twice,
explains a repair using observed evidence and revises a respectful message.
Record physical contribution/test **observed / prompted / not yet** separately
from reasoning/art. Score **1** decoration only; **2** prompted path;
**3** independent path/state/repair and message evidence; **4** explains a model
limitation or two possible open-path faults without unsafe live testing.

- **Grade 3/support:** traced diagram, large words/symbols, oral responses,
  motor help for snaps; still collect each child's prediction and switch turn.
- **Grade 4/challenge:** compare two drawn faults; no extra branches or loads.
- **25 pupils:** nine pairs/trios, five stations, two 12-minute waves.
  Each child must have an observed turn; defer unfinished evidence rather than
  rush inspection. No four-person hardware groups.
- **No working kit:** isolate/report, use a checked reserve or the guide's
  verified protected-AA route. Never connect Snap parts to that supply.
- **Paper route:** use identical times with drawn paths and an on/off token.
  Record **circuit reasoning/art only; electrical operation not observed**.
  A demonstration alone does not prove each child's assembly/testing.

## Family snippet

We traced, predicted and tested a switched light, then revised a hopeful Advent
message. Physical light is a symbol of Christ, not a theological experiment.
Describe the actual hardware or paper route. **Ask:** "Where is the gap when
the light is off?" Only paper goes home; no electrical homework or purchases.
