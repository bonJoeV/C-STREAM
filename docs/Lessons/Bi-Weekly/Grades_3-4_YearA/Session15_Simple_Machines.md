# Session 15: Simple Machines

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly A; Machines; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** label load/effort/fulcrum, compare two fulcrum positions and
explain a measured arm-length change with qualitative effort.
**Why:** tools trade force/distance/direction; they do not create energy.
Catholic/CST-C3 dignified careful work; CST-S2 fixed load/change; CST-S3 lever
system; CST-A1 labeled diagram; CST-M1 arm lengths.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**;
introductory. Prep 15; cleanup 5. Six machines introduced, not six rushed builds.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9): rigid 30 cm ruler, broad low wooden fulcrum block, paper
cup, five identical plastic counters, **20 cm tape**. Per pupil two paper sheets/
pencil. Teacher board/timer/demo from kit. Tape cup near ruler's zero end;
counter load stays five. Pretest low lift at fulcrum **10 cm then 5 cm** from
load end; pupil presses opposite end gently until cup lifts <=1 cm.
Draw six reference examples: ramp, wedge, screw, lever, wheel/axle, pulley.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Fulcrum = pivot; load = lifted object; effort = applied force; arm = pivot-to-
load/effort distance. Moving pivot toward load lengthens effort arm, generally
reducing needed effort but changing travel. Felt effort is not calibrated force.
**Misconception:** machine makes free energy. **If asked "How much easier?"**
We can describe feeling, not claim a Newton measurement without a force tool.
Low desk only; no knives/axes, suspended loads, elastic launches, pinches,
heavy weights or swinging ruler. Stop/report broken ruler; indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "How do good tools help workers?" Optional dignified-work prayer.
2. **4-10:** Show six examples, model lever/lift cap and arm reading.
3. **10-22:** Every pupil tests 10 cm fulcrum, records arms 10/20 cm and
   qualitative effort; rotate roles, same five-counter load.
4. **22-30:** Move fulcrum to 5 cm, repeat; arms 5/25 cm, observe load/effort
   movement. "What changed, and what did we hold constant?"
5. **30-35:** Each child draws labeled two-position diagram, measured arms/control
   and honest effort limit plus tool-care choice.
6. **35-40:** Remove counters/count, untape/store cups/rulers/blocks, file records.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: four arm lengths, correct labels/control and force-distance explanation
without invented force data. Grade 3/support: premarked positions/diagram/
dictation. Grade 4/challenge: compare arm differences/travel tradeoff, no
mechanical-advantage ratio required. Sliding cup: stop/reset, repeat invalid
trial. No block: teacher supplies stable broad low eraser under ruler, same
positions; if unsafe, diagram only and physical test not observed. Early finish:
identify one different machine's function from reference picture.

**Family:** We tested lever positions and tool tradeoffs. Ask, "Where was the
fulcrum relative to the load?" Optional: identify a safe tool by sight.
