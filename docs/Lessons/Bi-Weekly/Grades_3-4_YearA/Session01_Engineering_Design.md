# Session 1: Engineering Design

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly A; Foundations; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** define a display need, build/test a standing tower and retest a
base change with measured evidence. **Why:** engineering is solving a problem,
not winning tallest. Catholic/CST-C2 fair access/user need; CST-E1 criterion/
limits; CST-E2 retest; CST-M1 cm measurement. Official alignment **VERIFICATION
REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; novice. Prep 10; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9). Each: four build paper sheets, ruler, **40 cm tape**.
Each pupil: evidence paper/pencil. Teacher board/timer; demo from issued sheet.
Mark 20 cm square bases. Brief: visitor needs a visible desk marker **>=15 cm**,
standing ten seconds, **40 cm height cap**. Tools reusable, paper/tape consumable.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rulers (1/team) | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Build sheets (4/team) | 16 | 20 | 28 | 36 |
| Tape (40 cm/team, cm total) | 160 | 200 | 280 | 360 |
| Evidence paper; pencils (1/pupil each) | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Criterion = performance; constraint = limit; prototype = trial; redesign =
evidence-based change. ASK/IMAGINE/PLAN/CREATE/TEST/IMPROVE may repeat.
**Misconception:** tallest is the only success. **If asked "What if it falls?"**
Record failed standing trial, change base, retest; no judgment of child.
Desk paper only, no loads/climbing or snapping rulers. Stop/report damaged
tools. Indoor primary, no unsourced saint analogy needed.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "Who needs to see the marker?" Optional original service prayer.
2. **4-9:** Model roll/fold, zero-based height and hands-off ten seconds.
3. **9-21:** Each sketches, team selects/builds, rotate builder/tester/recorder.
4. **21-30:** Measure/standing test; change base with same stock, retest.
5. **30-35:** Each records two heights/pass results, criterion/limit, kept-same
   condition and fair-turn action. "Which failure informed your change?"
6. **35-40:** Count rulers, flatten reusable paper, save dated evidence/clear.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: user/criteria, two actual tests, justified change and individual fair
turn. Grade 3/support: starter roll, scribing; same criterion. Grade 4/challenge:
repeat standing trial and explain variation, no added weights.
Collapse: widen/shorten, record result. No tape: folding-only limit for all,
explicitly changed constraint. Early finish: new viewer, same height cap.

**Family:** We tested and retested a display tower. Ask, "What did your first
test teach you?" Optional: notice a structure in a picture, no home building.
## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

Prepare this pack for the paper-tower test; it does not alter the 15 cm
criterion, 40 cm cap, 20 cm base area, or ten-second hands-off test.

- **Student material, printable or no-print:** Use one half-sheet per pupil with
  these exact prompts: `Who needs the desk marker? ___`; `Sketch and label the
  base`; `I predict it will be ___ cm and stand / fall`; `TEST 1: ___ cm;
  stood 10 seconds? YES / NO`; `I changed the base by ___`; `TEST 2: ___ cm;
  stood 10 seconds? YES / NO`; `I kept ___ the same`; `My fair-turn action was
  ___`; `The first test taught me ___`. For no-print delivery, copy those nine
  prompts to the board and have pupils number 1-9 on plain paper.
- **Evidence and answer guidance:** Look for a viewer such as a visitor,
  teacher, pupil, or classroom helper; a labeled base sketch; two observed
  heights measured from desk zero; two stand/fall results; a specific base
  revision; and one controlled condition. Accept `wider base`, `more folds at
  the bottom`, or `shorter/wider tower` as base changes. Accept `same four
  sheets`, `same tape allowance`, `same 20 cm square`, or `same ten seconds` as
  controls. A 12 cm tower that stands or a 22 cm tower that falls is valid test
  evidence, but does not meet the full criterion.
- **Worked example:** Work aloud: `visitor -> predict 24 cm/stand
  -> Test 1 21 cm/NO -> widen folded feet -> Test 2 18 cm/YES -> same paper and
  ten-second test`. The finished example has the sketch, both real measurements,
  both outcomes, and the sentence `The wider base helped because Test 2 stood
  for ten seconds.` N/A is not appropriate for Test 2; if time ends, mark it
  `not tested`, not successful.
- **Board setup and visual example:** At the demo table place a 20 cm square, a
  ruler with zero touching the desk, and one intentionally narrow-footed tower.
  Draw a two-row chart: `version | height cm | 10 s stand? | base description`.
  Across the top write `VISIBLE >=15 cm | HEIGHT <=40 cm | HANDS OFF 10 s`;
  keep a separate `prediction` column so guesses are not copied as results.
- **Discussion with acceptable response:** Ask `Can a shorter tower be the
  better solution?`
  Accept yes, if it is visible, within the cap, and stable. Ask `What makes the
  retest useful?` Accept one named base change while materials and procedure
  remain the same. Ask `What should we write when it falls at second 6?` Accept
  `NO` and the observed height, not 6 seconds as a pass. Correct the ideas that
  tallest automatically wins, that holding the tower counts, or that a failed
  first version earns no evidence.
- **Kit contents, kit label and reset:** Bag exactly four plain build sheets and
  a 40 cm premeasured tape strip with one metric ruler; issue each pupil one
  evidence sheet and pencil. Kit label: `G3-4 YA S01 | PAPER TOWER | 4 SHEETS /
  40 CM TAPE / 1 RULER`. Cleanup and reset by removing tape scraps, flattening reusable
  paper separately from used paper, returning one undamaged ruler, and noting
  any short tape allocation before the next class.
- **Unfinished work:** On the pupil record circle the next required stage:
  `sketch / build / Test 1 / base change / Test 2 / explanation`. Write the last
  measured result beside it, place the team build and named records together,
  and resume at school with the original material allowance; do not send the
  construction home or invent a second result.
- **Annotated exemplar, common error reading and reteach:** On the finished
  sample, underline
  the user and criterion, box the two measured results, circle the base change,
  and draw an arrow from the Test 1 failure to the revision. `24 cm` in the
  prediction box but no Test 1 value means measurement evidence is missing;
  `45 cm/YES` means stable but over the cap; identical descriptions for both
  bases mean the claimed redesign is not visible. Reteach by measuring a
  teacher tower from zero and completing `I changed ___; I kept ___; the result
  changed from ___ to ___`.
- **Safety classification:** Severity **low** and likelihood **low** with
  desk-height paper only. Adult ratio is **1:25 maximum**, with teams of no
  more than three and the adult circulating during tests. Prohibit climbing,
  body-weight loads, ruler snapping, tape on skin, and thrown prototypes.
  The used commercial product category is masking tape. Enter the exact
  manufacturer/product in the [Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv),
  record whether an SDS applies and the school approval decision, and keep the
  tape on **HOLD** until that review is resolved. Do not substitute spray
  adhesive, hot glue, or another chemical product.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Format at 100% scale in black-and-white, minimum
  14-point type, bold field labels, 0.5-inch writing lines, and uncrowded
  `YES / NO` choices; do not encode pass/fail by color alone. Read prompts
  aloud and allow a labeled drawing, pointing, oral response, dictation,
  scribing, ruler support, or a seated tester role without changing criteria.
- **Technology artifact:** None applies; this lesson has no technology artifact.
