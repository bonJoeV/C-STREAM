# Session 17: Year Exhibition

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly A; Portfolio; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** support a learning claim with dated work, demonstrate reasoning
individually and revise a caption after a question.
**Why:** an exhibition celebrates truthful growth, not automatic "Master Engineer."
Catholic/CST-C3 responsible gifts/work; CST-E3 evidence/limit explanation;
CST-A3 critique/revision. Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.
Technology **None**; developing. Prep 15; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class and exact supplies

Each pupil portfolio/two sheets/pencil; teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) share ruler.
Teacher board/timer; select paper records of design/tests/code, no live kits.
Missing portfolio uses fresh standing-paper test: fold, ten-second stand,
measure cm, change base/retest; label today's evidence not past growth.
Board `date/task | result | changed decision | limit`. Reuse tools/save records.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Portfolios; pencils (1/pupil each) | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Paper (2/pupil) | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| Rulers (1/team) | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Artifact = saved work; evidence = attributable result; growth = supported change;
caption = explanatory display. **Misconception:** watching a robot proves coding.
**If asked "Am I certified?"** No; practice/operation/mastery differ. Local mastery
requires two dated occasions, individual demonstration and changed-context
explanation; this reflection alone is insufficient.
No live hardware, private photos, food, balloons or award claims.
Stop/report missing/damaged materials; indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Optional thanks prayer; "Which artifact shows a decision changed?"
2. **4-9:** Model source/date/result/limit, paper versus actual operation.
3. **9-20:** Choose two dated records/caption; individually re-trace code or
   explain a fair test. Missing work: fresh tower task above.
4. **20-29:** Parallel team turns, two minutes/pupil; show evidence, respond
   to "What stayed the same?" Receive specific caption feedback.
5. **29-35:** Each revises caption and submits fresh explanation/limit plus
   stewardship choice; teacher marks gaps, not enthusiasm.
6. **35-40:** Date/file, count rulers, return portfolios, clear displays.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: evidence-backed explanation, attributable demonstration and reasoned
caption revision. Growth requires genuine records. Grade 3/support:
pointing/stems/scribing. Grade 4/challenge: tradeoff/transfer evidence needed.
Missing earlier work: report growth not evidenced, assess today's reasoning.
Long presentation: task-result-limit within two minutes. Early finish: answer
new peer question. No serial 25-pupil speeches or automatic device claims.

**Family:** We exhibited evidence of learning. Ask, "Which record supports your
claim?" Optional: listen to the explanation; no summer assignment.
## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

Provide one **Student material (exhibition evidence-and-caption sheet) per pupil**, or post and have
pupils copy these exact prompts: `My learning claim: ____`; `Artifact 1 task/date:
____`; `Visible result: ____`; `Decision I changed: ____`; `Artifact 2 task/date:
____`; `Visible result: ____`; `What these records do NOT show yet: ____`;
`Today I individually retraced/explained/tested: ____`; `Peer question: ____`;
`My answer: ____`; `Caption before feedback: ____`; `Caption after feedback:
____`; `Stewardship choice for tools/work: ____`. For a missing
portfolio, replace the two artifact rows with `Fresh paper stand height: __ cm /
stood 10 seconds: yes-no / base change: __ / retest: __ cm and yes-no`, and
label it `today's reasoning only - past growth not evidenced`.

- **Evidence and answer guidance:** Accept a modest claim such as "I improved
  at making fair tests" only when a dated record shows a result and changed
  decision. A second dated occasion may support growth, but the pupil must still
  individually retrace code, explain a controlled test or complete today's fresh
  standing-paper test. Accept honest limits: one record does not prove growth,
  paper code does not prove robot operation, a team product does not identify each
  member's contribution and today's fallback does not reconstruct missing past
  work. A caption revision must respond to a real peer question, for example
  changing "My tower worked" to "On March 12, my wider base stood 10 seconds;
  this tests one paper design, not every tower." Watching a partner, enthusiasm,
  an undated display or an unsupported "Master Engineer"/certification claim is
  not acceptable evidence.
- **Worked example:** Display two anonymous dated sheets from a lever
  investigation. Start with the claim "I got better at testing." Point to the
  result and changed control on each sheet, then add the limit "These records show
  two lever tests, not mastery of all machines." Answer the peer question "What
  stayed the same?" with the recorded five-counter load and revise the caption to
  name that control. The finished example includes two dated sources, an
  individual explanation, question/answer, before-and-after caption and limit.
  Also demonstrate the missing-portfolio pathway by folding one paper stand,
  timing 10 seconds, measuring height, widening only the base and retesting; past
  growth is **N/A - not evidenced**, while today's reasoning can be assessed.
- **Board setup and visual example:** Arrange parallel tabletop spaces for teams of no
  more than three, with portfolios lying flat and names/private material covered.
  Post one annotated caption before/after pair as the visual example pupils may consult.
  Post the timer where each pupil can see the two-minute turn. Divide the board
  into `SOURCE + DATE | RESULT | DECISION CHANGED | WHAT STAYED SAME | LIMIT |
  PEER QUESTION -> CAPTION REVISION`; beside it post `Practice, one operation and
  mastery are different claims`.
- **Discussion with acceptable response:** Ask, "Which exact record supports your claim?" Accept a
  dated, attributable artifact and its visible result. Ask, "What changed because
  of that result?" Accept a design, command, procedure or explanation tied to the
  record. Ask, "What stayed the same in your test?" Accept the pupil's recorded
  controls; if none exist, the pupil should say the comparison is limited. Ask,
  "Does watching a robot show that you coded it?" Accept no; individual operation
  or attributable evidence is required. Ask, "Does this exhibition certify you?"
  Accept no; local mastery requires two dated occasions, individual demonstration
  and changed-context explanation, and this reflection alone is insufficient.
  Correct the misconceptions that attractive display equals learning, team success
  proves individual performance and every positive change proves mastery.
- **Kit contents, kit label and reset:** At each team table place the assigned pupil
  portfolios, two plain sheets and one pencil per pupil, one metric ruler and a
  two-minute turn card. Keep the teacher timer/board at the front. Kit label on the table
  folder: `S17 EXHIBITION - DATED EVIDENCE - COVER NAMES / NO PHOTOS`. Cleanup and reset by
  dating and filing every evidence sheet, returning artifacts to the correct
  portfolio, counting/returning the ruler, clearing temporary displays and
  recording `portfolio present`, `portfolio missing/fresh test` or `continuation
  needed` on the roster. No artifact leaves with another pupil.
- **Unfinished work:** Check the last completed component: `claim / source-date /
  result / individual demonstration / peer question / answer / revised caption /
  limit`. Clip the partial sheet inside the pupil's portfolio and schedule a
  school-only continuation; record today's evidence without upgrading an
  unsupported growth claim. There is no home or summer assignment.
- **Annotated exemplar, common error reading and reteach:** Underline each source/date, box the
  visible result, draw an arrow from result to changed decision, circle the peer
  question phrase that caused the caption revision and bracket the explicit
  limit. An undated claim is a common error signalling missing provenance, not failure of speaking;
  ask for the original record. A description of a partner's work signals
  attribution error; reteach by switching to the pupil's own retrace or fresh paper test. A
  vague caption signals explanation needs; reteach with `On [date], [result] led me to
  change [decision]. This does not yet show [limit].` Mark past growth `not
  evidenced` rather than guessing.
- **Safety classification:** Severity **low** and likelihood **low** because no
  live kits are used. Adult ratio is **1:3 maximum**: one adult supervises each team of no more than three,
  controls portfolio handling and keeps aisles/displays clear. Exclude live
  hardware, chargers, food, balloons and unsupported award materials; stop and
  report a missing/damaged portfolio. Only pupil portfolios, plain paper, pencils
  and metric rulers are handled, so no commercial mixture is opened and no SDS
  entry is required today. If any marker, tape, cleaner or similar product is
  later added to this exhibition, enter the exact manufacturer/product in the
  [Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv)
  and keep that product on **HOLD** until SDS applicability and school approval
  are resolved.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Use 16-point sans-serif prompts, high
  black-and-white contrast, generous writing space and source/result/limit icons that do not rely
  on color alone. Read prompts and peer questions aloud; allow pointing to an artifact,
  oral explanation, dictation, scribing, seated presentation, private teacher
  presentation or additional school time. Keep the two-minute structure but do
  not penalize approved communication supports.
- **Technology artifact:** None applies; this lesson has no technology artifact.
