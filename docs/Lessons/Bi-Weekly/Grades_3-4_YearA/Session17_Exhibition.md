# Session 17: Year Exhibition

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Bi-Weekly A; Portfolio; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** support a learning claim with dated work, demonstrate reasoning
individually and revise a caption after a question.
**Why:** an exhibition celebrates truthful growth, not automatic "Master Engineer."
Catholic/CST-C3 responsible gifts/work; CST-E3 evidence/limit explanation;
CST-A3 critique/revision. Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.
Technology **None**; developing. Prep 15; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class and exact supplies

Each pupil portfolio/two sheets/pencil; teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) share ruler.
Teacher board/timer; select paper records of design/tests/code, no live kits.
Missing portfolio uses fresh standing-paper test: fold, ten-second stand,
measure cm, change base/retest; label today's evidence not past growth.
Board `date/task | result | changed decision | limit`. Reuse tools/save records.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Portfolios; pencils (1/pupil each) | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Paper (2/pupil) | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| Rulers (1/team) | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Artifact = saved work; evidence = attributable result; growth = supported change;
caption = explanatory display. **Misconception:** watching a robot proves coding.
**If asked "Am I certified?"** No; practice/operation/mastery differ. Local mastery
requires two dated occasions, individual demonstration and changed-context
explanation; this reflection alone is insufficient.
No live hardware, private photos, food, balloons or award claims.
Stop/report missing/damaged materials; indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Optional thanks prayer; "Which artifact shows a decision changed?"
2. **4-9:** Model source/date/result/limit, paper versus actual operation.
3. **9-20:** Choose two dated records/caption; individually re-trace code or
   explain a fair test. Missing work: fresh tower task above.
4. **20-29:** Parallel team turns, two minutes/pupil; show evidence, respond
   to "What stayed the same?" Receive specific caption feedback.
5. **29-35:** Each revises caption and submits fresh explanation/limit plus
   stewardship choice; teacher marks gaps, not enthusiasm.
6. **35-40:** Date/file, count rulers, return portfolios, clear displays.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: evidence-backed explanation, attributable demonstration and reasoned
caption revision. Growth requires genuine records. Grade 3/support:
pointing/stems/scribing. Grade 4/challenge: tradeoff/transfer evidence needed.
Missing earlier work: report growth not evidenced, assess today's reasoning.
Long presentation: task-result-limit within two minutes. Early finish: answer
new peer question. No serial 25-pupil speeches or automatic device claims.

**Family:** We exhibited evidence of learning. Ask, "Which record supports your
claim?" Optional: listen to the explanation; no summer assignment.
