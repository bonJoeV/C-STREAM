# 🌟 Kindergarten Bi-Weekly C-STREAM

**17 Sessions | 25 Minutes Each | Every Other Week**

This folder contains **17 documents and 17 teaching meetings (425 minutes)** for schools with bi-weekly C-STREAM. It is an alternative to the weekly track, not an equally deep replacement for its 32 meetings. Plant care between meetings is an adult responsibility, not an eighteenth STREAM session.

See the [Kindergarten review and complete audit](../../../Review/Kindergarten_Review.md).
All **17 documents** are complete instructional references after autonomous
closure. Read [K routines](../../../Resources/Kindergarten_Reference_Routines.md)
with the selected lesson; actual kit/school checks and classroom trials remain.

**Source gate:** unverified inherited quotations/biographies were omitted from
rewritten minimum paths. CST codes are local. The
[source-verified arts candidates](../../../Review/Kindergarten_External_Alignment.md)
need MDE-copy/school review; science/math identifiers and diocesan approval
remain unverified.

**Minimum pathway gate:** use the single reference sequence and named alternative.
Pictures/models do not establish live observation or device operation when
those are the target. Physical help requires consent. No homework, home device,
family donation or purchase is a prerequisite. All minimum pathways are indoors.

**Quantity convention:** for 10/15/20/25 pupils, pairs with a final trio make **5/7/10/12 active teams**. Budget **5/8/10/13 kits**, including one reserve for odd enrollment; all supply totals include that reserve.

## 🎯 Learning Goals

By the end of the year, Kindergarten students will:

- ✅ Approach the world with wonder and curiosity

- ✅ Observe with safe senses and ask questions; never taste nature materials

- ✅ Build simple structures and compare tests; act out and correct a two-command picture algorithm in Session 15

- ✅ Understand that faith and discovery go together

- ✅ Show kindness and care for others and creation

- ✅ Share what they've learned with others

## 🛠️ Materials Used Throughout Year

### Optional library additions; availability not assumed

- KEVA or ordinary wooden blocks; 100/160/200/260 for 10/15/20/25 students

- Pattern Blocks

- Simple Machines Kit

- Magnifying glasses

- Flashlights with secured batteries (up to 13 plus teacher); no prism or device required for primary shadow work

### Classroom Supplies

- Construction paper

- Craft sticks

- Playdough

- Crayons/markers

- Large adult-drawn nature pictures or inspected clean specimens; no collecting prerequisite

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Connection

Family activities are **optional invitations**, never homework or required materials. Copy the unit snippets in the review; do not imply that every lesson used hardware or that attendance proves grade-one readiness. Consider:

- Sending home a bi-weekly **Wonder Journal page**

- Creating a **classroom photo book** families can check out

- Hosting a **Family Wonder Night** in spring
