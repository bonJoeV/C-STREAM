## Vocabulary, background and SAFETY

Source = makes light; shadow = area receiving less light because an object
blocks it; predict = expectation; evidence = result observed.
Misconception: a prism always produces a visible rainbow. No prism, broken CD
or filter activity is required or scored. If asked why shadows change, point
to the light paths/model positions; compare actual size rather than promise
every setup yields a particular result. No beams at eyes, Sun viewing, dark
walking room, lasers, glass or student battery access.

## Exact sequence: 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Identify lamp control and ask what happens behind the cutout.
2. **4-8:** Model one position, then change only cutout distance. Look at screen.
3. **8-17:** Partners predict/test both marked positions, swap roles, and each
   draw/point to a result. Teacher begins individual checks.
4. **17-21:** Child shows changed/kept-same conditions, actual outcome and
   flashlight input/output; accepts an unchanged or unclear result honestly.
5. **21-25:** Date/save drawings, switch off/count lights and shapes, clear.

## Success, access, troubleshooting and family copy

Check comparison, control and communicated observation, not rainbow colors.
Ask "What moved? What stayed still? What did you see?"
Support: adult handles lamp under child's direction; large tactile cutout and
scribed report. Challenge: repeat the same positions.
No shadow: teacher adjusts lamp/screen setup before pupils test and records
the adjustment; no unsafe darkening. Missing safe lights: demonstration/model
only, student operation deferred. All primary work is indoors.
**Family:** We compared shadows with one changed condition.
Ask, "What moved in your test?" No homework or Sun observation.
