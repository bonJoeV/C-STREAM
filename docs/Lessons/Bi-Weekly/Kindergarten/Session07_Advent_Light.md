# Session 07: Advent Light

## Lesson at a glance

| Field | Plan |
|---|---|
| Grade / unit / time | K / Purposeful symbol art / one 25-minute meeting |
| Domains / big idea | C, A, M; a chosen symbol communicates meaning but is not a light source |
| Objective / why | "I can count star points and choose a design that communicates kindness." Visual choices can serve an audience. |
| Catholic connection | Advent, when applicable, invites preparation and kindness; the classroom star is a chosen symbol, not a scientific explanation. |
| Local standards | CST-C2: kind recipient/action; CST-A2: purposeful design; CST-M1: point count. Official alignment: VERIFICATION REQUIRED. |
| Technology / difficulty / prep / cleanup | None / beginner / 10 minutes first shapes / 4 included |

## Before class and exact materials

Read [K routines](../../../Resources/Kindergarten_Reference_Routines.md).
Use the actual school calendar; this lesson also works as kindness art outside
Advent. Precut five large five-point paper stars per child. No tiny decorations.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Student: sheet; crayon | 10 each | 15 each | 20 each | 25 each |
| Student: five >=5 cm paper stars | 50 | 75 | 100 | 125 |
| Kit: glue stick, pairs/final trio plus reserve | 5 | 8 | 10 | 13 |

Teacher timer/roster and a paper sample from the student budget.
No flashlight, candle, dark-room setup, photograph or device required.

## Vocabulary, background and SAFETY

Point = corner of this paper symbol; purpose = intended meaning; source =
makes light; symbol = represents an idea. Paper reflects available light;
real stars make light and do not physically have five paper points.
Misconception: shiny paper makes its own light. If asked about a Bible story,
use only an approved school reading; no unverified quote/history is necessary.
No glitter, sequins, flame, button-cell candle, Sun viewing or dark movement.

## Exact sequence: 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Ask how a kind action can include a person; original prayer optional.
2. **4-8:** Count five points on a paper star, distinguish symbol/source, and model
   choosing placement/size for a visible meaning.
3. **8-17:** Each child chooses star placement and explains a kind message;
   partner interprets it, then child revises a visual choice. Begin checks.
4. **17-21:** Child counts three to five chosen points accurately, explains
   placement and identifies a recipient/action without stereotyping.
5. **21-25:** Save dated evidence, count unused stars, cap glue and clear.

## Success, questions, access and troubleshooting

Check count, purposeful choice and respectful meaning; no spiritual-maturity score.
Ask "What should someone notice first? Does paper make light?"
Support: larger/tactile star, adult positioning directed by child.
Challenge: make two arrangements and explain preference.
No precut stars/glue: teacher draws a five-point symbol; pupils count/represent
with crayons, same objective. Indoors; no night visit or household supply.
**Family:** We counted a symbol's points and chose how it communicates kindness.
Ask, "Why did you put that shape there?" No routine homework.
