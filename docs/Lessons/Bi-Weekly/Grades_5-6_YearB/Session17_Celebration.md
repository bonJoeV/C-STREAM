# Session 17: Celebration Exhibition

## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Artifact:** saved work. **Evidence:** recorded support. **Caption:** brief
explanation. **Limit:** what wasn't checked.
**Common misconception:** "Code Master" certificate proves platform mastery.
**If asked, "What if I never took A?"** Explain B work only; no missing-year
penalty. Practice difference 3-2=1 additional correct request; not universal
user benefit. Frame purpose/decision/result/change/limit/resource choice.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Give thanks; ask "What can this artifact actually show?"
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Model practice caption, accessible visual hierarchy and actual/simulated labels.
3. **11-24 (13 min):** Each chooses artifact and makes display/caption with one future school-task question.
4. **24-35 (11 min):** Paired two-minute defenses and reader questions; trio fits. Revise caption from specific feedback; teacher samples while all submit records.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual result/limit explanation, old/new caption and material reuse tradeoff.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Return portfolios, cap/count tools, save evidence and clear tables.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"Did the device really run? What changed in caption? What remains uncertain?"
Each supplies supported result/limit, meaningful reader-driven revision and
resource explanation. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent;
4 compares evidence/tradeoff; NE for missing, not mastery from participation.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

No artifact -> labeled practice card. Missing raw result -> mark untested.
Anxiety -> seated teacher/scribed exchange. No private health/prayer displays,
identifiable photos, live electrical/robot demos, food, balloons or unsafe
dismantling. Stop/report unsafe crowding; public event needs separate approval.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: caption stems, large text, oral/drawn explanation.
Grade 6/challenge: defend next validation priority and evidence reliability.
Indoor seated exhibition unchanged in winter.

## CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER

Save individual result/revision and actual/practice route; record missing checks.
**Explored:** evidence communication. **Did:** curated/shared/revised.
**Learned:** limits and stewardship. **Catholic connection:** gifts with gratitude.
**Ask:** "Which result supports your choice?" Optional conversation; no routine homework.

## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

- **Adult ratio:** 1 adult:25 pupils maximum for the seated in-class exhibition, with exchanges limited to pairs or one trio.
- **Student material (print or no-print):** Give each pupil a display/caption frame: `artifact/route (actual, simulated, or practice); purpose; decision; result with number/observation; change; limit; reader question; old -> revised caption; material reuse choice`. Board-copy the frame and use the supplied 2/3->3/3 practice record if no personal attended artifact exists.
- **Exact evidence/answers:** Claims must match saved evidence. Practice answer: clearer PASS label improved fictional routing from 2/3 to 3/3, difference one additional correct request; it does not prove real-user benefit or platform/two-year mastery. A meaningful caption revision answers a reader's specific confusion.
- **Worked example / Finished example / Visual example:** Model “My invention helped everyone” -> “In the practice model, clearer PASS wording changed correct routes from 2/3 to 3/3; real-user benefit was not tested.” Finished non-build exhibition artifact is the accessible display, two-minute defense record, reader question, revised caption, result/limit, and material-reuse rationale; new construction is **N/A**. The old/broad claim beside the revised evidence-bounded caption is the visual example.
- **Board setup:** Post **ARTIFACT/ROUTE | ACTUAL RESULT | SUPPORTING RECORD | CHANGE | LIMIT | READER QUESTION | CAPTION REVISION | REUSE** and “participation ≠ mastery.”
- **Discussion/misconceptions:** Ask “Was the selected result actual, simulated, or practice?” (use the saved record); “What changed in the caption?” (specific answer to feedback); “What remains uncertain?” (named limit); “What if no Year A?” (defend Year B work only). Correct “Code Master”/certificate and universal-benefit claims.
- **Kit contents / Kit label:** Each pupil: one portfolio/practice record, two display/caption sheets, pencil; each tool team: ruler/marker. Kit label: **G5-6 YB S17 CELEBRATION EVIDENCE—ACTUAL/SIMULATED/PRACTICE LABELS**. Reset by returning portfolios, capping/counting tools, stacking reusable displays, recycling approved scraps, saving defense/revision evidence, and clearing tables.
- **Unfinished work:** Mark missing record, reader check, defense, or caption revision; store at school and offer seated teacher/scribed completion. Do not create a result, award mastery, or require a public/home exhibition.
- **Annotated exemplar / Common error interpretation / reteach:** Underline evidence-based result, box `2/3 -> 3/3`, circle revised caption, bracket limit/reuse reason. Unsupported broad claims signal claim-evidence mismatch; missing raw result is **untested**, not failure. Reteach with purpose/decision/result/change/limit stems and the practice card, then repeat a one-minute defense.
- **Safety risk classification—severity/likelihood/ratio/SDS:** Severity **low to moderate** (privacy, crowding, unsafe display), likelihood **unlikely** in the seated in-class format. One adult monitors the class and checks each pair/trio; stop crowding and prohibit food, balloons, unsafe dismantling, identifying images, or private health/prayer displays. **SDS/product gate: HOLD — school-approved marker.** The exact product and manufacturer are not specified, and no exact approved marker row exists; apply the [`CONTROL-ROW` in the Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Do not issue or substitute the marker until the school safety lead approves the exact product and current SDS.
- **Print/accessibility:** Use 14-point high-contrast captions, large-print copies, non-color actual/simulated/practice labels, seated/oral/scribed defenses, extra response space, and no public-speaking requirement.
