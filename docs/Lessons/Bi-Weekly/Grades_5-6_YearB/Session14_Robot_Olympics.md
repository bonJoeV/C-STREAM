# Session 14: Robot Olympics

| Field | Reference |
|---|---|

## BEFORE CLASS / MATERIALS

Check contact/accommodations; 4/5/7/9 teams rotate operator/tracer/checker.
Paper grid start(0,0), target(3,1); program RIGHT3/UP1, reset to(0,0).
Bug RIGHT3/UP2 ->(3,2); correct UP1, repeat. Without reset a second correct
run from(3,1) ->(6,2), invalid outside 5x5 grid.
Optional approved charged BOLT/tablet/Sphero Edu setup/team: clear 1 m lane,
50 cm observers, 30 cm target. Pretest finite aimed heading0, roll speed<=20,
duration<=1 second, Stop; adult resets physically only after stopped.
No assuming north, collision games, slalom or directional light-seeking.
Adult checks the actual removable floor-marking tape's adhesion/removal
compatibility on the school's surface; no automatic masking/painter substitute.

### Optional shared-kit turn plan

Three approved BOLT/tablet stations with three safe staffed lanes can serve
the 4/5/7/9 balanced teams: two trios/two pairs, five trios, six trios/one pair,
seven trios/two pairs. Preload the exact finite roll/Stop program above.
Teams 1-3 use **11-19**, 4-6 **19-27**, 7-9 **27-35**; 10/15 then retest.
Each pupil has up to two minutes to run from the marked start, measure after
Stop, personally edit a duration within <=1 second/speed<=20, reset safely
and rerun. Three pupil pairs produce the team's three original/revised
endpoints; a two-pupil team repeats one pair in its spare third turn so all
teams retain three trials/condition. Two minutes/team cover stopped resets
and teacher checking.
Waiting pupils trace the target/reset bug independently.
Adult pretests actual access, supervision/space, retrieval and turn timing.
Demo uses an issued station, not another purchased kit. Each child's actual
edit/run is recorded; watching is not operation. One/two safe staffed stations
serve that many teams/window, with later checks and paper-only/NE for others.
Do not form groups of four, rush access or claim paper is robot motion.

## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Reset:** restore starting state. **Heading:** aimed frame direction.
**Debug:** correct identified error. **Variability:** differing repeat results.
**Common misconception:** medals/fast runs prove program understanding.
**If asked, "Can we race?"** No, score only criterion/reasoning/cooperation.
Key paper four steps, endpoint(3,1); optional real error example 31 versus
30 cm ->1 cm, actual readings required; paper cells aren't robot distances.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "What makes a cooperative event fair?" Introduce no-speed rule.
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Model target/reset and optional Stop/boundaries.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each traces buggy/correct program; optional teams assemble/run one short route, each has supervised edit/run. Shared hardware teams begin their stated waves.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Fix turn/reset and retest on paper; optional teams record three actual endpoints, revise duration within cap, retest from same marked start. Finish shared waves and retain every pupil's own baseline/retest pair.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual trace/debug/result comparison/limit and safe-role explanation, device turn recorded separately.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Stop before adult retrieval, save evidence, return devices/tokens and tidy.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"Did you reset? Where is heading zero? Which evidence supports the change?"
Each has correct trace/reset correction, baseline/retest and dignity/safety
reason. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent; 4 repeat variability;
NE for unobserved actual operation, no medals as mastery proxy.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

Device/access/lane missing -> paper event, hardware evidence deferred.
Drift -> Stop/re-aim with adult, no speed increase. No collisions, shaking,
moving retrieval, loose batteries, spectators in lane, public accounts or bright
lights in eyes. Adult isolates/reports damaged/hot equipment.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: route cards/large grid, oral/scribed trace.
Grade 6/challenge: compare routes/endpoint variation and explain reset confound.
Indoor only; no outdoor race or winter exposure.

## CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER

**Learned:** reset/accuracy. **Catholic connection:** respectful effort.

## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

- **Adult ratio:** 1 adult:25 pupils for paper tracing and exactly 1 adult:3 pupils at each active robot lane; a lane does not open without its dedicated adult.
- **Student material (print or no-print):** Give each pupil a 5 x 5 grid and trace rows for start, RIGHT3, UP1/UP2, endpoint, reset, second run, fault, fix, and retest; optional device table records actual baseline/revised endpoints. Board-copy the grid/rules if printing fails.
- **Exact evidence/answers:** Correct paper endpoint is `(3,1)`; bug RIGHT3/UP2 ends `(3,2)`; correct UP1. Without reset, a second correct run from `(3,1)` ends `(6,2)`, outside grid. Device evidence uses actual cm and requires Stop, same marked start, pupil edit/run; paper cells are not robot distances.
- **Worked example / Finished example / Visual example:** Trace `(0,0)->(3,0)->(3,2)`, compare target, replace UP2 with UP1, reset, and reach `(3,1)`. Finished example contains buggy/correct traces, reset explanation, baseline/retest, safety role, and honest paper/device label; optional real result might be 31 cm versus 30 cm target, but only if measured. The paired buggy/correct routes on the 5 x 5 grid are the visual example.
- **Board setup:** Post grid/target and **START | PROGRAM | PREDICTED END | ACTUAL END | FAULT | RESET/FIX | RETEST | VARIABILITY**; write “accuracy/cooperation, no speed ranking.”
- **Discussion/misconceptions:** Ask “Did you reset?” (must return start); “Where is heading zero?” (pre-aimed frame, not assumed north); “Which evidence supports change?” (endpoint pair); “Do medals/fast runs prove understanding?” (no). Correct drift by re-aiming, never increasing speed.
- **Kit contents / Kit label:** Each pupil: trace/pencil; each team: grid/rule sheet, token, ruler. Optional team/shared station: approved BOLT/tablet and selected removable floor tape route. Kit label: **G5-6 YB S14 TARGET (3,1)—RESET—PAPER/DEVICE SEPARATE**. Reset token/start, sort sheets, stop robot before adult retrieval, remove approved tape, inspect/store device for charging.
- **Unfinished work:** Mark last trace/trial and route; store at school and schedule missing individual device turn. Watching or paper work never becomes robot-operation evidence.
- **Annotated exemplar / Common error interpretation / reteach:** Box endpoints, circle UP2->UP1, underline reset and same-start comparison. `(6,2)` identifies missing reset; `(3,2)` identifies turn-count error. Reteach with physical token and explicit return-to-origin card, then retry bug/fix from `(0,0)`.
- **Safety risk classification—severity/likelihood/ratio/SDS:** Severity **moderate** (moving robot collision, trip, hot/damaged battery), likelihood **unlikely** with speed<=20, duration<=1 s, Stop, 1 m lane, and 50 cm observers. One adult directly supervises each active robot lane (maximum one team of three per lane); paper teams remain classroom-monitored. Adult retrieves/resets after Stop and isolates damage/heat. **SDS/product gate: HOLD — removable floor-marking tape.** The exact product and manufacturer are not specified. See the [`FLOORTAPE` row in the Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Do not issue or substitute floor-marking tape until the school safety lead approves the exact product and current SDS.
- **Print/accessibility check / Devices/external-service compliance:** Use 14-point high-contrast grids, arrows plus words, tactile token, oral/scribed trace, seated paper roles, and equitable turns. The actual hardware/service is an approved tablet running **Sphero Edu paired to BOLT**. Missing approval, lane, tape, tablet, BOLT, or pairing defers the observed run, reset, endpoint, and BOLT-operation evidence; paper tracing is reasoning evidence only and never runtime/device evidence. Use no personal account or upload.
