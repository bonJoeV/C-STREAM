# Session 3: Architecture Engineering

| Field | Reference |

| Time | 1 meeting of 45 minutes |
| Objective / why / big idea | I can design and revise a purposeful room layout with measured paths/doorways and an access decision. Architecture combines form and human function. |

## BEFORE CLASS / MATERIALS

Check contact/accommodations; seat 4/5/7/9 teams. Brief: "A community reading/
art room needs two activity zones, visible entrance/exit and clear movement
path." Base 20x20 cm, teaching scale 1 cm = 1 m; not actual building code.
Criterion: 2 cm-wide paper token moves entrance -> both zones -> exit without
obstacle; openings/path at least 3 cm. Teacher pre-cuts wall strips and pilots
layout; model walls below 10 cm, no roof loads.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Grid paper plan; paper evidence; pencil | 1 each/student | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Cardboard base; cardstock furniture/token sheet | 1 each/team | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Cardboard wall strips | 4/team | 16 | 20 | 28 | 36 |
| Ruler; marker; brief sheet | 1 each/team | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Masking tape | 0.5 m/team | 2 m | 2.5 m | 3.5 m | 4.5 m |
| Board; clock; roster | 1 each/teacher | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Paper/cardboard/tape consumable/reused; tools reusable.

## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Plan:** top view. **Elevation:** side/front view. **Scale:** model relationship.
**Function:** intended use. **Common misconception:** beautiful layout is accessible.
**If asked, "Does 3 cm mean legal doorway?"** No; teaching criterion only, real
architecture needs qualified code review. 20 cm represents 20 m at this scale;
token is a clearance test shape, not a person/disability portrayal.
Light/color/pattern choices direct attention; art isn't decoration alone.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "Who could use this room easily?" Introduce welcoming purpose.
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Model plan/elevation/scale and token criterion.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each sketches plan with dimensions and form choice; team builds selected low floor model with two zones/entrance/exit.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Rotate token operator/observer/checker; measure path/openings, record obstructions, revise furniture placement and retest.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual measured doorway/path, criterion, purposeful layout revision and access/model-limit reason.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Remove/store walls, return tools, save plans and tidy.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"Where does the path narrow? Why that visual focal point? What isn't certified?"
Each has dimensions with cm, stated criterion, tested visual/form revision and
dignity decision. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent; 4 tradeoff;
NE if missing, not real-building approval.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

Walls fall -> lower height/tape base, not loads. Missing cardboard -> paper
floor plan retains layout/path objective, physical construction deferred.
No body-weight tests, climbing, heavy roofs, real access claims, sharp cutting
or impairment simulation. Stop/report unstable kit.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: grid/zone starters, large token, oral/scribed explanations.
Grade 6/challenge: quantify activity area lost to wider path and defend tradeoff.
Indoor tabletop plan; no building tour prerequisite.

## CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER

Save measured plans and test notes, count strips/tools.
**Explored:** architecture. **Did:** modeled/tested a community room.
**Learned:** form/function/access. **Catholic connection:** welcoming dignity.
**Ask:** "What moved after your path test?" No routine homework.

## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

- **Adult ratio:** 1 adult:25 pupils maximum for the seated class; the adult checks each team of at most three and performs every cardboard cut.
Use this pack for the 20 x 20 cm community reading/art room model; its 3 cm rule is a classroom criterion, not building code.

- **Student material (print or no-print):** Provide a 20 x 20 grid plan labeled entrance, exit, reading zone, art zone, focal point, path/opening widths, obstruction found, and revision. Without printing, pupils rule the square in journals from the board brief and use a 2 cm paper token.
- **Exact evidence/answers:** Finished evidence shows scale `1 cm = 1 m`, openings/path at least 3 cm, and the 2 cm token traveling entrance -> both zones -> exit without obstruction after a documented furniture move. Accept different layouts; do not accept an attractive unmeasured plan or claim of legal accessibility.
- **Worked example / Finished example:** Model a chair narrowing a path to 2 cm; record fail, move it 2 cm, measure 4 cm, and retest the full route. A finished example includes dimensions, two zones, low walls, before/after token route, purposeful focal point, and “model does not certify a real room.”
- **Board setup:** Draw the scaled base and route arrows; post **FORM | FUNCTION | 3 cm CRITERION | TEST OBSTRUCTION | REVISION | RETEST | MODEL LIMIT**.
- **Discussion/misconceptions:** Ask “Where did the route narrow?” (name measured location); “Why that focal point?” (supports intended use); “Does beauty prove access?” (no, test/measurement does); “Is 3 cm legal?” (no). Correct any use of the token as a disability simulation: it is only a clearance-test shape.
- **Kit contents / Kit label:** Per team: 1 cardboard base, cardstock furniture/token sheet, 4 adult-precut wall strips, ruler, marker, brief, and 0.5 m masking tape; each pupil has plan/evidence/pencil. Kit label: **G5-6 YB S03 COMMUNITY ROOM—20x20 BASE/4 WALLS/2 cm TOKEN**. Reset by removing tape, flattening/storing walls and furniture, returning ruler/marker, and saving measured plans.
- **Unfinished work:** Mark the last measured segment and obstruction; bag the model with plan and resume the token route at school. If cardboard is unavailable, finish the paper plan and label physical build/retest deferred.
- **Annotated exemplar / Common error interpretation / reteach:** Box every width, underline the criterion, circle the moved object, and arrow the successful retest. A blocked token indicates layout clearance, not a user deficit; missing units indicate measurement evidence is incomplete. Reteach plan view and one 3 cm opening with grid/large token, then rerun entrance-to-first-zone.
- **Safety risk classification—severity/likelihood/ratio/SDS:** Severity **low** (paper/cardboard edge or unstable model), likelihood **unlikely** with adult-precut strips and walls below 10 cm. One adult monitors the class and checks every team of at most three; adult performs cutting and stops body-weight, climbing, roof-load, or sharp-tool tests. **SDS/product gate: HOLD — masking tape and school-approved marker.** Neither exact product nor manufacturer is specified. See the [`TAPE` and `CONTROL-ROW` entries in the Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv); no exact approved marker row exists. Do not issue or substitute either product until the school safety lead approves that exact product and its current SDS.
- **Print/accessibility:** Print the grid at 100%, 14-point high-contrast labels, dashed/patterned as well as colored zones, and wide writing spaces; permit tactile furniture, seated token operation, oral/scribed explanation, and enlarged plans.
