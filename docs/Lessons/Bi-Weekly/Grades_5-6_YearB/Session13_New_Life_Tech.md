## BEFORE CLASS / MATERIALS

Review accommodations/contact; offer silent/tactile pulse alternative, no loud
instruments. Tool teams 4/5/7/9. Religion teacher confirms season.
Original paraphrase: "At Easter Catholics celebrate Christ's Resurrection.
We can share hope through respectful service." Reference John 20:1-18,
not a direct quote. Prepare two-panel/score template; optional approved slides,
original drawings only, save school-only, no recordings of pupils.

## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Motif:** short repeated artistic idea. **Pulse:** steady beat.
**Caption:** message. **Audience:** intended recipients.
**Common misconception:** fast/happy music scientifically proves hope.
**If asked, "Does everyone hear the same feeling?"** No; artistic interpretation
varies. Describe intent, ask feedback, avoid emotional/therapeutic promises.
Quarter note=1 beat, two eighths=1, quarter rest=1.
Starter bar quarter/two eighths/quarter/rest totals 4; contrasting bar
two eighths/quarter/two eighths/quarter totals 4. Two bars=8 beats.
Pupils choose/revise motif and original visual spacing to convey their message.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "How could a silent reader receive this message?" State faith/paraphrase.
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Model original two panels, four-beat bars and quiet tapping/silent pointing.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each makes two panels and selects two four-beat motifs with intended contrast/repetition; optional teams create actual slides.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Partner reads caption and follows eight-beat sequence; records interpretation/confusion. Revise motif spacing or visual message and repeat.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual artistic intent/revision, source/original-media status and silent-access choice; distinguish actual paper/digital production.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Save school-only if digital, stop sounds, cap/count tools, save evidence.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"How many beats? What did the reader interpret? Which media are original?"
Each has coherent message, two valid four-beat bars, reader-backed revision
and safe/access reasoning. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent;
4 defends artistic tradeoff; NE if missing. No grading piety or singing talent.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

Rhythm confusing -> slower silent beat pointing. No device -> paper objective
complete, digital production deferred. Content unclear -> supplied paraphrase,
no fabricated quotation. No public media, private reflection, identifiable
recording, copied music, flashing effects or loud sensory demands.
Stop/report unsafe data/sound conditions.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: beat boxes, large captions and scribing.
Grade 6/challenge: defend rhythmic contrast versus visual access and clarify
symbolic meaning without claiming measured emotion. Indoor seated art/music.

## CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER

Record route, save original/revised motifs/panels, return devices/tools.
**Explored:** artistic Easter communication. **Did:** revised visual/rhythm work.
**Learned:** meaning/access/source care. **Catholic connection:** Resurrection
and hopeful service. **Ask:** "What did your motif intend?" No routine homework.
