## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Motif:** short repeated artistic idea. **Pulse:** steady beat.
**Caption:** message. **Audience:** intended recipients.
**Common misconception:** fast/happy music scientifically proves hope.
**If asked, "Does everyone hear the same feeling?"** No; artistic interpretation
varies. Describe intent, ask feedback, avoid emotional/therapeutic promises.
Quarter note=1 beat, two eighths=1, quarter rest=1.
Starter bar quarter/two eighths/quarter/rest totals 4; contrasting bar
two eighths/quarter/two eighths/quarter totals 4. Two bars=8 beats.
Pupils choose/revise motif and original visual spacing to convey their message.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "How could a silent reader receive this message?" State faith/paraphrase.
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Model original two panels, four-beat bars and quiet tapping/silent pointing.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each makes two panels and selects two four-beat motifs with intended contrast/repetition; optional teams create actual slides.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Partner reads caption and follows eight-beat sequence; records interpretation/confusion. Revise motif spacing or visual message and repeat.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual artistic intent/revision, source/original-media status and silent-access choice; distinguish actual paper/digital production.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Save school-only if digital, stop sounds, cap/count tools, save evidence.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"How many beats? What did the reader interpret? Which media are original?"
Each has coherent message, two valid four-beat bars, reader-backed revision
and safe/access reasoning. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent;
4 defends artistic tradeoff; NE if missing. No grading piety or singing talent.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

Rhythm confusing -> slower silent beat pointing. No device -> paper objective
complete, digital production deferred. Content unclear -> supplied paraphrase,
no fabricated quotation. No public media, private reflection, identifiable
recording, copied music, flashing effects or loud sensory demands.
Stop/report unsafe data/sound conditions.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: beat boxes, large captions and scribing.
Grade 6/challenge: defend rhythmic contrast versus visual access and clarify
symbolic meaning without claiming measured emotion. Indoor seated art/music.

## CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER

Record route, save original/revised motifs/panels, return devices/tools.
**Explored:** artistic Easter communication. **Did:** revised visual/rhythm work.
**Learned:** meaning/access/source care. **Catholic connection:** Resurrection
and hopeful service. **Ask:** "What did your motif intend?" No routine homework.

## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

- **Adult ratio:** 1 adult:25 pupils for silent/paper production; use 1 adult:3 pupils at an optional active slide setup and prohibit recording.
- **Student material (print or no-print):** One two-panel/score sheet per pupil with the supplied Easter paraphrase, two four-beat bars, intended visual/rhythm effect, partner interpretation, confusion, revision, original-media status, and silent-access choice. Board-copy beat boxes when no printer is available.
- **Exact evidence/answers:** Quarter=1, two eighths=1, rest=1. Starter `quarter + two eighths + quarter + rest = 4`; contrast `two eighths + quarter + two eighths + quarter = 4`; total 8 beats. Interpretation may vary; evidence is purposeful choice and reader-backed revision, not measured hope.
- **Worked example / Finished example / Visual example:** Count starter bar `1+1+1+1=4`, silently point each beat, then widen cramped caption after reader confusion. Finished paper example has two original panels, both valid bars, intent, partner response, before/after revision, John 20 reference/paraphrase label, and silent route. Digital example requires actual approved slide creation; otherwise digital is N/A. The two-panel layout with four large beat boxes under each panel is the visual example.
- **Board setup:** Post beat values and **MESSAGE | BAR 1 (4) | BAR 2 (4) | INTENT | AUDIENCE HEARD/SAW | REVISION | SOURCE/ACCESS**.
- **Discussion/misconceptions:** Ask “How many beats?” (4 each/8 total); “What did reader interpret?” (report, do not force); “Which media are original?” (pupil text/drawings); “Does fast music prove hope?” (no); “Does everyone feel the same?” (no).
- **Kit contents / Kit label:** Each pupil: two panel/score/evidence sheets and pencil; each team: ruler, marker, content/beat sheet; optional approved slide computer. Kit label: **G5-6 YB S13 EASTER MESSAGE—2 PANELS/2x4 BEATS—SILENT OPTION**. Reset beat sheets/tools, stop sound, close school-only slides, return devices, and retain original/revised work.
- **Unfinished work:** Mark incomplete panel/bar/reader test and actual paper/digital route; store at school. Do not require singing, recording, home software, or private reflection.
- **Annotated exemplar / Common error interpretation / reteach:** Box beat totals, underline paraphrase/source status, circle revision and silent access. A 5-beat bar is counting/notation evidence; unclear Resurrection message needs caption revision, not invented quotation. Reteach with four beat boxes and silent counters, then retest one bar/caption.
- **Safety risk classification—severity/likelihood/ratio/SDS:** Severity **low** (sensory/privacy/tool issue), likelihood **unlikely** with quiet/silent options and no pupil recordings. One adult monitors class and checks teams of at most three; stop loud sound, flashing effects, or identifiable capture. **SDS/product gate: HOLD — school-approved marker.** The exact product and manufacturer are not specified, and no exact approved marker row exists; apply the [`CONTROL-ROW` in the Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Do not issue or substitute the marker until the school safety lead approves the exact product and current SDS.
- **Print/accessibility check / Devices/external-service compliance:** Use 14-point high-contrast captions, large beat boxes, symbols plus words, silent/tactile pulse, scribing, and seated work. The optional hardware/service is a school computer with the **approved slide editor** using a local school-managed save. When unavailable, actual slide creation and editor-operation evidence are deferred; the paper composition meets the content objective but never counts as editor runtime/device evidence. Use original drawings only, with no public upload, copied music/image, or pupil recording.
