# Session 1: Engineering Thinking

## LESSON AT A GLANCE

| Field | Reference |
|---|---|
| Grade / schedule / rotation / unit | 5-6 / Bi-Weekly / B / Foundations |
| Time | 1 meeting of 45 minutes; design practice, not mastery certification |
| Objective / why / big idea | I can identify a stated user need, set a criterion/constraint and revise a tested organizer. Useful design begins with people and limits. |
| Domains / Catholic connection | C, E; dignity in shared tools, no private-frustration interview or stereotype. |
| Local standards | CST-C2: stated need; CST-E1: criterion/constraint; CST-E2: test/retest. Official benchmarks VERIFICATION REQUIRED. |
| Technology Requirement | None; paper/cardboard organizer primary. |

## BEFORE CLASS / MATERIALS

Check contact/accommodations; seat 4/5/7/9 teams. Brief: "A classroom user
needs three shared pencils laid flat, easy to retrieve and return, without
sharp points exposed in a standing cup." Criterion: three flat pencils
stay in a 20x15 cm base when one is removed/replaced three times.
Constraint one cardboard base/two paper strips/0.5 m tape. Teacher pilot
folded paper dividers; adult pre-cuts bases. Rotate designer/builder/tester.

| Item | Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Paper evidence; pupil pencil; journal | 1 each/student | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Cardboard base; ruler; brief sheet | 1 each/team | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Paper divider strips | 2/team | 8 | 10 | 14 | 18 |
| Blunt demonstration pencils | 3/team | 12 | 15 | 21 | 27 |
| Masking tape | 0.5 m/team | 2 m | 2.5 m | 3.5 m | 4.5 m |
| Board; clock; roster | 1 each/teacher | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Demo pencils are additional to pupil pencils; paper/tape consumable, tools reusable.

## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Empathy:** listen to stated needs. **Criterion:** success check.
**Constraint:** limit. **Iteration:** evidence-based change.
**Common misconception:** 20 quick ideas proves better thinking.
**If asked, "Is my idea original?"** Explain choices; no patent novelty claim.
Three justified ideas suffice. Key: 3 pencils x 9 teams = 27 demo pencils at 25;
three retrieval cycles test one function, not lifetime durability.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "Which need is stated?" Connect care of shared resources.
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Model five stages, base constraint and three-cycle test.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each sketches three ideas and criterion; team builds one with rotating roles.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Each tests one retrieval/replacement, record escapes; revise divider spacing, repeat three cycles.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual criterion/constraint, original/retest count and stated-versus-assumed/access explanation.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Count pencils, store prototypes/logs, return rulers and tidy.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"Did you measure the base? What changed? Could everyone take a turn?"
Each has criterion/constraint, two three-cycle records and evidence-backed
revision/dignity reason. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent;
4 measured space tradeoff; NE for untested work.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

Pencils roll -> raise folded divider, not tape pencils permanently.
No cardboard -> cardstock base, record constraint change. No demo pencils ->
three paper pencil models, physical-tool retrieval deferred. No standing sharp
points, private interviews, medical/electrical projects or public media.
Stop/report unsafe handling.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: pre-folded dividers, criterion stems, scribing.
Grade 6/challenge: compare two divider widths and quantify desk-space tradeoff.
Indoor desk plan independent of Year A/devices.

## CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER

Count pupil/demo pencils separately, save each test and report shortages.
**Explored:** user-centered engineering. **Did:** tested/revised flat organizer.
**Learned:** criteria/limits. **Catholic connection:** dignity/shared care.
**Ask:** "What was actually requested?" No routine homework.

## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

- **Adult ratio:** 1 adult:25 pupils maximum for the seated class; the adult checks each team of at most three during every retrieval test and alone handles cutting.
Use this pack with the stated organizer brief; it does not alter the 45-minute sequence.

- **Student material (print or no-print):** Give each pupil a half-sheet with: “Stated need: __. Criterion: three flat pencils stay inside a 20 x 15 cm base during three remove/replace cycles. Constraint: one base, two strips, 0.5 m tape. Trial 1 escapes: __; change: __; retest escapes: __.” Without printing, copy this grid to the board and use journals; accept oral/scribed results.
- **Exact evidence/answers:** Independent evidence names the user’s stated retrieval need, both a criterion and constraint, two sets of three-cycle results, and a revision tied to escapes. A defensible result is “0 escapes after narrowing divider spacing”; a pretty organizer without six recorded cycles is incomplete.
- **Worked example:** Sketch three flat pencils on the 20 x 15 cm base, fold two paper strips into low dividers, then test one pencil three times. If trial marks are `1,0,1` escapes, say “2/3 failed; move the loose divider inward,” change only that spacing, and repeat.
- **Finished example:** A completed organizer holds all three pencils flat; its record reads `before 2 escapes/3; divider moved 1 cm inward; after 0/3`, with the limit “this tests retrieval, not lifetime durability.” Display the measured sketch beside the prototype.
- **Board/chart setup:** Post **NEED | CRITERION | CONSTRAINT | BEFORE 1-2-3 | CHANGE | RETEST 1-2-3**; draw a labeled 20 x 15 cm rectangle and list `base 1, strips 2, tape 0.5 m`.
- **Discussion/acceptable answers and misconceptions:** Ask “What was requested?” (flat storage and easy return, not decoration); “What proves improvement?” (fewer escapes under the same three-cycle test); “Could everyone take a turn?” (adapt reach/role without changing criterion). Correct “many ideas guarantee quality” and “attractive means successful” by requiring measured test evidence.
- **Exact kit / Kit label:** Per team: 1 adult-precut cardboard base, 2 paper divider strips, 3 blunt demonstration pencils, 1 ruler, 1 brief, and 0.5 m masking tape; each pupil has evidence paper/pencil. Kit label: **G5-6 YB S01 ORGANIZER—3 PENCILS/2 STRIPS/1 BASE**. Reset by removing loose tape, flattening reusable bases/strips, separating pupil pencils from demo pencils, and counting all three demo pencils.
- **Unfinished work:** Date and bag the prototype with the pupil’s last completed cycle and next step (“retest cycles 2-3”); store at school and resume there—no home completion or invented pass.
- **Annotated exemplar/error/reteach:** Underline the stated need, box `2/3 -> 0/3`, circle “moved divider 1 cm,” and label absent retest **not yet evidenced**. Escapes suggest spacing/height or inconsistent replacement, not low effort. Reteach by modeling one slow remove/replace with a pre-folded divider, then supervise one unchanged three-cycle retry and record it separately.
- **Safety risk classification—severity/likelihood/ratio/SDS:** Severity **low** (minor poke/cut or trip), likelihood **unlikely** with blunt pencils, adult-precut bases, and seated work. One adult actively monitors the class, visually checking each team of at most three during tests; adult does all cutting and stops exposed sharp points. **SDS/product gate: HOLD — masking tape.** The exact product and manufacturer are not specified. See the [`TAPE` row in the Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Do not issue or substitute masking tape until the school safety lead approves the exact product and current SDS.
- **Print/accessibility:** Print black-and-white at 100%, minimum 14-point text, high contrast, non-color trial marks, and generous writing boxes; read the brief aloud and allow enlarged sheets, tactile dividers, seated roles, pointing, dictation, or scribing.
