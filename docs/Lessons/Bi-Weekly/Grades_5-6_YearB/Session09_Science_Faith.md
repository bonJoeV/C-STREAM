## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Observation:** inspected information. **Inference:** interpretation.
**Provenance:** origin. **Faith teaching:** belief communicated by Church.
**Common misconception:** science only asks HOW, faith only WHY.
Questions overlap; methods/claims differ. **If asked, "Was there never conflict?"**
This packet cannot establish universal history; study episodes with sources.

**A:** "This line is 10 cm" -> measure/inspect; actual value may differ.
**B:** Adapted from the original
[Mendel heritage card](../../../Resources/Catholic_Scientists_Heritage.md#gregor-johann-mendel-observation-and-inquiry).
[Masaryk University's Mendel Museum](https://mendelmuseum.muni.cz/en/about-the-museum/mendel-museum)
identifies Mendel's Augustinian membership/abbacy, pea experiments and preserved
work/objects. Paraphrase supports role/work, not private motives, every
inheritance pattern or a universal claim about Church/science history.
The child's own line measurement in A remains separate inquiry evidence;
the biography does not replace it.
**C:** [Fides et Ratio, opening](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_jp-ii_enc_14091998_fides-et-ratio.html)
affirms faith/reason toward truth; Church teaching, not measured pea result.
**D:** "Every Catholic institution always accepted every scientific theory"
-> unsupported absolute, omit. No quotation required.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "Would the same evidence support all four claims?"
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Read packet; model source type/provenance/date/limit.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each measures A, classifies A-D with reasons and makes three-panel explanation observation/history/faith.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Peer challenges one claim; revise caption/source specificity. Keep unresolved question rather than invent an answer.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual supported claim/source/date/limit, rejected absolute and original/revised explanation.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Save records/posters, cap/count tools, reuse paper and tidy.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"What does C establish? What would check B? Why not repeat D?"
Each supplies correct claim/source match, limit, provenance and meaningful
revision. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent; 4 distinguishes inference/
source dependence; NE for missing, no required predetermined historical verdict.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

Line differs -> record actual, correct card. Source offline -> supplied packet;
no unsupervised browsing. Missing ruler -> teacher-verified mark, measurement
practice deferred. No personal belief survey, private records, public media
or copied quotes/images. Stop/report unsafe data use.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: read aloud, claim-type icons and scribing.
Grade 6/challenge: explain why one scientist's example cannot establish all
history, suggest needed source. Indoor packet plan.

## CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER

Keep individual source/limit and revision, count tools, log open questions.
**Explored:** faith/reason and claims. **Did:** inspected/cited/revised.
**Learned:** appropriate evidence. **Catholic connection:** truth with humility.
**Ask:** "Which source fits your claim?" No routine homework.

## Teacher artifact pack (release checklist)

- **Adult ratio:** 1 adult:25 pupils maximum for the seated source-packet class, with teams capped at three.
- **Student material (print or no-print):** Provide Cards A-D and a sheet `claim | type (observation/history/faith/unsupported) | source/provenance | limit | revised caption`, plus a 10 cm line to inspect. If no printer, board-copy cards and have pupils draw/measure the line in journals.
- **Exact evidence/answers:** A is inspected measurement and may differ; B's museum source supports Mendel's Augustinian role/pea work but not motives or all history; C is Church teaching about faith/reason, not measured science; D is an unsupported absolute and must be omitted. A biography does not replace measurement.
- **Worked example / Finished example / Visual example:** Work B: cite Mendel Museum, state supported role/work, add limit “one case does not prove all Church/science history.” Finished non-build example is a three-panel observation/history/faith explanation with provenance/date, rejected D, peer challenge, and revised caption. The three visibly separated observation/history/faith panels are the visual example.
- **Board setup:** Post **CLAIM | METHOD/SOURCE | WHAT IT SUPPORTS | WHAT IT CANNOT SUPPORT | REVISION** and separate FACT/PARAPHRASE/REFLECTION labels.
- **Discussion/misconceptions:** Ask “What does C establish?” (teaching); “What checks B?” (reliable historical sources); “Why reject D?” (absolute without evidence); “Does science only ask how and faith only why?” (questions overlap; methods/claims differ). Do not require a predetermined historical verdict.
- **Kit contents / Kit label:** Each pupil: two claim/poster sheets and pencil; each team: exact packet/line sheet, ruler, marker. Kit label: **G5-6 YB S09 CLAIM TYPES—A-D—SOURCES CHECKED 2026-10-04**. Reset packets A-D in order, verify 10 cm marks/rulers, cap markers, and log unresolved questions.
- **Unfinished work:** Mark the last classified card and unresolved source limit; store at school and resume from the supplied packet without inventing research or a conclusion.
- **Annotated exemplar / Common error interpretation / reteach:** Underline claim, box provenance/date, circle limit/revision. Treating C as experimental evidence is category confusion; treating B as universal history is overgeneralization; a line not exactly 10 cm should be recorded, not forced. Reteach by matching A/B/C to ruler/museum/Church document cards.
- **Safety risk classification—severity/likelihood/ratio/SDS:** Severity **low** (misrepresentation, privacy, minor tool use), likelihood **unlikely** with supplied sources and no belief survey. One adult monitors class and checks each team of at most three; stop personal-faith polling. **SDS/product gate: HOLD — school-approved marker.** The exact product and manufacturer are not specified, and no exact approved marker row exists; apply the [`CONTROL-ROW` in the Safety Data Sheet Register](../../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Do not issue or substitute the marker until the school safety lead approves the exact product and current SDS.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Use 14-point high-contrast cards, readable source names, read-aloud, icons plus words, oral/scribed classification, and an enlarged line sheet.
