## BEFORE CLASS

Release the hardware route only after completing the
[OLP Snap Circuits guide](../../Resources/Snap_Circuits_Classroom_Guide.md).
Use the actual model's documented battery/slide-switch/lamp project from the
eight individual **Snap Circuits STEM Classroom Activity Kits (OHM-135)**.
Do not assume a push button, protected holder, LED/resistor specification or
battery count. The filename is retained for existing links, not LittleBits wiring.

User brief: "A classroom model needs an ON/OFF light and a label readable from
1 m by someone who cannot rely on color alone." It is **not a medical,
emergency or real safety device**, and it will not be distributed to patients.

## VOCABULARY, BACKGROUND AND SAFETY

**Circuit:** full conducting return path. **Input/output:** switch action/light.
**Load:** component transferring electrical energy. **Open:** interrupted path.
**Criterion/constraint:** success requirement/limit. **Tradeoff:** a choice with
benefits and costs. Battery chemical energy becomes electrical energy and then
light/thermal energy; current is not "used up" at the lamp.

**Evidence change:** Snap's slide switch has **two states tested twice**, not
the old independently controlled supply/button four-state test. Teach ON/OFF,
not push/release. LED polarity/current-limiting evidence is not claimed from a
lamp project. The separate protected-AA option retains its four-state key.

**Misconception:** a lit lamp proves any wiring is safe.
**If asked:** "Can I join the battery terminals?" No; that is a dangerous short.
Trace a safe open path on paper instead.

**SAFETY:** follow the guide's manufacturer/age/holder gates; adult isolates
the source before snap changes and inspects before restoring power. No pupil
cell access, loose/button cells, mains/USB substitutions, shorts, bypassed
protection, extra branches, fan blades, soldering or take-home circuits.
Warm/damaged/smelly/leaking -> stop/report, adult isolates, OUT OF SERVICE.
Keep labels beside the grid, not over parts or contacts.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

### Meeting 1: exactly 45 minutes - path and troubleshooting

1. **0-4: Purpose.** Read the user brief; discuss why a clear signal can support dignity and access.
2. **4-9: Model.** Trace the approved return path, demonstrate OFF/ON, source isolation and adult inspection. Explain the two-state evidence change.
3. **9-12: Predict.** Each child labels source/switch/light/conductors/return, predicts OFF/ON and identifies a drawn gap.
4. **12-26: Wave A.** Guide routine: isolated team build, adult check, each child's two OFF/ON cycles, one teacher-managed open-path repair and checked retest. Waiting teams label diagrams and draft an accessible ON/OFF label.
5. **26-27: Reset.** Adult isolates power and transfers/resets station kits.
6. **27-41: Wave B / individual evidence.** Remaining teams get the same hardware routine. Finished teams record predicted/actual results and explain one repair and safe resource choice; smaller classes use the window for repeat tests and individual checks.
7. **41-45: Cleanup.** Isolate/count/store parts and cells, save data and note unfinished physical evidence.

### Meeting 2: exactly 45 minutes - accessible indicator

1. **0-4: Retrieve.** Revisit each child's state/path record without assuming prior mastery.
2. **4-9: Define criteria.** Two correct OFF/ON cycles; a peer reads/interprets the label from **1 m**, without color-only cues. Constraints: supplied grid, unchanged approved circuit, paper label beside it.
3. **9-12: Plan.** Each child predicts an access obstacle and sketches a justified label/layout change.
4. **12-26: Wave A.** Build/check/test; peer reads label at 1 m without coaching. Record pass/not yet, revise one paper feature and repeat the same test. No electrical redesign outside the documented project.
5. **26-27: Reset.** Adult isolates power and transfers station kits.
6. **27-41: Wave B / explain.** Remaining teams run the same routine. Finished teams document before/after results; each child explains path, observed change, user benefit and limitation/tradeoff. Do not claim a real alert device is safe.
7. **41-45: Cleanup.** Isolate/count/store; collect individual diagrams/state/revision records. Paper only goes home.

## SUCCESS, SUPPORT AND TROUBLE

Each child contributes to the isolated build, operates the switch, labels the
full path, predicts two states, records two cycles and explains test-backed
repair/redesign evidence. Log **observed / prompted / not yet** for physical
contribution/testing separately from diagram reasoning; a functioning team kit
does not certify every child's operation.

Score **1** participation only; **2** prompted path/test; **3** independent path,
state and before/after explanation; **4** additionally defends an access tradeoff
and the model's limitations.

- **Grade 5/support:** traced diagram, large word/symbol labels, oral records,
  motor assistance; still collect each child's prediction and switch turn.
- **Grade 6/challenge:** justify label size/layout/readability tradeoffs using
  feedback. Compare two drawn faults; no undocumented loads or V=IR requirement.
- **25 pupils:** nine teams, five stations, two 14-minute hardware waves.
  Inspection/access overruns defer evidence, never compress safety checks.
- **No light:** isolate/report; adult compares approved diagram/contacts/cells,
  then supplies a checked reserve. Never bypass protection or improvise parts.

## ALTERNATIVES / INDOOR FALLBACK

The guide retains the original separate protected-AA push-switch/LED-resistor
route and its four-state evidence key; no Snap/LittleBits components are attached
to that supply. Use the same waves, replacing slide-switch tests with the
specified supply/button states. Record which hardware and state table were used.
Without safe hardware, retain the timed paper diagram/state/design work;
record **reasoning/design only; electrical operation not observed** and schedule
supervised completion. All work is dry and indoors.

## TEACHER ARTIFACTS

Use this operational set for the approved Snap slide-switch/lamp path, two ON/OFF cycles and accessible 1 m label.

### Student material and evidence

- **Printable/no-print material:** Diagram/state/design sheet: label source/switch/lamp/conductors/return; predict and record two **OFF=open/lamp off; ON=closed/lamp on** cycles; teacher-managed gap repair/retest; 1 m label trial, revision/retest, access rationale and limitation.
- **Expected evidence, worked example, and finished example:** Individual switch turn and full return path are logged separately from paper. Work an open gap: isolate source, adult repairs against approved diagram, inspect, restore and retest. The finished model stays on supplied grid with unchanged documented circuit and word+symbol paper label beside it; two correct cycles/readability pass do not certify a medical/emergency safety system. Paper-only result states electrical operation not observed.

### Teacher display and discussion

- **Board setup/discussion:** Draw the approved path loop, OFF/ON table, **isolate→adult inspect→power→observe**, and 1 m read test. A lit lamp proves path function, not arbitrary wiring safety; current is not used up; color-only signal excludes. Misconceptions: shorting terminals is a test, push/release is the Snap slide-switch evidence, lamp proves real alert suitability.

### Kit, closure, and continuation

- **Kit/reset/unfinished:** Exact listed per-pupil/team/station contents and actual OHM-135 setup card—no assumed part count. **Kit label:** **SNAP INDICATOR—W26-27—APPROVED SLIDE SWITCH/LAMP / ADULT CELLS / 2×OFF-ON**. Adult isolates, counts against manufacturer diagram, trays parts/cells in dry storage and marks OUT OF SERVICE damage. Record last cycle/read test; schedule supervised hardware completion, not paper inference.

### Interpretation and reteach

- **Annotated exemplar, common error interpretation, and reteach:** Trace source→switch→lamp→return, box two predicted/actual cycles, circle adult-repaired gap and enlarged word+symbol label after failed 1 m read. Lamp off may be valid open state, not pupil failure; working team kit without individual turn is access gap; color-only pass is false accessibility. Reteach on paper with loop/gap, then one adult-checked OFF/ON cycle and one silent read.

## SAFETY / support / challenge

- **Safety classification:** Battery short/leak/heat has **serious severity / unlikely likelihood** under guide controls. **Adult supervision ratio:** **1:1 energized station**, maximum five stations, all source changes/cells adult-only. Warm/damaged/smelly/leaking = isolate and report. Battery manufacturer safety data **applies and must be available**. Print ≥16 pt circuit lines and word+symbol states; tactile tracing, oral record and motor assistance. Do not substitute mains or USB power, and do not send the kit home.
- **SDS/product gate: HOLD — school-approved classroom marker.** The exact marker product and manufacturer are not specified. The [Safety Data Sheet Register CONTROL-ROW](../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv) does not approve a marker product; do not issue this marker or substitute another marker until the school safety lead approves the exact product and its current SDS.
