## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Category:** group defined for counting. **Frequency:** count.
**Axis:** graph reference line. **Provenance:** where data came from.
**Common misconception:** graph counts generosity/grace.
**If asked, "May I share my thanks?"** Privately/voluntarily, but not this dataset.
Key: 6+5+4+3+2 = 20; nature minus quiet = 4; nature 6/20 = 30%,
then 25/20/15/10%, sum 100%. No pie chart required; five bars enable comparison.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "What can these fictional counts tell us?" Brief thanksgiving reflection without disclosure.
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Model axes/one-unit scale, total and honest "fictional" title.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each makes all five bars, labels axes/count units and total; partners check heights without doing work for them.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Compare greatest/least/difference; diagnose deliberately missing category in teacher graph and fix. Grade 6 uses percentage scaffold.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual graph, total 20, difference 4, private-data exclusion and respectful opt-out explanation.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Cap/count tools, save graphs, reuse paper backs and tidy.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"Where did data come from? Are categories overlapping? Can a bar show grace?"
Each has five correct bars/labels/total, numerical comparison and privacy/
choice reason. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent; 4 explains another
representation limit; NE if missing, not handwriting/art grade.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

Total wrong -> sum original counts; retain corrected work. Graph scale changed
midway -> redraw consistent zero-based scale. No graph paper -> ruled axes.
No personal survey, private names/prayers, health promise, public uploading
or spiritual ranking. Stop/report accidental disclosure.

## TEACHER ARTIFACTS

### Student prompt, evidence, and examples

- **Student material (printable/no-print prompt):** Give each student the invented table `nature 6 | helpful actions 5 | learning 4 | creative play 3 | quiet time 2` with “Source: fictional practice data; one response/category.” Below it print or board-copy: “Draw five bars from a zero baseline; one square = one response. Label title, axes, scale, categories, and total. Write greatest, least, difference, provenance, and one thing the graph cannot measure.”
- **Expected evidence / answer key:** Bars must be 6, 5, 4, 3, 2; total 20; greatest nature, least quiet time, difference 4. Grade 6 percentages are 30%, 25%, 20%, 15%, 10% and total 100%. Accept “cannot measure grace, gratitude, faith, or human worth”; do not accept a personal survey as evidence.
- **Visual example / worked example:** Model nature only: title “Fictional category responses,” vertical axis 0-6, horizontal label “nature,” bar to 6, then check `6 of 20 = 30%`. Deliberately show a bar beginning at 2 and correct it to zero while explaining why the first version exaggerates.
- **Finished example:** A complete five-bar graph with source label, total 20, `6 - 2 = 4`, and caption “Nature occurred four more times than quiet time in this invented dataset; this does not rank anyone’s gratitude.” This is a non-build data product; no physical prototype is applicable.

### Board/chart and discussion

- **Board setup:** Left: the five-row dataset and `6+5+4+3+2=20`. Center: blank axes marked categories and frequency (responses), ticks 0-6, one-unit scale. Right: `Provenance | Numerical claim | Limit`, the privacy boundary “NO names/prayers/personal gratitude survey,” and exit prompts “difference?” and “cannot tell?”
- **Discussion key:** “Where did data come from?”—an invented practice card, not classmates. “Are categories overlapping?”—not in this defined dataset because each fictional response has one category; real collection would require rules. “Can a bar show grace?”—no. Misconceptions to correct: bar height is not category value or holiness; a missing bar is not zero unless recorded; changing scale mid-graph invalidates comparison; 6/20 is 30%, not 6%.

## Exact supplies and class-size allocation / kit, reset, and unfinished work

- **Kit contents / Kit label:** Per student: one graph-paper sheet, one exit paper, and one pencil; per team: one ruler, one marker, and one `6/5/4/3/2` dataset sheet; teacher: board, clock, and roster. Label `CATEGORY DATA — WEEK 11 — FICTIONAL 6/5/4/3/2 — NO PERSONAL SURVEY — 1 ruler/1 marker — team ___`.
- **Reset:** Cap and count markers; return rulers and dataset sheets by team; file each graph/exit together; erase any volunteered personal information immediately and report a disclosure. Restock graph/exit paper to the stated enrollment column.
- **Unfinished work:** Circle the next missing item (bar, label, total, comparison, or limit), write “unfinished—no inference,” and file flat. Resume from the original five counts; never fill missing bars or invent an interpretation for the student.

### Annotated exemplar and reteach

- **Annotated exemplar / common error interpretation:** Underline the source/title, box all five bar tops and `20`, bracket `nature 6 - quiet 2 = 4`, and star the privacy/limit sentence. A bar at 5 for nature means a transcription/plotting error; total 19 means arithmetic or omitted-data error; a correct graph captioned “nature is most important” means interpretation overreach.
- **Reteach:** Return to the dataset, place six tally marks beside nature, map each tally to one square, and repeat for quiet time. Have the student recompute the total and orally complete “The graph shows ___ counts; it cannot show ___.” Preserve the original and attach the corrected graph.

## SAFETY

- **Risk/adult ratio:** Physical severity **low** and likelihood **unlikely** for seated paper/ruler work; privacy harm severity **moderate** and likelihood **possible** if students disclose or rank personal faith. One adult may supervise the whole class at the school-approved adult ratio; maintain teams of at most three and direct sightline, with no separate hazardous station.
- **SDS/product gate: HOLD — school-approved marker.** The exact marker product/manufacturer is not specified, and the [Safety Data Sheet Register CONTROL-ROW](../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv) does not approve a marker product. Do not issue or substitute any marker until the school safety lead approves the exact product and its current SDS.
- **Print accessibility:** Print the dataset/axes at 100%, minimum 12-point text, high-contrast patterns as well as labels, ample bar spacing, and no color-only key. Offer large print, pre-labeled axes, oral/scribed response, tactile/high-contrast ruler support, and ruled-paper board copy without changing the five-count reasoning.
