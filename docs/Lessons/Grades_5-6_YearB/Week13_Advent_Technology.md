# Week 13: Advent Technology

## LESSON AT A GLANCE

| Field | Reference |
|---|---|
| Grade / schedule / rotation / unit | 5-6 / Weekly / B / Communication |
| Time | 1 meeting of 45 minutes |
| Objective / why / big idea | I can design a readable four-panel Advent resource and justify its time/privacy choices. Technology should serve purpose, not demand attention/data. |
| Domains / Catholic connection | T, A, C; Advent prepares for Christ; responsible time/resources and voluntary reflection. Four activity panels are not official weekly theme assignments. |

## BEFORE CLASS / MATERIALS

Review contact/accommodations. Religion teacher confirms local seasonal timing;
core content below needs no quotation, song or stock image. Seat tool teams
4/5/7/9. Copy four panels: Pause (quiet reflection may stay private), Notice
(observe a useful classroom tool), Share (offer a turn), Care (reuse paper).
Each is an optional classroom invitation, not fasting requirement or faith score.
Pilot fold into four equal panels. Optional IT-pretested school slides: four
slides, title/text/original drawing only, save school-only; no personal accounts.

| Item | Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Paper panel sheet; evidence sheet; pencil | 1 each/student | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Ruler; marker set; content sheet | 1 each/team | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Computer with approved slide editor, optional | 1/team | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Board; clock; roster | 1 each/teacher | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Paper consumable; tools reusable; software is access, not material.

## VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND

**Intentional use:** use for a chosen purpose. **Hierarchy:** visual priority.
**Privacy:** control of personal information. **Stewardship:** responsible care.
**Common misconception:** more notifications mean more spiritual growth.
**If asked, "Are these official weekly Advent themes?"** No; devotional resource
choices, not universal liturgical assignments. Four two-minute invitations =
8 minutes, not a collected personal screen-time survey.

## EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE

1. **0-4 (4 min):** Ask "Could a tool support reflection without collecting it?"
2. **4-11 (7 min):** Model four panels/title/body contrast and eight-minute example.
3. **11-25 (14 min):** Each makes four-panel layout, original image and one brief invitation/panel; approved digital teams actually create/test slides if chosen.
4. **25-35 (10 min):** Peer finds Pause/Share without coaching, reads one instruction; revise visual order/text, retest and record.
5. **35-41 (6 min):** Individual layout purpose, privacy exclusion and time/resource tradeoff; name actual paper/digital route.
6. **41-45 (4 min):** Save school-only if digital, cap/count tools, save panels and tidy.

## QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

"What does the user need first? Why no personal journal input?"
Each supplies four coherent panels, reader-backed visual revision and privacy/
time rationale. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent; 4 compares tool
tradeoff; NE for missing; paper never called digital production.

## IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY

No devices -> paper route unchanged objective. Too much text -> one action
per panel. Unapproved content -> use supplied invitations, no fabricated quote.
No private prayers, personal usage tracking, compulsory fast, public posts,
unlicensed music/images or flashing effects. Stop/report unsafe data use.

## SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK

Grade 5/support: predivided panels, word bank, dictation/large text.
Grade 6/challenge: defend paper versus digital accessibility and time cost.
Entire indoor resource lesson; no home device/activity required.

## CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER

Retain tests and actual production route, return devices/tools.
**Explored:** intentional tools. **Did:** revised an Advent invitation resource.
**Learned:** layout/privacy matter. **Catholic connection:** preparation and
stewardship. **Ask:** "Why did your resource avoid collecting prayers?"
Optional conversation; no routine homework.
