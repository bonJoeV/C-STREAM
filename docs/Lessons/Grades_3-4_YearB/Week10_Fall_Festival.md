# Week 10: Fall Festival Engineering

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly B; Applied design; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** move three paper crop tokens 30 cm, revise a hauler and compare
two repeated tests. **Why:** harvest transport combines useful design and
careful use of resources. Catholic/CST-C3: gratitude/responsible stock use;
CST-E1 criteria/limits; CST-E2 real retest; CST-M1 distance/token counts.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**;
introductory. Prep 10; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class / supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9); kit two cardstock sheets, three large paper crop squares,
ruler, **40 cm tape**. Each pupil paper/pencil. Teacher board/timer, fold sample
card tray from issued kit; mark 30 cm desk route. Manual pushing/pulling only;
no wheels/extra supplies needed. Reuse rulers; paper/tape consumable.

## SAFETY

**Background, vocabulary, and troubleshooting:**

Load = carried objects; friction = resistance to sliding; constraint = limit;
criterion = tested requirement. Feeling drag is qualitative, not measured
force. **Misconception:** hauler must have a motor. **If asked "Is this farming?"**
It models one transport problem, not an actual harvest or saving measurement.
No produce/acorns, drops, elastic launchers or flights retained. Desk tokens,
no projectiles; stop/report unsafe material. Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "How can transport avoid losing a harvest?" Optional gratitude prayer.
2. **4-9:** Model 30 cm route, three-token criterion, two sheets/40 cm tape limit.
3. **9-21:** Each sketches; build folded sliding tray, rotate roles.
4. **21-30:** Push along route twice, count arriving tokens; revise side height/
   shape, repeat twice at same distance/surface/load.
5. **30-35:** Each child records four counts, criterion/limit, changed feature and
   reuse choice. "Did a taller side cause another difficulty?"
6. **35-40:** Count rulers/tokens, flatten scraps for reuse, save dated evidence.

## What success looks like

Meets: distance checked, four real counts and justified retest/stock choice.
Grade 3/support: starter fold/dictation. Grade 4/challenge: load-retention versus
sliding tradeoff, no force ratio. Tokens fall: note result/widen tray, not add
unbudgeted glue. No card: two ordinary paper sheets, name changed constraint.
Early finish: new tester, same route. No serial festival-station rotation.

**Family:** We tested harvest-hauler models. Ask, "What stayed the same when
you retested?" Optional: discuss how farmers' work serves neighbors.

## Teacher artifact pack

- **Student hauler record (print/no-print):** One page per pupil with `Name/team/date`, a 30 cm route line, `Criterion: ___ of 3 crop tokens arrive`, `Limits: 2 cardstock sheets / 40 cm tape`, `Version 1 sketch`, `Test 1 tokens arrived`, `Test 2 tokens arrived`, `Problem observed`, `One feature changed`, `Retest 1`, `Retest 2`, and `Resource reused/saved`. No-print: copy the same fields and draw the route on plain paper.
- **Expected evidence and acceptable answers:** Four counts must be 0-3 and come from the same 30 cm surface, load, and push/pull method. Accept revisions such as taller sidewalls, a wider tray, a folded front stop, or reduced rubbing area if only one planned feature changes. A design meets the criterion when all three tokens arrive; partial counts remain valid. “Add a motor” is outside the constraints.
- **Worked finished example:** `V1 low-sided tray: 2, 1 tokens; observation: squares slid over left side. Change: fold left wall from 1 cm to 3 cm. V2: 3, 3 tokens. Kept same: three squares, 30 cm desk route, same operator/push. Reuse choice: second cardstock offcut became the taller wall.` Annotate the before/after counts and note that improved retention does not measure pushing force.
- **Board/visual setup:** Tape a 30 cm route to a demo desk and draw a start/finish plan. Post a four-cell count chart `V1 T1 | V1 T2 | V2 T1 | V2 T2`, a `criterion / constraints` box, and a cross-section of a folded tray with side height labeled.
- **Discussion prompts:** “What evidence identifies the failure point?” (the side and moment where tokens left), “Why keep the load at three?” (fair comparison), “Could a taller wall make another problem?” (more rubbing/material or harder loading), and “Does the model need wheels?” (no). Correct the misconception that a motorized or elaborate design is automatically better.
- **Kit, label, reset:** Per team: `2 cardstock sheets, 3 large paper crop squares, 1 metric ruler, 40 cm masking tape`; per pupil: `1 evidence sheet, 1 pencil`. Label the tray/envelope `W10 | assigned team ID | 3 CROPS + HAULER`; mark crop squares `assigned team ID-1/2/3`. Return all three tokens and ruler, place reusable cardstock offcuts behind the record, discard only unusable tape/paper under school rules, and verify 4/5/7/9 complete kits.
- **Unfinished work:** Store the hauler without crushing it in `W10 IN PROGRESS` with a slip naming one next action: `finish V1`, `run two baseline tests`, `make one revision`, or `run two retests`. Resume on the same desk route; no home materials or unrecorded tests count.
- **Annotated errors and reteach:** Mark a 20 cm run `distance criterion not tested`; replacing two materials at once `cause unclear`; recording “worked” without 0-3 `count missing`; dropped tokens returned during a trial `reset and repeat`. Reteach by sliding the teacher tray once, pausing where a token escapes, then folding one wall and completing one identical retest.
- **Safety classification:** Severity **minor** (paper edge, ruler damage, or floor litter); likelihood **unlikely** with all tokens and models kept on desks. Adult ratio is **1 adult to at most 9 teams/25 pupils**, with teams no larger than 3 and no floor racing or projectile pushes. The masking tape is a commercial tape article; record the exact manufacturer/product in [Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv](../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Keep that product **ON HOLD** until school approval and SDS applicability are resolved.
- **Accessible printing:** Use 14-point text, high-contrast boxes, a 30 cm route diagram not dependent on color, and large 0-3 count circles. Allow dictation, sticker/pointing responses, precreased cardstock, or a partner mover with the pupil serving as counter/designer. Check a 100% grayscale copy for clear test/retest separation and adequate writing room.
- **Student material:** Student material: give each pupil the hauler record with the 30 cm route, three-token criterion, V1 and V2 sketches, four 0-3 test counts, observed problem, sole changed feature, and resource reused or saved.
- **Board setup:** Board setup: tape the 30 cm route to the demo desk and display the V1/V2 four-count chart, criterion/constraints box, and labeled tray-wall cross-section.
- **Kit label:** Kit label: mark each tray or envelope `W10`, the school-assigned team identifier, and `3 CROPS + HAULER`; mark the three crop squares with that identifier and numbers 1, 2, and 3.
- **Annotated exemplar:** Annotated exemplar: on the low-sided tray record, mark V1 counts `2, 1`, the left-side escape observation, the sole change from a 1 cm to 3 cm wall, and V2 counts `3, 3`.
- **Common error:** Common error: a 20 cm route, a word such as `worked` without a 0-3 count, or returned tokens during a trial does not meet the hauler test; annotate the exact invalid condition and repeat at 30 cm.
