# Week 11: Thanksgiving Gratitude

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly B; Gratitude data; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** tally one voluntary/practice choice, graph three category counts
and explain a whole-number comparison without ranking gratitude.
**Why:** displays communicate what data actually measure.
Catholic/CST-C2: privacy/no judgment of feelings; CST-M2: tally/bar chart;
CST-M3: total/difference; CST-A3: critique/revision of chart message.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**;
introductory. Prep 10; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class / supplies

Per pupil: ballot scrap, graph sheet, pencil. Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) share ruler/
two markers. Teacher board/timer; three category headings: people/places/
experiences. Ask "Which fictional thank-you card topic would you choose?"
One answer, no names; abstention allowed. If no actual survey consent, use
clearly labeled **practice counts 4/3/5**. Board tallies, no five serial lists
or medical claims. Retain charts, reuse tools.

## SAFETY

**Background, vocabulary, and troubleshooting:**

Category = grouping; tally = count marks; scale = value per unit; anonymous =
not named. **Misconception:** larger bar means more grateful/healthier.
**If asked "Who chose people?"** Anonymous tally does not tell us who; counts
are not faith/happiness measurements. Catholic thanks is a practice, not scored
personal emotion. Paper work indoors; no food, private blessings or public
names. Report damaged tools; ballots handled by adult.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Optional prayer of thanks. Explain choice/privacy/abstention.
2. **4-10:** Model one ballot/one tally, zero baseline, one square = one response.
3. **10-20:** Collect all anonymous ballots simultaneously; teacher/volunteer
   count each category, class checks total equals valid responses.
4. **20-29:** Each draws three bars/title/labels/scale; peer checks counts and
   misleading caption, pupil revises.
5. **29-35:** Each child reads two bars, gives total/difference and a limitation.
   Practice key: total 12, experiences exceed places by 2.
6. **35-40:** Collect dated charts, adult disposes ballots, count/cap tools.

## What success looks like

Meets: accurate counts/labels, correct comparison and privacy/interpretation
limit with visible revision. Grade 3/support: 0-25 pre-drawn axis/scribing.
Grade 4/challenge: explain abstention/sample limitations, not percentages.
Total mismatch: recount ballots, mark invalid double answers; do not silently
adjust numbers. No participation: use labeled practice dataset. Early finish:
ask which question these counts cannot answer.

**Family:** We graphed anonymous choices, not gratitude levels. Ask, "What does
a bar actually count?" Optional: share a thanks if comfortable.

## Teacher artifact pack

- **Student materials (print/no-print):** Ballot prompt: `Which fictional thank-you card topic would you choose? Mark one: [ ] people [ ] places [ ] experiences [ ] abstain.` No name field. Graph page fields: `Name/date`, `Data source: class anonymous choices OR practice 4/3/5`, title, three labeled bars, `1 square = 1 response`, `valid-response total`, `___ exceeds ___ by ___`, `This graph does NOT measure ___`, and `Caption before/after peer check`. Without printing, adult cuts blank ballot scraps and pupils copy the graph fields onto paper.
- **Answer key/evidence:** For practice counts 4/3/5, bars are people 4, places 3, experiences 5; total 12; experiences exceed places by 2, experiences exceed people by 1, and people exceed places by 1. Accept any correct whole-number comparison. The limitation should say the chart counts fictional card-topic choices, not gratitude, happiness, holiness, or who selected each category.
- **Finished exemplar:** Draw a zero-based three-bar chart titled `Practice thank-you card choices (not class responses)`, with heights 4/3/5 and the source note. Revise the misleading caption `Our class is most grateful for experiences` to `In the practice data, experiences received 5 choices, two more than places.` Annotate `practice`, the numerical comparison, and the removal of the unsupported emotion claim.
- **Board/chart setup:** Prepare anonymous tally columns `people | places | experiences | invalid/blank`, a valid-total equation, and a 0-5 model axis. Keep a privacy box reading `one choice; no names; abstention allowed; counts do not identify people`. Cover practice counts until needed.
- **Discussion guidance:** Ask “Can the graph tell who chose places?” (no), “Does the tallest bar mean more gratitude?” (no), “Why start bars at zero?” (to represent count size honestly), and “What if tallies do not match valid ballots?” (recount and label invalid/blank; never alter counts). Correct claims about a person or emotional state from category totals.
- **Kit, labels, reset:** Per pupil: `1 ballot scrap, 1 graph sheet, 1 pencil`; per team: `1 metric ruler, 2 markers`. Label ballot container `W11 ANONYMOUS TOPIC CHOICES | ADULT HANDLING`; label tool pouch `W11 assigned team ID | 1 RULER + 2 MARKERS`. Adult destroys ballots after verified tally according to school practice; pupils return/cap markers and rulers, and completed charts go in the class evidence folder.
- **Unfinished work:** File incomplete graphs in `W11 CHARTS TO FINISH` with the verified counts attached and one status (`bars`, `labels/scale`, `comparison`, or `caption revision`). Finish from the recorded aggregate only; do not retain or recreate individual ballots and do not send survey work for family completion.
- **Error interpretation and reteach:** Mark a bar starting above zero `visual scale exaggerates`; a total of 11 `re-add 4+3+5`; a named chooser `privacy breach—remove name and notify teacher`; “most thankful” `construct not measured`. Reteach with 12 counters grouped 4/3/5, transfer each stack to a bar, then orally complete “The bar counts ___, not ___.”
- **Safety/privacy severity:** Physical severity **minor** and likelihood **rare** for ordinary paper/markers; privacy harm severity **moderate** and likelihood **unlikely** when ballots are unnamed and adult-handled. Adult ratio is **1 adult to no more than 25 pupils**, with adult custody of ballots and teams of at most 3 for graphing. The markers are a commercial marker/ink product; record the exact manufacturer/product in [Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv](../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Keep that product **ON HOLD** until school approval and SDS applicability are resolved.
- **Print accessibility:** Use 14-point minimum, wide checkboxes, thick axes, patterns and category words rather than color-only bars, and a large-print 0-25 axis option. Read the choice privately, allow pointing or an adult mark without recording identity, and accept oral comparisons/scribing. Test a monochrome copy to confirm patterns, zero line, and source label remain clear.
- **Worked example:** Worked example: graph the practice counts `people 4`, `places 3`, and `experiences 5`, total 12, then calculate that experiences exceed places by 2 without interpreting gratitude or identity.
- **Finished example:** Finished example: a zero-based graph titled `Practice thank-you card choices (not class responses)` shows bars 4/3/5, the simulated source, and the caption `experiences received 5 choices, two more than places`.
- **Visual example:** Visual example: display anonymous tally columns for `people`, `places`, `experiences`, and `invalid/blank`, a valid-total equation, a 0-5 axis, and the privacy reminder that counts do not identify people.
- **Kit label:** Kit label: mark the ballot container `W11 ANONYMOUS TOPIC CHOICES | ADULT HANDLING`; mark each tool pouch `W11`, the school-assigned team identifier, and `1 RULER + 2 MARKERS`.
- **Annotated exemplar:** Annotated exemplar: mark the finished graph’s zero baseline, 4/3/5 bar heights, practice-data source, numerical comparison, and removal of the unsupported `most grateful` claim.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Print/accessibility check: print the black-and-white ballot and graph at 100% with at least 14-point text, wide checkboxes, thick axes, patterned bars, and large tally and caption spaces.
