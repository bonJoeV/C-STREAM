# Week 23: Catholic Schools Week

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly B; Community data; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** make a three-category class survey chart, read two bars and
revise a caption to respect privacy/sample limits. **Why:** appreciation and
truthful data communication can coexist.
Catholic/CST-C2 no coerced disclosure; CST-T3 data/privacy; CST-M2 chart;
CST-M3 counts/difference; CST-A3 caption critique. Official alignment
**VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; introductory.
Prep 10; cleanup 5. No dated national theme/history statistics required.

## Before class / supplies

Question on board: "Which classroom appreciation activity would you choose:
thank-you drawing, helpful cleanup, quiet prayer?" One anonymous choice,
abstention permitted; not a holiness survey. Per pupil: ballot scrap, graph
sheet/pencil. Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) share ruler/two markers. Teacher board/timer.
If survey unsuitable, **simulated practice counts 6/4/3**, labeled not class
data. No family finances/years of attendance questions. Save charts, reuse tools.

## SAFETY

**Background, vocabulary, and troubleshooting:**

Survey = question with recorded responses; anonymous = unnamed; sample =
responding group; caption = explanatory label. **Misconception:** choices
measure faith. **If asked "Can we say all Catholic children prefer drawing?"**
No; this group/simulation cannot represent all pupils.
Paper indoors; adult keeps ballots private/destroys after tally, no public names/
media, no obligatory prayer disclosure. Report broken supplies.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Optional gratitude prayer; explain Catholic appreciation/privacy.
2. **4-10:** Model one response/one tally; valid total excludes blank/double marks.
3. **10-20:** Simultaneously collect ballots, tally/check counts on board.
4. **20-29:** Each graphs three bars 0-based scale, title/unit/source label;
   peer critiques misleading caption and pupil revises.
5. **29-35:** Each child reads two bars, total/difference and privacy/sample limit.
   Practice key: total 13; drawing exceeds cleanup by 2.
6. **35-40:** Collect dated charts, adult handles ballots, cap/count/clear.

## What success looks like

Meets: accurate chart/comparison, responsible caption/source and access choice.
Grade 3/support: pre-drawn 0-25 axis/oral evidence. Grade 4/challenge:
nonresponse/sample bias in words, no required percentages/pie chart.
Mismatch: recount, label invalid ballots; never adjust silently. No survey:
labeled practice data. Early finish: explain an unanswered question.

**Family:** We used school-appreciation data responsibly. Ask, "Whose responses
does your graph represent?" Optional: thank a school helper.

## Teacher artifact pack

- **Student survey/chart materials (print/no-print):** Anonymous ballot: `Which classroom appreciation activity would you choose? [ ] thank-you drawing [ ] helpful cleanup [ ] quiet prayer [ ] abstain`; no name. Graph sheet: `Name/date`, `Source: anonymous class choices OR simulated practice 6/4/3`, zero-based three-bar chart, `valid total`, `two-bar comparison`, `Who the sample represents`, `Caption draft`, `Privacy/sample check`, and `Revised caption`. Without printing, use blank ballot scraps and copy these exact graph prompts to paper.
- **Expected evidence/answers:** Practice key: drawing 6, cleanup 4, quiet prayer 3; total 13; drawing exceeds cleanup by 2 and prayer by 3; cleanup exceeds prayer by 1. Accept any correct comparison. The sample is only the responding class or a simulation, not all Catholic pupils. No answer may infer faith, holiness, prayer practice, or identity.
- **Worked exemplar:** Chart title `Simulated classroom appreciation choices`, bars 6/4/3, source `practice data—not our class`. Revise `Catholic students like drawing best` to `In this simulated 13-response dataset, thank-you drawing received 6 choices, two more than cleanup.` Annotate the bounded sample, count, difference, and removed generalization.
- **Board/visual setup:** Prepare tally columns plus `blank/double mark`, a valid-total check, and a 0-6 axis. Post `ANONYMOUS: no names | one choice | abstention allowed` and a caption checklist `source + count comparison + no faith claim`.
- **Discussion and misconception checks:** “Can this chart tell who chose quiet prayer?” (no), “Does a choice measure holiness?” (no), “Can we generalize to all schools?” (no), and “What happens to blank/double ballots?” (label invalid/nonresponse and exclude from valid total, without guessing). Correct public speculation about classmates immediately.
- **Kit, labels, reset:** Per pupil: `1 ballot scrap, 1 graph sheet, 1 pencil`; per team: `1 metric ruler, 2 markers`. Label ballot box `W23 APPRECIATION CHOICE | UNNAMED | ADULT ONLY`; label pouches `W23 assigned team ID | 1 RULER + 2 MARKERS`. Adult verifies tally then destroys ballots under school practice; pupils cap/count markers, return rulers, and file charts by class without ballots attached.
- **Unfinished work:** Place incomplete charts in `W23 AGGREGATE DATA ONLY` with the final tally card and mark `bars`, `labels`, `comparison`, or `caption`. Finish using aggregate counts; individual ballots are not retained, reconstructed, or taken home.
- **Annotated error/reteach:** Flag truncated axis `bar difference exaggerated`; total 14 for 6/4/3 `addition error`; caption “our school prefers” `sample too broad`; named or guessed voters `privacy violation—remove and report`. Reteach by mapping 13 counters to bars, then revise one sentence using the frame `In this [source], ___ received ___, which is ___ more/less than ___.`
- **Safety/privacy classification:** Physical severity **minor**, likelihood **rare**; privacy or coercion harm severity **moderate**, likelihood **unlikely** with anonymous simultaneous voting and abstention. Adult ratio **1:25 maximum**, with the adult controlling ballots and teams capped at 3 for chart work. The markers are a commercial marker/ink product; record the exact manufacturer/product in [Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv](../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Keep that product **ON HOLD** until school approval and SDS applicability are resolved.
- **Print accessibility:** Use 14-point text, 10 mm checkboxes, patterned bars with written labels, and a 0-25 large-print axis option. Read choices neutrally, accept pointing or private adult marking, and allow oral comparison/scribing. Test black-and-white copies for category distinction and ensure `abstain` is as visible as the three choices.
- **Student material:** Student material: give each pupil one unnamed appreciation-choice ballot, one graph sheet with simulated-source option `6/4/3`, one pencil, and fields for total, comparison, sample, draft caption, privacy check, and revision.
- **Worked example:** Worked example: tally the simulated choices `drawing 6`, `cleanup 4`, and `quiet prayer 3`, total 13, and state that drawing exceeds cleanup by 2 without inferring faith or identity.
- **Finished example:** Finished example: the zero-based graph titled `Simulated classroom appreciation choices` shows bars 6/4/3, source `practice data—not our class`, and the bounded caption comparing drawing and cleanup.
- **Visual example:** Visual example: display tally columns with `blank/double mark`, a valid-total check, a 0-6 axis, the anonymous-voting rule, and the caption checklist `source + count comparison + no faith claim`.
- **Kit label:** Kit label: mark the ballot box `W23 APPRECIATION CHOICE | UNNAMED | ADULT ONLY`; mark each tool pouch `W23`, the school-assigned team identifier, and `1 RULER + 2 MARKERS`.
- **Annotated exemplar:** Annotated exemplar: mark the finished graph’s simulated source, 6/4/3 heights, total 13, two-choice difference, bounded sample, and removal of the unsupported `Catholic students like drawing best` claim.
- **Common error:** Common error: a truncated axis, total 14, school-wide generalization, or named/guessed voter is invalid; annotate the exact scale, arithmetic, sample, or privacy issue before caption revision.
