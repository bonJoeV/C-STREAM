# Week 13: Advent Preparation

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly B; Light; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** compare transmission through one/two tissue layers and revise
an Advent symbol for reader clarity under equal light conditions.
**Why:** optical evidence supports design, while Advent light signifies hope.
CST-C1 measured/symbolic distinction; CST-S1 observation; CST-S3 light path;
CST-A2 purposeful form; CST-A3 critique/revision.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**, physical
flashlight needed; introductory. Prep 10 plus battery check; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class / supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9): enclosed flashlight, three tissue squares, cardstock frame,
glue stick. Adult precuts window, checks secured batteries. Per pupil: paper/
pencil. Teacher board/timer; pretest at fixed 20 cm light-to-frame distance,
demo from kit. Use star or simple branch as **classroom devotional convention**,
not claim about every Jesse Tree. No flame/jar/electrical ornament.

## SAFETY

**Background, vocabulary, and troubleshooting:**

Transmission = light passes through; absorption = energy taken up by material;
symbol = image communicating an idea; contrast = visible difference.
**Misconception:** colored paper makes new light. **If asked "Is Jesus' light
what the flashlight measures?"** No; religious imagery is not an optical unit.
No glass, flames, lasers, loose cells, sunlight viewing or dark-room travel.
Beam onto paper, no eyes. Adult handles batteries; stop/report damage.
Indoor flashlight pathway works without winter daylight.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Advent as preparation/hope; "What can physical light tests tell us?"
2. **4-9:** Demonstrate one/two layers at 20 cm, same viewing point.
3. **9-21:** Each observes both, records appearance/path, then contributes a
   purposeful tissue/star/branch arrangement communicating hope.
4. **21-30:** Peer names intended symbol at 1 m; record correct/not yet, revise
   one overlap/outline and retest at identical distances.
5. **30-35:** Each child explains two layer observations, visual revision and symbol
   versus measured-light distinction.
6. **35-40:** Off/count lights, cap glue, save paper art/dated evidence.

## What success looks like

Meets: accurate comparisons/path and critique-backed symbolic design, not
beautiful decoration alone. Grade 3/support: precut frame/arrow stems/dictation.
Grade 4/challenge: transmission-versus-readability tradeoff. Weak contrast:
change outline/overlap, not brighter unsafe light. Missing lights: share across
neighbor teams within work block; absent hardware means art/model evidence
only, optical test not observed. Early finish: new reader, same conditions.

**Family:** We compared light transmission and revised Advent art. Ask, "What
changed after feedback?" Optional: notice a light symbol in a picture.

## Teacher artifact pack

- **Student light-and-symbol record (print/no-print):** Fields: `Name/team/date`, `Flashlight-to-frame distance: 20 cm`, a table `one tissue layer / two tissue layers` with rows `amount of light seen (more/less)`, `edges visible`, and `evidence words`, then `Intended Advent symbol`, `Reader at 1 m named ___`, `correct/not yet`, `outline/overlap revised`, `retest`, and `Physical light evidence is different from the religious meaning because ___`. Pupils may copy these fields onto plain paper.
- **Expected evidence/answers:** With the same light and position, one layer will usually appear to transmit more light than two; accept careful observations such as brighter/dimmer or sharper/less sharp without pretending to measure lux. The peer may identify a star, simple branch, or another teacher-approved hope symbol. Accept `the flashlight test compares material transmission; it does not measure Jesus, hope, or faith` for the distinction.
- **Completed example:** `At 20 cm, one layer: brighter, hand outline easy to see; two layers: dimmer, outline harder to see. Symbol: five-point star. First reader at 1 m said "flower"; revision: separate points and add a dark cardstock outline; retest: "star."` Annotate equal distances, the increased layer count, the observed difference, and the reader-driven design change.
- **Board/visual arrangement:** Mark a side-view light path `flashlight -> 20 cm -> tissue frame -> viewer`, a one-layer/two-layer comparison table, and a separate box `OPTICAL OBSERVATION` versus `ADVENT SYMBOLIC MEANING`. Add a 1 m floor/desk reference for the symbol reading test.
- **Discussion and misconceptions:** Ask “Did the colored tissue create new light?” (no; it transmitted/absorbed portions of the flashlight's light), “What stayed equal?” (light, distance, viewing place), “Why did the reader see a flower?” (overlap/outline reduced clarity), and “What does the flashlight not measure?” (religious hope or belief). Correct any claim that more layers make the source brighter.
- **Kit, label, and reset:** Per team: `1 enclosed flashlight, 3 tissue squares, 1 cardstock frame, 1 glue stick`; per pupil: `1 record sheet, 1 pencil`. Label kit `W13 | assigned team ID | 1 LIGHT/3 TISSUE/1 FRAME/1 GLUE`; adult marks each secured flashlight with team ID. Turn lights off, adult checks casing/battery door, cap glue, place unused tissue flat, keep finished window and record together, and reconcile 4/5/7/9 lights without opening battery compartments.
- **Unfinished work:** Store the frame flat in the team kit with a note `layer comparison`, `symbol assembly`, `reader test`, or `retest`. Resume under the same 20 cm and 1 m conditions; do not send flashlights home or claim optical results for a pupil who missed the comparison.
- **Annotated errors/reteach:** Label a beam aimed at eyes `stop—unsafe direction`; changed flashlight distance `comparison not controlled`; “two layers made darkness” `relative transmission, not absence of all light`; unrecognized symbol `communication not yet clear, not bad art`. Reteach with two loose tissue samples over the same paper target, then trace a simple separated outline before one fresh reader check.
- **Safety/supervision:** Severity **moderate** for eye exposure or loose-cell access, though consequence is limited by an enclosed low-power flashlight; likelihood **unlikely** with beam-to-paper rules and adult battery handling. Adult ratio is **1 adult to no more than 9 teams/25 pupils**, all lights visible. Stop for a hot, cracked, flickering, or unsecured unit. The glue stick is a commercial adhesive product; record the exact manufacturer/product in [Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv](../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Keep that product **ON HOLD** until school approval and SDS applicability are resolved; batteries remain enclosed and are not handled by pupils.
- **Print accessibility:** Use 14-point text, high-contrast layer symbols (`1` and `2`, not colors), tactile tissue samples, and spacious observation boxes. Permit oral comparison, pointing, a scribe, or nonvisual description of warmth-free light path and material layers; do not require dark-room travel. Test the sheet in grayscale and verify the optical/symbolic columns remain distinct.
- **Student material:** Student material: give each pupil the light-and-symbol record for the controlled one-layer/two-layer comparison, intended Advent symbol, 1 m reader response, outline revision, retest, and optical-versus-religious distinction.
- **Visual example:** Visual example: draw the controlled path `flashlight -> 20 cm -> tissue frame -> viewer`, the one-layer/two-layer table, separate `OPTICAL OBSERVATION` and `ADVENT SYMBOLIC MEANING` boxes, and the 1 m reader-test distance.
- **Board setup:** Board setup: display the light path, controlled layer comparison, optical/symbolic distinction, and 1 m symbol-reading reference without adding a technology artifact.
- **Kit label:** Kit label: mark each kit `W13`, the school-assigned team identifier, and `1 LIGHT/3 TISSUE/1 FRAME/1 GLUE`; mark the secured flashlight with the same identifier.
- **Annotated exemplar:** Annotated exemplar: mark the equal 20 cm distances, brighter one-layer observation, dimmer two-layer observation, first `flower` reading, separated star points, dark outline, and successful `star` retest.
- **Common error:** Common error: a changed flashlight distance, beam aimed at eyes, claim that tissue creates light, or optical observation presented as a faith measurement must be annotated and corrected before retest.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Print/accessibility check: print the black-and-white light record at 100% with 14-point text, high-contrast `1` and `2` layer symbols, separate optical/symbolic columns, and spacious observation boxes.
