# Week 13: Advent Preparation

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly B; Light; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** compare transmission through one/two tissue layers and revise
an Advent symbol for reader clarity under equal light conditions.
**Why:** optical evidence supports design, while Advent light signifies hope.
CST-C1 measured/symbolic distinction; CST-S1 observation; CST-S3 light path;
CST-A2 purposeful form; CST-A3 critique/revision.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**, physical
flashlight needed; introductory. Prep 10 plus battery check; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9): enclosed flashlight, three tissue squares, cardstock frame,
glue stick. Adult precuts window, checks secured batteries. Per pupil: paper/
pencil. Teacher board/timer; pretest at fixed 20 cm light-to-frame distance,
demo from kit. Use star or simple branch as **classroom devotional convention**,
not claim about every Jesse Tree. No flame/jar/electrical ornament.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Transmission = light passes through; absorption = energy taken up by material;
symbol = image communicating an idea; contrast = visible difference.
**Misconception:** colored paper makes new light. **If asked "Is Jesus' light
what the flashlight measures?"** No; religious imagery is not an optical unit.
No glass, flames, lasers, loose cells, sunlight viewing or dark-room travel.
Beam onto paper, no eyes. Adult handles batteries; stop/report damage.
Indoor flashlight pathway works without winter daylight.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Advent as preparation/hope; "What can physical light tests tell us?"
2. **4-9:** Demonstrate one/two layers at 20 cm, same viewing point.
3. **9-21:** Each observes both, records appearance/path, then contributes a
   purposeful tissue/star/branch arrangement communicating hope.
4. **21-30:** Peer names intended symbol at 1 m; record correct/not yet, revise
   one overlap/outline and retest at identical distances.
5. **30-35:** Each child explains two layer observations, visual revision and symbol
   versus measured-light distinction.
6. **35-40:** Off/count lights, cap glue, save paper art/dated evidence.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: accurate comparisons/path and critique-backed symbolic design, not
beautiful decoration alone. Grade 3/support: precut frame/arrow stems/dictation.
Grade 4/challenge: transmission-versus-readability tradeoff. Weak contrast:
change outline/overlap, not brighter unsafe light. Missing lights: share across
neighbor teams within work block; absent hardware means art/model evidence
only, optical test not observed. Early finish: new reader, same conditions.

**Family:** We compared light transmission and revised Advent art. Ask, "What
changed after feedback?" Optional: notice a light symbol in a picture.
