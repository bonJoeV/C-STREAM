# Week 12: Gifts for Others

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly B; Service design; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** make a readable two-step classroom-help card, test a reader's
response twice and revise. **Why:** a functional gift responds to need, not
assumed medical benefit. Catholic/CST-C2: dignity/consent; CST-E1: readable
instructions criterion; CST-E2 retest; CST-A2 purposeful layout/contrast.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**;
introductory. Prep 10; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class / supplies

Fictional recipient brief: "A visitor needs to know: put a book in the return
tray, then place the marker beside it." Classroom peers test by pointing,
not real delivery. Per pupil: two half-sheet cardstock blanks, evidence paper/
pencil. Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) share two markers/ruler. Teacher board/timer and drawn
book/tray/marker symbols; demo uses issued blank. Adult approves any later gift
delivery; absent permission, keep display at school.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Card blanks (2/pupil) | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| Evidence paper; pencils (1/pupil each) | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |

## SAFETY

**Background, vocabulary, and troubleshooting:**

Recipient = intended user; sequence = order; contrast = visible difference;
criterion = success. **Misconception:** nice appearance proves comprehension.
**If asked "Will this comfort a hospital patient?"** We did not test that;
organizations have restrictions, adult approval needed.
No food, medical items, small manipulatives for younger pupils, private
photos/names/health data or unapproved visits. Paper indoors; report damaged
tools. No unverified Seton quote/history claim needed.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "How does listening make a gift useful?" Optional service prayer.
2. **4-9:** Model two numbered actions with word/symbol; criterion reader names
   both in order at 50 cm within ten seconds.
3. **9-21:** Each designs card 1, purposeful visual choices, brief original thanks.
4. **21-30:** Peer tests twice; record correct actions 0-2; revise on card 2 and
   repeat twice, same distance/time.
5. **30-35:** Each child shows need/criterion, four counts and visual rationale.
6. **35-40:** Cap/count, save dated versions/evidence; adult handles delivery.

## What success looks like

Meets: user/criterion, actual four test counts and justified layout revision.
Grade 3/support: large numbered boxes/pictures/scribing. Grade 4/challenge:
clarity-versus-space tradeoff without relying on color. Reader confused: simplify
one word/symbol; do not coach during test. No card: folded paper, same criterion.
Early finish: another reader, note sample limit. Joy/prayer not scored.

**Family:** We made and tested functional gifts. Ask, "What could your reader
actually understand?" Optional: thank a helper, no donation/home task.

## Teacher artifact pack

- **Student design/test sheet (print/no-print):** Fields: `Designer/date`, `Fictional recipient need`, `Card 1 sketch`, `Step 1 words + symbol`, `Step 2 words + symbol`, `Test distance 50 cm`, `Reader test 1/2: actions named in order (0-2)`, `One clarity problem`, `Card 2 revision`, `Retest 1/2 (0-2)`, and `Why the layout helps`. If printing is unavailable, pupils copy these headings on evidence paper and use the two cardstock blanks for the actual cards.
- **Expected evidence:** Accept wording equivalent to `1. Put the book in the RETURN TRAY. 2. Put the marker BESIDE the book`, paired with recognizable symbols and numbering. A score of 2 means the reader independently states both actions in order within ten seconds at 50 cm; coaching invalidates that test. Four test counts and one specific layout revision are required; beauty alone is not evidence of comprehension.
- **Worked finished example:** Card 1 uses small unnumbered pictures and tests `1, 0`; readers reverse the sequence. Card 2 adds large `1` and `2`, the words `RETURN TRAY` and `BESIDE`, and black outlines; retests `2, 2`. Annotate the sequence markers, high contrast, unchanged two actions, and the evidence-based reason for revision.
- **Visual/board setup:** Draw a 50 cm ruler line between `CARD` and `READER`, two numbered action boxes, and a four-cell score chart. Post the fictional brief exactly and a contrast check: `Can it be understood without color? Can both actions be named in order?`
- **Discussion questions:** “What does a score of 1 tell the designer?” (one action was communicated; identify which), “May the designer explain during a test?” (no), “Why use words and symbols?” (more than one access path), and “Does this prove the card will help a hospital patient?” (no; only classroom peer clarity was tested). Correct assumptions about a real recipient's needs or medical benefit.
- **Kit, labels, reset:** Per pupil: `2 half-sheet cardstock blanks, 1 evidence paper, 1 pencil`; per team: `1 metric ruler, 2 markers`. Label work envelope `W12 | assigned designer ID | CARD 1 + CARD 2 + TESTS`; label team pouch `W12 assigned team ID | 1 RULER + 2 MARKERS`. Cap and count markers, return ruler, keep both card versions with the evidence sheet, and place any proposed delivery in `ADULT APPROVAL REQUIRED`.
- **Unfinished work:** File partial work by designer with one box checked: `Card 1`, `two reader tests`, `Card 2`, or `two retests`. Resume with a classroom peer at the same 50 cm condition; an unfinished or unapproved card remains at school and is not delivered or assigned at home.
- **Annotated error/reteach:** Mark a card with decorative script but unreadable steps `appearance is not comprehension`; a coached score `not an independent test`; color-only order `add number/word/symbol`; a real person's name/health detail `remove private information`. Reteach by physically sequencing book, tray, and marker, converting each action to one short sentence and one symbol, then testing with a new reader.
- **Safety classification:** Severity **minor** (paper edge or marker misuse), likelihood **unlikely**. Adult ratio **1:25 maximum**, teams no larger than 3; teacher controls any contact with outside organizations and prohibits food, medical items, photos, and personal data. The markers are a commercial marker/ink product; record the exact manufacturer/product in [Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv](../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv). Keep that product **ON HOLD** until school approval and SDS applicability are resolved.
- **Print accessibility:** Use at least 18-point type on student-facing card templates, numbered boxes with thick black borders, ample symbol space, and no color-dependent meaning. Offer large-print blanks, tactile number labels, dictation, adapted grip, or a scribe. View the printed exemplar from 50 cm under classroom lighting and verify both steps remain legible in grayscale.
- **Kit label:** Kit label: mark each work envelope `W12`, the school-assigned designer identifier, and `CARD 1 + CARD 2 + TESTS`; mark each team pouch with its school-assigned identifier and `1 RULER + 2 MARKERS`.
- **Annotated exemplar:** Annotated exemplar: on Card 2, mark the added large numbers `1` and `2`, the words `RETURN TRAY` and `BESIDE`, black outlines, unchanged actions, and retest scores `2, 2`.
- **Common error:** Common error: decorative script, coaching during the reader test, color-only order, or private recipient information invalidates the evidence; annotate the specific issue before a new-reader retest.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Print/accessibility check: print the black-and-white card and evidence sheets at 100% with at least 18-point student-facing card text, numbered thick-border boxes, and enough room for symbols, dictation, or a scribe.
