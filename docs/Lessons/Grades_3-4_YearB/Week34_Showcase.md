# Week 34: Year B Showcase

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly B; Portfolio; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** support a growth claim with dated artifacts, independently read
a data record and revise an explanatory caption.
**Why:** evidence celebrates effort accurately, not automatic all-domain mastery.
Catholic/CST-C3 gifts/work stewardship; CST-E3 explanation/limit;
CST-A3 critique/revision; CST-M2 data reading. Official alignment
**VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; developing.
Prep 15; cleanup 5. No live hardware or award categories asserting unobserved skill.

## Before class / supplies

Per pupil: portfolio, two sheets/pencil. Teacher board/timer; retrieve Week 18
tracker if present. Write **simulated backup counts 3 then 5** for a new
two-bar reading when artifacts absent; not the pupil's historic results.
Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) share at desks. No photos/private media required.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

## SAFETY

**Background, vocabulary, and troubleshooting:**

Growth = supported change; artifact = saved work; demonstration = individual
showing; limitation = inference not supported. **Misconception:** four years of
attendance equals mastery. **If asked "Can I say programmed?"** Only if actual
operation was individually observed; otherwise say traced/planned.
Local mastery requires two dated observations on separate occasions including
individual demonstration/changed context, not this showcase alone.
Dry indoor paper only, no restarted devices/circuits, food/balloons or private
photos. Report missing artifacts openly.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Optional gratitude prayer; "Which evidence shows a changed choice?"
2. **4-9:** Model dated-result caption and observed/simulated distinction.
3. **9-20:** Select two artifacts; read table/graph independently; compare
   Week 18 goal tracker if present. Missing artifacts: draw/read backup bars,
   total 8/difference 2, label new simulated practice.
4. **20-29:** Parallel team turns, two minutes/pupil; demonstrate evidence/
   explain data, peer gives specific caption feedback.
5. **29-35:** Each revises caption, states supported growth or missing evidence,
   explains two values and stewardship action.
6. **35-40:** Date/file, return portfolios/pencils, clear; optional recognition
   means participation, not certification.

## What success looks like

Meets: attributable explanation/data reading, accurate evidence label/limit,
critique-backed caption. Growth credit requires actual dated records.
Grade 3/support: two-caption stems/scribing. Grade 4/challenge: transfer check/
tradeoff explanation. Missing tracker: do not invent goal success. Long shares:
task-result-limit within two minutes, not serial speeches. Early finish: explain
what next evidence would resolve a gap.

**Family:** We shared Year B evidence honestly. Ask, "What does your record
support?" Optional: listen to one explanation, no summer homework.

## Teacher artifact pack

- **Student showcase folio (print/no-print):** Sheet 1: `Name/date`, `Artifact 1 title/date/result`, `Artifact 2 title/date/result`, `Supported growth claim`, `Evidence that changed`, `What the artifacts do not prove`, and `Week 18 goal connection or not available`. Sheet 2: `Data source: saved artifact OR new simulated bars 3 and 5`, `value 1`, `value 2`, `total`, `difference`, `caption draft`, `peer's specific feedback`, `revised caption`, and `two-minute demonstration checklist`. No-print: copy these exact headings onto the two supplied sheets.
- **Expected evidence/answer guidance:** A growth claim must cite attributable dated work and a changed decision/result; attendance or enthusiasm alone is insufficient. For backup bars, values are 3 and 5, total 8, difference 2, labeled `new simulated practice—not my past performance`. Accept an honest `missing evidence` statement. “Programmed” is acceptable only where individual device operation was recorded; otherwise use `traced`, `planned`, or `paper-tested`.
- **Finished worked example:** `Artifact 1, W04 V1 dated Oct. 20: three distances recorded but no controls. Artifact 2, W29 dated Apr. 7: graph includes units and source. Claim: I improved at labeling evidence because the later graph names mm and simulated source. Limit: these two artifacts do not show mastery of every science tool.` Backup caption example: revise `I grew from 3 to 5` to `In a new simulated practice graph, the second bar is 5, two more than the first; it is not my historical result.`
- **Board/visual setup:** Post a model sequence `CLAIM -> DATED ARTIFACT -> SPECIFIC RESULT -> LIMIT`, the backup 3/5 bars, a two-minute timer structure `task/result/limit`, and columns `observed`, `simulated`, `missing`. Keep portfolio viewing at desks rather than on a public wall.
- **Discussion prompts and acceptable responses:** “What makes the claim attributable?” (name/date/artifact or class identifier), “Can missing work be replaced by an invented success?” (no), “What does 5-3 show?” (difference 2 in the simulated practice only), and “Does showcase participation certify mastery?” (no). Correct broad “I mastered STREAM” claims by narrowing them to the evidence shown.
- **Kit contents:** Per pupil: `1 student portfolio, 2 plain-paper sheets, 1 pencil`; class: `timer and board`.
- **Kit label:** Kit label: mark the collection tray `W34 | SHOWCASE FOLIOS | RETURN TO PORTFOLIOS`; use only existing school identifiers on the pupil folios.
- **Cleanup and reset:** Label collection tray `W34 | SHOWCASE FOLIOS | RETURN TO PORTFOLIOS`; use existing school identifiers only. Clip the two new sheets together, restore artifacts in original portfolio order, count/return pencils, remove backup practice sheets from display, and secure portfolios under the school's records practice.
- **Unfinished work:** Check one status: `select artifacts`, `read data`, `draft claim/limit`, `peer feedback`, or `caption revision`. Complete during a scheduled in-class portfolio interval; do not fabricate dates/results, restart old hardware, require a family presentation, or display unfinished personal work.
- **Annotated exemplar:** Annotated exemplar: mark the later artifact’s `mm` unit and simulated-source label, the supported improvement in evidence labeling, and the limit that two artifacts do not establish mastery of every science tool.
- **Safety/privacy classification:** Physical severity **minor**, likelihood **rare** for seated paper work; privacy/emotional severity **moderate**, likelihood **unlikely** when portfolios stay at assigned desks and feedback addresses artifacts, not worth. Adult ratio **1:25 maximum**, teams no larger than 3, with teacher monitoring portfolio access and two-minute turns. **SDS: not applicable**.
- **Print accessibility:** Use 14-point minimum, large evidence boxes, a visual two-minute sequence, and labels that do not depend on color. Offer large print, read-aloud, oral demonstration, scribing, or pointing to artifact evidence. Test one grayscale copy and ensure the simulated-source warning appears beside—not below—the 3/5 bars.
- **Student material:** Student material: give each pupil the two-sheet showcase folio for two dated artifacts, supported growth claim, evidence change, limitation, 3/5 simulated backup bars, caption feedback/revision, and two-minute demonstration.
- **Finished example:** Finished example: the folio compares the dated W04 record lacking controls with the dated W29 graph containing units and source, then states only the supported growth in evidence labeling and its limit.
- **Visual example:** Visual example: display `CLAIM -> DATED ARTIFACT -> SPECIFIC RESULT -> LIMIT`, the backup 3/5 bars labeled new simulated practice, and the `observed | simulated | missing` columns.
- **Board setup:** Board setup: post the claim-evidence-limit sequence, backup bars, and two-minute `task/result/limit` structure while keeping private portfolios at assigned desks.
- **Common error:** Common error: an undated artifact, `I programmed` supported only by a paper trace, simulated 3/5 bars presented as personal history, or an all-domain mastery claim must be annotated and narrowed.
- **Reteach:** Reteach: use two teacher sample artifacts to mark date/result, claim/limit, and observed/simulated source, then rehearse a 30-second `task -> result -> limit` explanation before revising the caption.
- **Print/accessibility check:** Print/accessibility check: print the black-and-white showcase folio at 100% with high contrast, 14-point minimum text, large evidence boxes, and the simulated-source warning beside the 3/5 bars.
