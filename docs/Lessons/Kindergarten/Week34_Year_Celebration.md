## Before class and supplies

Read [K routines](../../Resources/Kindergarten_Reference_Routines.md).
Retrieve each child's dated evidence, ideally two observations of the same
competency. A single new demonstration cannot establish year-long growth.
Fresh-evidence fallback: 3-by-3 grid, START lower-left, target right then up,
large token and RIGHT/UP/DOWN/STOP cards.
Budget 5/8/10/13 kits; active teams 5/7/10/12.

## Vocabulary and background

**Evidence:** an actual dated demonstration. **Growth:** change supported by
comparable observations. **Next step:** a skill to practice. If asked whether
everyone is ready for Grade 1, report documented skills/supports; attendance,
certificates and group products do not establish every readiness target.
Misconception: today's success proves a year of independent mastery.

## Exact sequence: 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Celebrate effort and gratitude; introduce honest demonstration.
2. **4-8:** Model explaining one record plus a limitation. For missing work,
   model RIGHT, UP, STOP and correction of an intentionally wrong DOWN.
3. **8-17:** Each child selects/explains evidence or performs the fresh route.
   Partners ask what the record shows; teacher begins individual checks.
4. **17-21:** Child shows a real result, states prompted/independent help as
   appropriate and names a next action. Record absent evidence, never invent it.
5. **21-25:** Share concurrently in pairs, date/save records for school handoff,
   return kits/crayons and clear. No serial 25-child speeches.

## Success, access, SAFETY and troubleshooting

Check truthful result, understandable explanation and next practice action.
Support: picture choices, pointing, scribing and large parts. Challenge:
compare two dated records and explain a limit.
Desk-only paper kits; no food, public names/images or compulsory summer work.
Missing portfolio: fresh dated evidence and a note that growth comparison
was unavailable. Child cannot yet demonstrate: record the needed support,
celebrate belonging and schedule instruction, not a success-shaped certificate.
Works indoors with no devices; camera documentation needs school approval.

**Family:** We celebrated one demonstrated skill and an honest next step.
Ask, "What can you show, and what will you practice?" Optional questions only.
