## Before class and materials

Read [K routines](../../Resources/Kindergarten_Reference_Routines.md).

Draw a 3-by-3 grid, START lower-left and recipient one square right and one up.
Draw four large cards: RIGHT, UP, DOWN, STOP. Arrows refer to the page.
Use an 8 cm paper token with a child's drawn gratitude image.

## Vocabulary, background and SAFETY

**Algorithm:** steps in order. **Predict:** say where steps will end.
**Debug:** find and correct an error. **Message:** idea for another person.
Misconception: a motion-lines drawing is executable code. A person follows
our paper rules; that is an algorithm model, not software execution.
If asked about computers, explain that real programs must be run on a device.
Desk grids, large parts, no body maze, small tokens or public student uploads.

## Exact sequence: 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Choose a school helper or trusted recipient; draw/describe a thankful
   message without sharing private details. Original prayer optional.
2. **4-8:** Model RIGHT, DOWN, STOP missing the target. Reset; replace DOWN
   with UP and test again. STOP is not a movement. Explain the human process:
   name the delivery goal, break it into two moves, then put the moves in order.
3. **8-17:** Partners predict RIGHT, UP, STOP, follow cards, reset and fix the
   intentionally wrong path. Rotate each planner/mover turn; begin checks.
4. **17-21:** Each child predicts the endpoint, changes one wrong card and
   explains/points to the message's meaning and recipient. Ask who chose the
   instructions and have the child show the goal, smaller moves and their order.
5. **21-25:** Share two messages without personal information; store four-card
   kits, retain dated records and clear.

## Success, support, troubleshooting and extension

Record prediction, tested correction and communicated meaning separately.
Ask "Which step changed? Did the output match?" Support: two pictured choices
and adult movement directed by the child. Challenge: test reversed order.
Misoriented grid: fix page orientation before retesting. Missing cards: draw
arrows on ordinary paper. Entire primary lesson works indoors without devices.

**Optional digital extension:** only after regular-teacher compatibility/
access preflight, replace practice time with an already-open ScratchJr project.
Attach a green-flag start followed by two blue movement blocks; tap the flag,
observe, change a direction and rerun. Each child must execute to receive
software evidence; observing or paper movement does not qualify. Installed
controls: [ScratchJr interface](https://www.scratchjr.org/learn/interface).
If setup fails, return to paper and mark execution unassessed.

**Family:** We communicated thanks and corrected picture instructions.
Ask, "Which card did you change?" No home app, purchase or routine homework.
