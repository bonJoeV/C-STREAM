## Vocabulary and background

**Retest:** repeat a check. **Record:** saved information. **Repeat:** recurring
unit in a pattern. **Debug:** correct instructions.
Misconception: visiting a station proves mastery. Each child supplies the
specific evidence below. If asked why weather did not change, explain that
unchanged observations are valid; two records cannot establish a season.
Pattern drawings are representations; paper routes are not software/hardware operation.

## Meeting 19: structure retest, 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Recall a low six-block tower's ten-second unaided criterion.
2. **4-8:** Model a base change with the same six blocks and timing.
3. **8-17:** Partners test, change base and retest; swap roles and begin checks.
4. **17-21:** Each child indicates a change and compares two actual results,
   including a fall. Save a two-result drawing.
5. **21-25:** Count blocks, retain dated evidence, clear trays.

## Meeting 20: two weather records, 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Safely observe sky/cloud coverage away from the Sun; date/time a
   first record if no actual previous class record exists.
2. **4-8:** Model mostly clear/partly cloudy/mostly covered symbols. Explain
   that a forecast, preferred picture or room temperature is not this observation.
3. **8-17:** Children select/draw today's symbol and compare a prior actual
   record when available. Identify matching/different features; begin checks.
4. **17-21:** Make a second timed observation if using the same-day route.
   Each child compares the two labeled records and states what was observed.
5. **21-25:** Save records with dates/times; collect cards/crayons and clear.

## Meeting 21: a readable repeat, 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Name circle/square and a repeated unit, not a favorite color.
2. **4-8:** Model circle-square-circle-square and predict next circle-square.
3. **8-17:** Partners arrange/extend six pieces; swap turns and change spacing
   to make the pattern readable to a partner. Begin individual checks.
4. **17-21:** Each child selects the next two shapes and explains/points to
   why the spacing or arrangement helps communication.
5. **21-25:** Save a drawing, count three of each shape per kit, clear.

## Meeting 22: sequence correction, 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Target is one square right and one up from START.
2. **4-8:** Model RIGHT, DOWN, STOP missing it; reset and correct DOWN to UP.
3. **8-17:** Every child predicts, follows and corrects the path in a full turn;
   partners move only by the shown rule. Begin checks.
4. **17-21:** Child shows endpoint and tested correction; save individual evidence.
5. **21-25:** Repack grids/tokens/cards, retain evidence and clear.

## Success, SAFETY, access and troubleshooting

Record four separate checks across the unit, not one participation score.
Support: a modeled choice, large tactile shapes, adult-directed movement or
scribing. Challenge: fair repeated test, record limitation, new repeat or
reversed route. No height contests, small tokens, body mazes, unknown nature
items, Sun viewing or outdoor prerequisite; structures stay low and seated.
No sky view: labeled teacher observation, or explicitly **model data** with
actual-weather evidence deferred. Missing blocks: shared <=3 groups, same test.
All minimum pathways are indoors and device-free.

Optional robot extension is a separately pretested teacher choice; charge/aim
and verify compatible controls before class. Replace, do not add, work time;
actual operation/execution evidence requires each child's real device turn.
If equipment fails, paper evidence remains computational thinking only.

**Family:** We checked structure changes, two weather records, a readable
repeat and picture instructions. Ask about one actual result, not a station
stamp. No homework or donations.
