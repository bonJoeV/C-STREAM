# Week 33: New Life Engineering

The existing filename is retained. This lesson teaches a **science/art model**,
not engineering mastery; no criteria-based prototype is being tested.

## Before class and materials

Read [K routines](../../Resources/Kindergarten_Reference_Routines.md).
Draw four >=5 cm pictures per child: small oval egg on leaf; long segmented
caterpillar; hanging pupa/chrysalis; winged adult. Each child has a full set.
Teacher has one large demonstration set; label the reverse for teacher use.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Student: four large stage cards | 40 | 60 | 80 | 100 |
| Student: model sheet; crayon | 10 each | 15 each | 20 each | 25 each |
| Teacher: additional four-stage set | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 |

Teacher timer/roster; no glue, food, pasta, insect kit or field trip required.

## Vocabulary, background and source

**Life cycle:** stages across generations. **Larva:** caterpillar. **Pupa:**
living changing stage; a butterfly's chrysalis. **Model:** representation.
Order/key: egg -> larva/caterpillar -> pupa/chrysalis -> adult butterfly ->
next-generation eggs. Do not label a butterfly pupa a cocoon, death or tomb.
Source: [USDA monarch biology](https://www.fs.usda.gov/wildflowers/pollinators/Monarch_Butterfly/biology/index.shtml).
Misconception: the pupa is dead. It is alive and changing. If asked about
Easter, distinguish the taught belief in [CCC 638-645](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P1S.HTM)
from this living-insect process; one does not establish the other.

## Exact sequence: 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Name living change; optional original prayer for care/truth. No assumed Easter date.
2. **4-8:** Model four stages in order; show that the pictures are not live animals.
3. **8-17:** Each child orders cards, draws arrows and shows one changed feature.
   Partner interprets the model; teacher begins individual checks.
4. **17-21:** Child independently orders/points to four stages, explains living
   change and a model limit. Record prompts, not craft completion.
5. **21-25:** Save dated drawing, count four cards into each envelope and clear.

## Success, questions, access, SAFETY and troubleshooting

Assess order, biological change and representation/limit separately.
Ask "Which stage is next? Is this a real butterfly? Is the pupa alive?"
Support: two adjacent stages at a time and pointing/adult scribing.
Challenge: add next-generation eggs without implying the same adult becomes an egg.
Paper only: no live insect release, pasta, tiny decorations or specimens.
Missing pictures: teacher draws the four described forms on the board; pupils
point and make a directed drawing. Entire lesson works indoors in winter.

**Family:** We ordered a living butterfly's stages and explained a model's limits.
Ask, "What comes after the caterpillar?" No home collection or routine homework.
