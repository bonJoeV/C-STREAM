## Before class and exact materials

Read [K routines](../../Resources/Kindergarten_Reference_Routines.md).
Use 5/7/10/12 active pair/trio teams and 5/8/10/13 budgeted kits.
Inspect blocks; test one low six-block model; draw a two-attempt picture record.
KEVA is optional branding; ordinary large stable wooden blocks work.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Kit: 20 large blocks | 100 | 160 | 200 | 260 |
| Kit: tray/mat | 5 | 8 | 10 | 13 |
| Student: sheet; crayon, per meeting | 10 each | 15 each | 20 each | 25 each |

Teacher: timer, roster and one shared measuring ruler. Demonstrate using a
budgeted kit, then return it. Retain the first meeting's sheets at school.

## Vocabulary and background

**Base:** bottom supporting parts. **Stable:** stands without a hand holding it.
**Test:** check against a criterion. **Redesign:** change and test again.
**Misconception:** tallest means best. Criterion is ten seconds unaided,
with the same six blocks, not maximum height.
**If asked why a tower falls:** identify slipping, leaning or a changed support;
do not promise widening always wins.

## Meeting 1: a fair starting test, 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Discuss buildings serving people and caring for shared supplies.
2. **4-8:** Model six blocks on a low base. Teacher times ten seconds, hands away.
3. **8-17:** Partners build with six selected blocks, swap turns, count and test.
   Teacher gives a common start/stop signal; begin individual checks.
4. **17-21:** Each child counts the chosen set, points to the base and reports
   standing/falling. Record actual results, including falls.
5. **21-25:** Draw/retain a first-attempt picture; count blocks back; clear trays.

## Meeting 2: change and retest, 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Retrieve drawings, not unverifiable memories. Restate six blocks/ten seconds.
2. **4-8:** Demonstrate changing only base arrangement with the same blocks.
3. **8-17:** Each partner chooses a base change, rebuilds and observes the same
   timed test. Rotate the trio's builder turn; start individual checks.
4. **17-21:** Child indicates the change, counts the set and compares two
   outcomes. Ask "What stayed the same? Did your change help in this test?"
5. **21-25:** Save dated evidence, return all 20 kit blocks, thank partners, clear.

## Success, support, challenge and SAFETY

Record counting, observed result and tested change separately. Falling is
evidence, not failure of a child. Support: a three-block model and prompted
count, with reduced criterion recorded. Challenge: repeat the six-block test
to check reliability, not height. Build seated below shoulder height; no
throwing, walking gallery, shaking another model or standing on furniture.

## Troubleshooting, indoor alternative and family copy

Missing first sheet: run a fresh first test, label it today, then compare.
No blocks: groups <=3 may share kits using the routines; do not claim a paper
picture is a tested tower. Repeated collapse: use flat blocks on a wider base.
All work indoors. **Family:** We counted blocks and compared a ten-second test
before and after a change. Ask, "What changed, and what did the test show?"
