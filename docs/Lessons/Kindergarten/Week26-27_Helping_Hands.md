# Weeks 26-27: Helping Hands Engineering

## Lesson at a glance

| Field | Plan |
|---|---|
| Grade / unit / time | K / Requested-need design / two 25-minute meetings |
| Domains / big idea | C, E, A; serving a user means listening and balancing criteria |
| Objective / why | "I can plan a stand, test it and explain a change." A useful design needs more than an appealing appearance. |
| Catholic connection | Dignity means asking what is wanted, not stereotyping age, illness or disability. |
| Local standards | CST-C2: requested need; CST-E1: two criteria/limits; CST-E2: retest; CST-A1: plan; CST-E3: tradeoff explanation. Official alignment: VERIFICATION REQUIRED. |

## Before class and exact materials

Read [K routines](../../Resources/Kindergarten_Reference_Routines.md).
Use this complete fictional request: "The reader wants our folded paper book
to stand open for ten seconds, without holding, and wants its picture visible."
Pretest a small folded-cardboard stand on a desk. Do not tape across the picture.
Budget 5/8/10/13 kits for active 5/7/10/12 teams.

| Kit/student allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Kit: two smooth 20 cm cardboard squares | 10 | 16 | 20 | 26 |
| Kit: folded paper book; storage tray | 5 each | 8 each | 10 each | 13 each |
| Kit: ten sticks; ten 10 cm tape strips | 50 each | 80 each | 100 each | 130 each |
| Student: plan/result sheet; crayon, per meeting | 10 each | 15 each | 20 each | 25 each |

Teacher: timer/roster, ruler, adult scissors. Draw a large circle on the open
book as the picture; measure a <=20 cm model. Store first-test records at school.

## Vocabulary, background and SAFETY

**Request:** stated need. **Criterion:** success condition. **Constraint:**
limit. **Tradeoff:** one choice can help a feature while making another harder.
A higher front edge may support the book but obscure its picture; test both.
Misconception: everyone in a group needs the same aid. Use only this fictional
request. If asked to give the stand to a real person, explain it is a classroom
model, not an approved assistive device. No wearables, cords, infant/pet trials,
loaded cups, body weight, reaching high shelves or simulated disability.

## Meeting 1: request, plan and first test, 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Read the request; children identify both success conditions.
2. **4-8:** Show simple folded support, ten-second timed test and picture visibility.
3. **8-17:** Each child draws a plan; partners build within the ten-stick/tape
   allowance and test the model. Swap builder/tester turns; begin checks.
4. **17-21:** Record standing yes/no and visible picture yes/no. Child points to
   the plan and identifies a requested feature, not a supposed disability.
5. **21-25:** Date/save records, label dry models by kit, count and clear.

## Meeting 2: improve and defend a tradeoff, 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Retrieve actual two-criterion results; missing results are freshly tested.
2. **4-8:** Model changing a front support while keeping book/test time the same.
3. **8-17:** Partners change one feature and retest both criteria. Rotate each
   child's choice/test turn; begin individual checks.
4. **17-21:** Child compares results and explains/points to a tradeoff:
   support versus visible picture, or material use versus function.
5. **21-25:** Save evidence, reclaim sticks, sort dry cardboard, clear trays.

## Success, questions, access and troubleshooting

Record requested need, plan, two test outcomes, change and tradeoff separately.
Ask "What did the reader request? Did both criteria work? What got harder?"
Support: adult-started stand, two choices, pointing/dictation. Challenge:
compare another support arrangement with the same book/time.
Stand collapses: record failure, then fold a triangular support and retest.
No cardboard: stiff school cardstock with the same criteria; record material.
No safe supplies: plan/criteria only, with testing deferred. Fully indoors.

**Family:** We listened to a request, tested a model twice and explained a
tradeoff. Ask, "Which choice helped one thing but made another harder?"
No home or real-person use is required.
