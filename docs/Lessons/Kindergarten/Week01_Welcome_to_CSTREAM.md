# Week 1: Welcome to C-STREAM

## Lesson at a glance

| Field | Plan |
|---|---|
| Grade / unit / time | K / Welcome and observation / one 25-minute meeting |
| Domains / big idea | S, A; a detail we notice is different from a question we want to investigate |
| Student objective / why | "I can show a detail and ask a question." Scientists begin by noticing and wondering. |
| Catholic connection | Wonder at creation and respectful listening; use the source guidance in the routines below. |
| Local standards | CST-S1: observed detail/question; CST-A3: picture/oral communication. Official alignment: VERIFICATION REQUIRED. |
| Technology / difficulty / prep / cleanup | None / beginner / 5 minutes / 4 minutes included |

## Before class and exact materials

Read [K routines](../../Resources/Kindergarten_Reference_Routines.md). Make
5/7/10/12 active pair/trio teams; budget 5/8/10/13 kits at 10/15/20/25 pupils.
Draw one large leaf with visible branching lines on each kit's half-sheet.
Alternatively use an adult-inspected clean large leaf; pictures avoid allergies.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Kit: large leaf drawing | 5 | 8 | 10 | 13 |
| Student: half-sheet; crayon | 10 each | 15 each | 20 each | 25 each |

Teacher: timer, roster, crayon and one demonstration drawing reused from a kit.
No letter cards, slideshow, purchased journal or printing required.

## Vocabulary and teacher background

**Observe:** notice a detail. **Question:** something we want to find out.
**Represent:** show an idea with a picture, gesture or words.
**Misconception:** a guess is a detail we saw. Point to the actual drawing.
**If asked "Why does a leaf have lines?"** Say that living leaves have veins
that transport water and other materials; our drawing is a model, not a live leaf.

## Exact sequence: 25 minutes

1. **0-4:** Invite wonder and a short original prayer if customary. Say C-STREAM
   is a time to notice, make, test and care; acronym recall is not the target.
2. **4-8:** Model "I see a line" versus "I wonder what the line does." Point to
   the evidence. Draw the detail, not a perfect leaf.
3. **8-17:** Partners inspect, swap pointing roles and each draw/select a
   detail. Ask "What can you point to?" Begin individual checks.
4. **17-21:** Each child points to a genuine detail and asks/selects one question.
   Offer "What does this part do?" or "Are all leaves alike?" Record support.
5. **21-25:** Two children share; retain dated sheets; count drawings and crayons;
   clear tables. Thank partners for listening.

## Success, access and SAFETY

Success: each child connects a picture/gesture to an actual detail and a
question. Do not score enthusiasm, letters or drawing realism. **Support:**
offer two details; scribe an oral question. **Challenge:** ask how a question
could be checked. Large pictures only for mouthing/allergy concerns; no taste,
close smelling or collecting. Fully indoors.

## If things go wrong and family copy

No leaf/drawing: draw a plain leaf with three branching lines on the board.
Child only guesses: ask where the claimed feature appears; keep the question.
Early finish: compare a partner's representation. Follow the routines' cleanup.
**Family:** We noticed a detail and asked a question. Ask, "What did you actually
see, and what do you still wonder?" No routine homework or supplies required.
