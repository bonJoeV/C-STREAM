# Weeks 19-22: STREAM Choice Labs

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly A; Choice inquiry; **four 40-minute meetings**.
**Objective:** conduct a common shadow test, choose a prepared project, manage
stock and use repeated feedback to revise a final explanation/performance.
**Why:** independent choice needs a testable question, not unspecified stations.
Catholic/CST-C3: steward talents/time; CST-S2: common controlled shadow test;
CST-E2: project test/revision; CST-A3: visual/musical critique and revision;
CST-M1: common cm measurements plus counted beats/lengths.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; ordinary
flashlight physically required for shadow inquiry. Developing; no electronics,
chemistry, autonomous robotics or advanced Scratch options retained.
Prep 20 minutes; cleanup 5 each meeting.

## Before class and exact supplies

Balanced teams <=3 (4/5/7/9). Per team/unit: enclosed flashlight, two cardstock
sheets (standing screen and silhouette), ruler, **60 cm tape**. Per pupil:
four passport sheets/pencil. Teacher board/timer; demo uses issued kit. Pretest
desk light at 10/20 cm from same silhouette/screen; normal room lighting.
Write two complete choice cards:

- **Shadow story:** three silhouette arrangements communicate "welcome/help/
  thanks"; test reader's interpretation, no scientific claim from symbolism.
- **Rhythm message:** four-beat welcome pattern using quiet desk taps or gestures;
  listener repeats it, revise for clarity. No loud instruments or faith ranking.

All pupils do the common science test before choosing; no station movement.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Variable = changed feature; silhouette = outline; beat = steady pulse;
iteration = tested revision. Shadow size depends on geometry, not only object
size. Music communicates through purposeful rhythm, not volume.
**Misconception:** choice means no plan. **If asked "Can I invent another lab?"**
Not today; these two have supplies/tests ready. No flames, laser, dark-room
walking, loose cells or skin tape. Lights onto screens, never eyes; adult
battery handling. Report damaged gear. Quiet/seated gestures and indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Optional prayer for stewardship; introduce both choices/passport.
2. **4-10:** Model fixed screen/silhouette, change only light distance 10/20 cm.
3. **10-22:** Every child measures shadow width at each distance; rotate roles,
   repeat both readings and record cm with two controls.
4. **22-30:** Try one shadow phrase and one four-beat rhythm; select project
   using a reason and criterion: reader identifies phrase/listener repeats beat.
5. **30-35:** Individual four readings/controls, choice/stock-use plan.
6. **35-40:** Off/count/store, save passport.

## Meeting 2: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Retrieve choice/question and remaining stock.
2. **4-9:** Model trial log `attempt | response | change`.
3. **9-21:** Build three-image sequence OR compose/notate four-beat pattern;
   every child authors a part and practices.
4. **21-30:** Neighbor tests twice without being told intended answer; record
   correct/not yet and specific visual/rhythmic feedback.
5. **30-35:** Individual explanation of two results and planned revision.
6. **35-40:** Count/save; passport records actual supply use.

## Meeting 3: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "What evidence makes this change worth trying?"
2. **4-9:** Model one changed visual feature or beat pause, keeping task length.
3. **9-21:** Revise chosen project; repeat two equal reader/listener tests.
4. **21-30:** Compare results; every child performs/shows revised part; prepare
   explanation distinguishing science findings from artistic meaning.
5. **30-35:** Individual before/after, quantity comparison and stewardship choice.
6. **35-40:** Store/off/count, no required home finishing.

## Meeting 4: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Review final criterion; no all-domain certification.
2. **4-9:** Model claim supported by passport, plus sample limitation.
3. **9-20:** Final two tests with a new neighboring reader/listener; record.
4. **20-29:** Parallel team showcase, two minutes per child; feedback specific
   to meaning/rhythm, not artistic talent.
5. **29-35:** Each submits four-meeting passport, common science claim and
   critique-backed artistic revision/limit.
6. **35-40:** Return/count stock, flatten reusable silhouettes, archive work.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: common four readings/controls, repeated project tests and deliberate
artistic revision with responsible stock use. Grade 3/support: two shadow
shapes or four pictured beats, scribing. Grade 4/challenge: explain sample
limits/tradeoff between complexity and readability. Weak shadows: closer screen,
repeat equal geometry. No light: common test teacher-led with individual turns;
otherwise model reasoning only, physical inquiry not observed. Rhythm access:
gesture instead of sound, same repeat criterion. Early finish: new listener,
not an unprepared hazardous lab. All work indoors.

**Family:** We chose prepared science/art projects, including rhythm, and revised
using evidence. Ask, "What did feedback change?" Optional: repeat four quiet beats.
