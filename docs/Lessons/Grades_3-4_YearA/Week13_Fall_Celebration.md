# Week 13: Fall STREAM Celebration

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly A; Portfolio; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** explain one artifact using actual evidence, demonstrate its
reasoning again and revise a display caption after feedback. **Why/big idea:**
celebrating growth is more informative than ranking favorite decorations.
Catholic/CST-C3: gifts used responsibly, inventory care; CST-E3: evidence/limit
in explanation; CST-A3: audience critique/caption revision. Official alignment
**VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; developing. Prep 10 minutes;
cleanup 5 included. Celebration is not an all-domain mastery certificate.

## Before class and exact supplies

Return each child's portfolio; identify an earlier plan/test record, not only a
photo. Missing portfolios use a fresh paper-tower evidence task below, labeled
today rather than invented growth. Per pupil: portfolio, two paper sheets
(caption/evidence and demonstration), pencil. Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) share ruler.
Teacher board/timer, prompt written large. All share at desks, no four-device
station rotation, food or awards requiring extra time.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Portfolios; pencils (1/pupil each) | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Paper (2/pupil) | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| Rulers (1/team) | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Artifact = saved work; evidence = recorded result; caption = explanatory label;
reflection = reasoned look back. **Misconception:** attendance proves skill.
**If asked "Am I a master coder?"** Only observed individual operation supports
that skill; a paper trace is a different kind of evidence.
Dry paper displays; no restarted robots/circuits, small prizes, balloons or
private pupil photos. Report missing/broken supplies. Indoor primary.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Optional prayer of thanks; "Which work shows a decision you changed?"
2. **4-9:** Model caption: task, real result, reason, limit; distinguish measured
   from predicted and actually operated from watched.
3. **9-19:** Select artifact and caption it. Each independently re-explains a
   test or code trace. Missing artifact: fold a standing paper tower, measure cm
   and ten-second standing, then change base/retest, recording today's evidence.
4. **19-29:** Parallel team round: each has two minutes to explain/demonstrate;
   peers ask "What evidence supports that?" Give one specific caption suggestion.
5. **29-35:** Each revises caption and submits artifact reference plus fresh
   explanation/limit and material-care choice; teacher checks individual slips.
6. **35-40:** Date/store portfolios, count rulers, collect captions and clear
   desks. Gratitude may be private; enthusiasm not scored.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: identifiable evidence, fresh reasoning demonstration, accurate limitation,
caption revision and responsible return. Grade 3/support: point/dictate; two
caption stems. Grade 4/challenge: compare two dated artifacts and identify what
cannot be inferred. Unsupported caption: change "proved" to evidence-supported
claim, never fabricate data. No earlier artifact: today's demonstration only,
not year-growth certification. Early finish: revise for a new reader.

**Family:** We celebrated learning with evidence and revised captions. Ask,
"Which result supports your explanation?" Optional: listen to the explanation.

## Teacher artifact pack

- **Student material — caption-and-demonstration sheet (print/no-print):** `Name ___  Date ___`; `Artifact title/date or “today's paper tower” ___`; `The task was ___`; `Actual evidence on the artifact: ___`; `Fresh demonstration/trace I completed today: ___`; `My claim: ___`; `My evidence supports the claim because ___`; `A limit—this evidence does not show ___`; `Peer question or suggestion (exact words): ___`; `Original caption: ___`; `Revised caption—underline the change: ___`; `I returned/cared for materials by ___.` No-print: display the exact fields; pupils use one sheet for the caption and the second for the fresh demonstration.
- **Expected evidence and acceptable answers:** Valid artifact evidence includes a dated measurement, observed load/result, before/after test, or saved code trace; `I liked it` and attendance are not evidence. The fresh demonstration may be a truthful reconstruction of reasoning, a sequence trace, or the paper-tower measurement/retest. Accept limitations such as `one test does not prove every design`, `a paper trace does not show I operated a device`, `the 20-counter cap did not find a maximum`, or `today's tower does not prove growth across the term`. A revision must respond to peer feedback by adding specificity, correcting overclaim, or clarifying the result.
- **Worked finished example:** Artifact: Week 10 bridge record. Original caption: `My bridge was the strongest.` Evidence: `flat held 4; folded held 12 at the same 15 cm span`. Fresh explanation: pupil redraws center loading and computes `8 more`. Peer asks, `Strongest compared with what?` Revised caption: `In our two tests, my folded paper bridge held 12 counters, 8 more than my flat version; we stopped at 20, so this does not prove a maximum.` Annotate the numbers `actual evidence`, `in our two tests` as claim boundary, and the final clause as limitation.
- **Visual example and Board setup:** Make a four-box caption frame: `TASK | RESULT | WHY | LIMIT`. Beside it, show `Predicted 10` crossed out as evidence and `Observed 8` accepted. Post discussion stems: `Where is that recorded?`, `What can you conclude?`, `What can you not conclude?`, and `I suggest changing ___ so a new reader understands ___.` Set a clearly labeled `RETURNED PORTFOLIOS` area and a separate `TODAY'S TOWER` station with ruler.
- **Discussion questions, answers, and misconceptions:** `Why is a favorite artifact not automatically the best evidence?` (`preference and evidence answer different questions`); `What makes a claim too broad?` (`it says more than the recorded result supports`); `Why demonstrate reasoning again?` (`to show current understanding rather than relying only on saved work`); `Can watching a teammate operate code prove your operation?` (`no`); `How does careful return of materials relate to responsible gifts?` (`shared resources remain available to others`). Correct `celebration means everyone mastered every domain` with the lesson's evidence limits, and correct `a missing portfolio means no learning` by using today's task without inventing past growth.
- **Kit contents. Kit label: and Cleanup/Reset:** Per pupil packet: `returned portfolio; 2 plain sheets; 1 pencil`. Per team: `1 metric ruler`. Label the ruler bin `C-STREAM G3-4 | W13 PORTFOLIO SHARE | RULERS` and the collection trays `W13 CAPTIONS` and `W13 RETURNED PORTFOLIOS`. Reset by counting rulers, pairing each dated caption/demonstration with the correct portfolio using the school's existing identifier, removing loose blank paper, and recording missing/damaged artifacts without reconstructing or discarding them.
- **Unfinished work:** Check one next action on the caption sheet: `select evidence`, `complete fresh demonstration`, `receive peer question`, `revise caption`, or `file portfolio`. Store all components together in `W13 UNFINISHED` under the school's normal classroom identifier. Provide a brief school-time conference; do not require family access to portfolios or claim completion based on an unrevised caption.
- **Annotated exemplar, Common error interpretation, and Reteach:** If a caption says `I predicted 20, so it held 20`, label `prediction substituted for result` and return to the dated record. If a pupil accurately states a result but gives no limit, mark `evidence present; boundary missing`, not wholly incorrect. If the fresh paper tower falls before 10 seconds, record the seconds/height actually observed, widen or fold the base, and retest; falling is evidence. Reteach by color-coding one sample sentence: claim, recorded number, reason, limit, then have the pupil revise one overclaim orally before writing.
- **Safety classification: Severity low; likelihood unlikely** after controls. Paper, portfolios, and rulers present possible low-severity risks (paper cuts, clutter, ruler misuse). Clear desk lanes, seated sharing, rulers used only for measurement, and no legacy devices/circuits reduce residual risk to **low / unlikely**. **Adult ratio: 1:25 maximum** for the seated portfolio share, with active monitoring during two-minute explanations and closer support during the paper-tower fallback as needed. No chemical product is used and **SDS is not applicable**.
- **Print/accessibility check — black-and-white:** Use at least 12-point high-contrast text, wide caption lines, and labeled boxes that remain distinct in grayscale. Offer a full-page large-print version, oral presentation, pointing to saved evidence, speech-to-scribe, or a teacher-read peer suggestion; do not grade eye contact, handwriting, or enthusiasm. Test one monochrome copy and ensure `claim/evidence/limit` labels are not color-dependent. No technology artifact applies.
