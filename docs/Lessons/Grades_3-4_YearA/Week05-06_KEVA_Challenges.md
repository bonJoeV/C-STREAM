# Weeks 5-6: KEVA Engineering Challenges

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly A; Structures; **two 40-minute meetings**.
**Objective:** measure/retest a tower and load-test/retest a plank bridge.
**Why/big idea:** limits make comparisons fair and resources manageable.
Catholic/CST-C3: count back stock and justify reuse; CST-E1: criterion/constraint;
CST-E2: test/change/retest; CST-M1: cm/count comparisons.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; developing,
no prior architecture assumed. Prep 20 minutes with stock; loan acquisition
extra. Cleanup 5 per meeting.

## Before class and exact supplies

Balanced teams <=3: 4/5/7/9. Per team: 30 inspected KEVA planks, ruler, two flat
books, ten identical plastic counters, tray. Per pupil: two unit recording
sheets/pencil. Teacher board/timer; demo uses issued kit. Pretest an overlapping
deck across **8 cm**; mark a 20 cm square for towers, **40 cm height cap**.
Save records, dismantle between meetings. Identical rectangular classroom blocks
may replace KEVA for all teams, labeling the substitution.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Foundation = base; span = gap; load = test objects; constraint = limit;
criterion = performance requirement. Wide bases/overlap may help, not guarantee
stability; loose unjoined triangles are not rigid trusses.
**Misconception:** tallest is best. **If asked “More weight?”** Fixed cap permits
comparison, not a dangerous maximum. Desk tests only; no climbing/throwing/
heavy loads/hands below bridges/forced collapses. Stop/report damaged stock.
Indoor primary. Religious building imagery is reflection, not structural proof.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** “How can a tower use stock responsibly?” Explain 30-plank limit,
   40 cm cap and ten-second unassisted standing criterion.
2. **4-9:** Model base, ruler zero and hands-off test.
3. **9-20:** Each sketches; build tower, rotate builder/measurer/recorder.
4. **20-30:** Record height/time; change base arrangement, rebuild with same
   allowance, retest. Failed standing trial is evidence.
5. **30-35:** Each child records two heights/pass results, reason for change and reuse
   choice. “Which observation informed the new base?”
6. **35-40:** Dismantle top-down; count 30 planks, return tools, date records.

## Meeting 2: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Bridge as access model, not for people; 8 cm gap/30 planks/ten counters.
2. **4-9:** Model overlapping deck; add center counters singly, three-second
   hold; record last successful count, or “at least ten.”
3. **9-20:** Sketch/build version 1; check book gap/supports.
4. **20-30:** Test, change overlap arrangement, retest with equal span/allowance/
   placement. “Where did movement begin?”
5. **30-35:** Each child gives two counts, whole-number difference, kept-same condition
   and responsible stock choice; no ratios.
6. **35-40:** Remove loads, dismantle/count/store, collect dated records.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets each meeting: criterion/constraint, two measured results, test-backed
change and count-back (C3/E1/E2/M1). Grade 3/support: starter base, oral evidence,
counting piles of five. Grade 4/challenge: repeat same capped test and explain
why “at least ten” is not a maximum. Immediate fall: record zero/widen base.
All hold ten: compare arrangement not unsupported strength rankings.
Missing stock: equal lower allowance for everyone and stated revised limit;
no home purchase. At 25, nine simultaneous desk tests avoid serial queues.

**Family:** We retested structures and stewarded shared stock. Ask, “Which test
informed your change?” Optional: notice a support in a picture.
