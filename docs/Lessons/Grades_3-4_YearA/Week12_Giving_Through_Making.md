# Week 12: Giving Through Making

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly A; Service design; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** design a page marker for a reader, test twice and revise one
feature. **Why/big idea:** a gift serves a stated need rather than its price.
Catholic/CST-C2: listen to recipient; CST-E1: function/size criteria;
CST-E2: actual before/after tests; CST-A2: visual choice supports use.
Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Technology **None**;
introductory. Prep 10 minutes; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class and exact supplies

Adult cuts two **5 x 15 cm** cardstock blanks per pupil; one is a revision spare.
Each pupil gets one paper evidence sheet/pencil. Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9) share one
closed classroom book, two markers and blunt scissors. Check book edges/cards;
demo from issued blank. Teacher board/timer; no lamination/gift templates.
Book/tools reusable; card consumable; save evidence.

| Allocation | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Card blanks (2/pupil) | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| Paper; pencils (1/pupil each) | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Books; scissors (1/team each) | 4 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Markers (2/team) | 8 | 10 | 14 | 18 |

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

User = reader; function = job; prototype = trial; contrast = visible difference.
Criterion: protrudes above closed book by at least 2 cm and permits finding
the marked page twice. **Misconception:** beauty alone proves function.
**If asked “Does it have to cost money?”** Thoughtful time/attention can serve.
Catholic generosity is application, not a claim that this gift guarantees joy.
No blades, laminator, string tassels, food or private photos. Seated scissors;
stop/report damaged edges. Indoor primary; no recipient delivery without adult
consent, and paper gift only.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Optional prayer for generous work. “What does a reader need?”
2. **4-9:** Model placement/protrusion/two retrieval tests; ask a peer which
   symbol would help. Fictional recipient allowed.
3. **9-21:** Each makes marker with visible top symbol and brief encouraging
   original message; explain color/form choice to peer.
4. **21-30:** Test locating page twice; record results, revise top/placement or
   contrast and retest twice. Do not glue anything into book.
5. **30-35:** Each child shows need, criterion, before/after record and visual choice.
6. **35-40:** Cap/count tools, return books, collect dated evidence; approved
   paper gifts may go home, no compulsory recipient response.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: stated user need, two versions actually tested, criterion checked and
purposeful visual explanation (C2/E1/E2/A2). Grade 3/support: pre-cut blank,
picture message, scribing. Grade 4/challenge: compare readability without relying
only on color. Marker buried: adjust placement/shape, not thicker book damage.
No book: folded paper booklet for every team, label model test. Early finish:
try another book and explain limits. Missing feedback recorded, not invented.

**Family:** We made functional gifts and tested a page marker. Ask, “What
changed after the test?” Optional: discuss what makes a thoughtful gift.

## Teacher artifact pack

- **Student material — user-test sheet (one page, printable/no-print):** `Maker ___  Date ___`; `My reader needs a marker that ___`; sketch `Version 1` and label the top symbol/message; `Closed-book protrusion: ___ cm`; `Page-find test 1: found / not yet`; `Page-find test 2: found / not yet`; `Reader feedback: ___`; `I changed the top shape / placement / contrast / message by ___`; sketch `Version 2`; `Version 2 test 1: found / not yet`; `Version 2 test 2: found / not yet`; `The revision helped/did not help because ___`; `My visual choice serves the reader by ___.` No-print: put these exact prompts on the board; pupils use front/back of plain paper.
- **Expected evidence and acceptable answers:** The need may be `find the saved page`, `see the marker quickly`, `read an encouraging message`, or another non-private reader need. The marker meets the functional criterion only when at least 2 cm shows above the closed book and the marked page is found in both trials. Accept a revision that enlarges the top, raises placement, strengthens light/dark contrast, simplifies the symbol, or clarifies the brief original message. A beautiful design that remains buried, a single retrieval, or feedback invented for an absent reader is `not yet evidenced`.
- **Worked finished example:** Version 1 has a pale yellow 1 cm star at the top, protrudes 1 cm, and the partner misses the page twice. Feedback: `I could not see the top.` Version 2 has a 3 cm black-outlined star placed higher; 2.5 cm protrudes, and the partner finds the page twice. Message: `Your reading grows one page at a time.` Finished explanation: `The dark outline and higher star made the marker easier to see.` Annotate the first result `criterion not met`, circle `2.5 cm`, and bracket the two successful tests as evidence rather than praising decoration alone.
- **Board setup and Visual example:** Show a life-size 5 x 15 cm rectangle beside a ruler, a closed-book side sketch with a `>= 2 cm` arrow, and a results table: `version | protrusion | find 1 | find 2 | revision`. Display one high-contrast and one low-contrast symbol in grayscale. Post the design cycle `listen -> make -> test twice -> revise -> retest twice`.
- **Discussion prompts, answers, and misconceptions:** `Who is the user?` (`the reader, real or fictional`); `Why test twice?` (`one success may be chance and the criterion requires repeat use`); `Does a more decorated marker work better?` (`not necessarily; function and readability need evidence`); `What can change without damaging the book?` (`marker top, placement, contrast, symbol, or message`); `Can a thoughtful gift be inexpensive?` (`yes; time, listening, and useful design can serve`). Correct `favorite color means good contrast` by comparing visible light/dark difference, and `thicker is better` by checking that the page and book remain undamaged.
- **Kit contents. Kit label: and Cleanup/Reset:** Team kit: `1 closed classroom book; 1 blunt scissors; 2 washable markers`. Per pupil: `two adult-cut 5 x 15 cm cardstock blanks; 1 evidence sheet; 1 pencil`. Label team bins `C-STREAM G3-4 | W12 PAGE MARKER | BOOK + SCISSORS + 2 MARKERS` and the blank envelope `W12 | 5 x 15 cm BLANKS | 2 PER PUPIL`. Cleanup and reset require checking book edges/pages, capping and counting two markers, closing scissors, sorting clean unused blanks from scraps, and returning completed markers/evidence separately so ink does not transfer to books.
- **Unfinished work:** Write one status on the sheet: `need stated`, `V1 made`, `V1 tested twice`, `revision planned`, or `V2 retest complete`. Store the marker flat with its dated sheet in `W12 UNFINISHED`; do not leave it inside a book or require gift delivery/home completion. Resume with the next unchecked test step using the same classroom criterion.
- **Annotated exemplar, Common error interpretation, and Reteach:** If a pupil reports `found/found` but measured 1 cm, annotate `retrieval succeeded; size criterion not met` and revise placement before retesting. If 3 cm protrudes but peers cannot identify the top, annotate `function partly met; visibility needs revision`. If the book is opened or shaken during a test, label the trial `method changed—repeat`, not a design failure. Reteach by comparing two teacher markers, measuring protrusion together, then conducting two silent page-find trials and choosing one evidence-based change.
- **Safety classification: Severity low; likelihood unlikely** after controls. Minor scissor nicks, paper cuts, marker contact, or damaged book edges remain possible before controls. Seated blunt-scissor use, inspected materials, capped markers, and no cutting while walking reduce residual risk to **low / unlikely**. **Adult ratio: 1:25 maximum** for seated use of blunt scissors, with active scanning and direct 1:1 cutting assistance when required by a pupil's motor/safety plan. The used commercial product category is classroom markers. Enter the exact manufacturer and product in the [Safety Data Sheet Register](../../Review/Safety_Data_Sheet_Register.csv), and keep it on **HOLD** until school approval and SDS applicability are resolved.
- **Print/accessibility:** Use 12-point minimum type, black outlines, words plus checkboxes (not color-only results), and generous drawing/measurement space. Offer a large-print sheet, high-contrast marker choices, tactile/raised top shapes made only from the provided cardstock, pre-cut blanks, adapted scissors, oral feedback, dictation, or scribing. Print one grayscale sample at 100% and use a ruler to confirm any depicted 5 x 15 cm template has not been scaled. No technology artifact applies.
