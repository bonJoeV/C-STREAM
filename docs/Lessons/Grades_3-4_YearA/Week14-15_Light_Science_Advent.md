# Weeks 14-15: Light Science & Advent

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly A; Light; **two 40-minute meetings**.
**Objective:** record reflected/refracted light observations and revise a
transmitting Advent composition after a same-distance test. **Why:** optics
helps explain tools/windows; purposeful art communicates hope.
Catholic/CST-C1: distinguish measured light from religious symbolism;
CST-S1: observations; CST-S3: light-path model; CST-A2: purposeful composition;
CST-A3: critique/revision. Official alignment **VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.
Technology **None**, but physical flashlights required; introductory.
Prep 15 minutes plus adult battery check; cleanup 5 per meeting.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9). Each kit: enclosed flashlight, acrylic mirror, uncut clear
plastic cup, tray, three tissue squares, cardstock sheet, glue stick.
Per pupil: two recording sheets/pencil. Teacher board/timer and **50 mL water
per team**, poured by adult only in meeting 1; demo uses issued kit.
Class: one graduated plastic pouring cup and one roll of towels for drying/spills.
Check secured batteries/no cracked mirrors; pretest mirror aiming onto desk
paper and pencil appearance through water. Adult cuts a window in cardstock
before meeting 2; keep room normally lit. Dry kits before storage.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Reflection = light changes direction at a surface; refraction = change of
direction between materials; transmission = passage through; composition =
arrangement conveying meaning. **Misconception:** pencil is actually bent.
**If asked "Is spiritual light measurable?"** No; Advent light is a religious
symbol, not a lux reading. No flames, glass, lasers, sun-viewing, loose cells or
fully dark rotations. Beam onto desks, never eyes. Adult handles batteries/
water; stop/dry spills and report damaged kit. Indoor flashlight tests do not
depend on daylight. No unverified liturgical/saint claims needed.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "How can light change direction?" Optional prayer for careful inquiry.
2. **4-10:** Model desk mirror target, water-pencil observation and safe beam.
3. **10-22:** Every pupil aims mirror once and draws source/mirror/target arrows.
   Adult pours water; each draws pencil dry then viewed through water.
4. **22-30:** Repeat from a second viewing angle; compare appearance and explain
   that a picture models light, not a real bent pencil.
5. **30-35:** Individual reflection/refraction observations and symbol-versus-
   measurement distinction; "Which evidence showed a change?"
6. **35-40:** Adult empties cups; dry/count/store, collect dated sheets.

## Meeting 2: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** Review observations; Advent preparation/hope is the faith application.
2. **4-9:** Show tissue/window frame; flashlight always 20 cm behind frame.
3. **9-21:** Design three-panel composition using tissue; each pupil explains
   one placement/color choice communicating hope, not just decoration.
4. **21-30:** Peer identifies intended shape at 1 m; record correct/not yet,
   revise one overlap or outline and retest at same light/view distance.
5. **30-35:** Each draws path through tissue, records two results and explains
   critique-backed visual choice and religious/scientific distinction.
6. **35-40:** Switch off/count equipment, save paper art, cap glue/clear desks.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: two accurate optical observations/path, explicit symbolic distinction and
test-backed visual revision. Grade 3/support: trace arrows, oral explanation,
precut window. Grade 4/challenge: explain overlapping layers/readability tradeoff.
No visible reflection: reset angle onto closer paper, not eyes. No water:
reflection/transmission only; refraction not observed. Missing flashlight:
share between neighboring teams within work windows; never infer operation
from watching. No home hardware.

**Family:** We investigated light and revised Advent art. Ask, "Which part was
science and which was a symbol?" Optional: notice a safe light source.
