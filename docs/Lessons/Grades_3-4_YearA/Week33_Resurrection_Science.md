# Week 33: Resurrection & New Life Science

## Lesson at a glance

Grades 3-4; Weekly A; Life science; **one 40-minute meeting**.
**Objective:** observe a seed, order four living butterfly stages and explain why
development is not biological Resurrection. **Why:** accurate biology and
religious symbolism can coexist without confusing evidence.
CST-C1 observation/faith distinction; CST-S1 seed detail; CST-S3 living stages/
generation link; CST-A1 labeled diagram. Official alignment **VERIFICATION
REQUIRED**. Technology **None**; introductory. Prep 10; cleanup 5 included.

## Before class and exact supplies

Teams <=3 (4/5/7/9). Each: four large drawn cards egg/larva/pupa/adult, two
untreated dry bean seeds, plastic magnifier. Per pupil: two paper sheets/pencil.
Teacher board/timer; allergy/handling check; demo uses issued set. Copy stage
information from [Monarch Joint Venture](https://www.monarchjointventure.org/monarch-biology/life-cycle):
living immature stages develop to adults; adults reproduce, beginning another
generation. No invented timing, live specimens, planting or hidden caretaker.

## Vocabulary/background/SAFETY

Embryo = young plant in viable seed; dormancy = reduced activity; metamorphosis
= developmental change; reproduction = new generation.
**Misconception:** pupa/seed dies to transform. Viable embryos/pupae are alive;
dead embryos do not germinate. **If asked "Does every seed grow?"** No; viability
and conditions matter, and appearance alone does not test viability.
Easter celebrates Jesus' Resurrection; growth is a metaphor of hope, not proof.
No tasting, broken seeds, animal handling/releases or outdoor collection.
Count seeds away from mouthing pupils; wash hands, report loss/allergy concern.
Photo/drawn-card seed observation substitutes if allergy gate fails, labeled.

## Meeting 1: exactly 40 minutes

1. **0-4:** "Can something alive look still?" Optional prayer of Easter hope.
2. **4-10:** Model observed seed surface versus inferred embryo; read four stages.
3. **10-22:** Each observes seed with lens, draws two visible details, then
   orders stage cards and explains living pupa.
4. **22-30:** Draw arrows through stages and adult-to-new-eggs reproduction.
   Peer checks labels; revise one unclear arrow/detail.
5. **30-35:** Each identifies pupa/embryo as living, generation link, actual
   observed detail and separate Easter metaphor. "What did we not test?"
6. **35-40:** Count seeds/cards/lenses, file dated diagrams, wash/clear.

## Success/access/troubleshooting

Meets: visible detail, ordered living stages/generation link, labeled visual
revision and science/faith distinction. Grade 3/support: pictured stages,
dictation. Grade 4/challenge: explain observation cannot prove a seed viable.
Wrong order: compare source card and revise; never call a pupa dead. Missing
lenses: individual turns with shared lens or unaided observation. Early finish:
identify what longer-term evidence a growth investigation would need.
Indoor primary works without spring specimens.

**Family:** We studied living development and Easter symbolism. Ask, "Why is
a pupa not dead?" Optional: notice a plant picture, no planting homework.

## Teacher artifact pack

- **Student material — observation and sequence sheet (print/no-print):** Page 1 fields are
  `name/date`, a 10 cm drawing circle headed `Dry bean seed--outside only`, two
  leader lines labeled `visible detail 1 ___` and `visible detail 2 ___`, and
  the prompts `I directly observed ___`; `I infer but did not test ___`; `Can
  appearance alone tell whether this seed is viable? yes/no, because ___`.
  Page 2 has four boxes to draw or place labels, connected by arrows, with exact
  fields `1 ___`, `2 ___`, `3 ___`, `4 ___`, `adult -> new ___ begins another
  generation`, `The pupa is alive because ___`, and `Development is a useful
  Easter metaphor, but it is not biological Resurrection because ___`. With no
  printer, pupils use two plain sheets, trace a teacher-drawn observation circle
  and four-box arrow loop, and copy every prompt.
- **Expected evidence and acceptable answers:** Seed evidence must name two
  features actually visible on the intact seed, such as color/pattern, curved
  outline, smooth/rough surface, seam, or small scar; do not accept an unseen
  embryo as an observation. Accept the inference `an embryo may be inside`, but
  require `we did not open, plant, or test it`. Correct butterfly order is egg,
  larva/caterpillar, pupa/chrysalis, adult/butterfly, followed by adult
  reproduction/new eggs. Accept `it is a living developmental stage` or `its
  body is changing inside` for the pupa, not `it died and came back`. The
  science/faith distinction must indicate that metamorphosis is normal living
  development while Jesus' Resurrection is a religious event and is not proved
  by the seed or butterfly evidence.
- **Worked and finished example:** Display a drawing based on a separate teacher
  seed: two arrows labeled `dark curved line` and `small pale oval scar`.
  Example text: `I directly observed a smooth brown surface and a pale scar. I
  infer an embryo may be inside, but we did not open or grow this seed, so I
  cannot tell from appearance alone whether it is viable.` The completed cycle
  reads `egg -> larva -> pupa -> adult -> new eggs`. Finish: `The pupa remains
  alive while it develops. This change can remind Christians of hope and new
  life, but it is not Jesus dying and rising and does not scientifically prove
  Resurrection.` Annotate `observation`, `inference`, `living stages`,
  `reproduction link`, and `metaphor/biology boundary`.
- **Visual/board setup:** Divide the board into `WHAT WE CAN SEE`, `WHAT WE
  INFER`, and `WHAT WE DID NOT TEST`; place `seed coat pattern`, `possible
  embryo`, and `viability/germination` respectively. Below, mount or draw the
  four stage cards in a horizontal arrow sequence with a return arrow from adult
  to new egg labeled `reproduction: another generation`; never use a tombstone,
  death symbol, or arrow labeled `resurrection` in the biology sequence. Post a
  sentence frame: `___ is like ___ in the limited sense that ___; it is not the
  same because ___`.
- **Discussion questions, answers and misconceptions:** Ask `Can something alive
  look still?` (yes; dormancy or an immobile developmental stage does not by
  itself mean death); `What may we claim from the seed surface?` (only visible
  features, not viability); `Does every seed grow?` (no, viability and suitable
  conditions matter); `What follows the adult stage?` (reproduction and new
  eggs, not the same butterfly becoming an egg); `Is a pupa dead?` (no, it is a
  living stage of the same organism's development); and `Why is metamorphosis
  not proof or a biological model of Resurrection?` (it is an observable
  natural life process, while Resurrection is a distinct faith claim). If a
  pupil says `the butterfly is born again`, replace the phrase with `develops
  through living stages` and have the pupil trace the generation arrow.
- **Kit contents. Kit label: and Cleanup/Reset:** Each team bag contains exactly `4 large stage cards:
  egg, larva, pupa, adult; 2 untreated dry bean seeds; 1 plastic magnifier`.
  Label it `W33 NEW LIFE | TEAM CODE | 4 STAGE CARDS / 2 SEEDS / 1 MAGNIFIER |
  NO TASTING`. Store bags in a bin labeled `C-STREAM G3-4 YRA W33 | 4/5/7/9
  TEAM BAGS`; keep pupil paper/pencils outside. Reset by matching all four cards
  to the teacher picture key, counting two intact seeds without cracking them,
  wiping magnifier handles with the school-approved routine and allowing them
  to dry, recording any lost seed, and sealing each bag. Return observed seeds
  to the same team bag; do not plant, eat, or send them home.
- **Unfinished work:** Circle the next required element on the pupil sheet:
  `second visible detail`, `four-stage order`, `adult-to-egg arrow`, or
  `metaphor distinction`. File both dated pages together in the W33 class
  envelope. A pupil with handling restrictions completes the missing observation
  from the labeled teacher drawing/photo during a supervised catch-up; stage
  cards and seeds stay at school.
- **Annotated exemplar, Common error interpretation, and Reteach:** On the exemplar, underline
  only visible seed words, put a dotted box around the embryo inference, number
  the four stages, circle the adult-to-egg arrow, and double-underline `not the
  same`. An embryo drawn as directly seen signals observation/inference
  confusion; a pupa crossed out as dead signals a living-stage misconception;
  no return arrow signals a missing generation concept. Reteach by sorting just
  `egg/larva` and `pupa/adult`, merging the pairs into one sequence, then adding
  one differently colored teacher arrow for adult reproduction. Use an intact
  seed or substitute image to practice `I see` versus `I infer` before the pupil
  revises the exact incorrect label.
- **Safety classification: Severity moderate; likelihood unlikely** after the
  allergy/handling gate and substitution. Ordinary classroom risks are low
  severity; a known seed/legume allergy or mouthing risk could have a moderate
  consequence.
  **Adult ratio: 1:15 maximum** during seed issue, observation, and return; for
  16-25 pupils, add a second school-approved adult or place affected pupils on
  the no-contact image task before seeds are distributed. Provide direct 1:1
  handling prevention where an individual plan requires it.
  No tasting, seed breaking, animal contact, or release; wash hands and stop for
  allergy symptoms or a missing seed under school procedure. **SDS
  applicability: not applicable** to untreated dry seeds, paper cards, and
  plastic magnifiers; if a school disinfectant is used during adult reset, its
  existing site SDS and label govern adult use and it is not a pupil material.
- **Print accessibility:** Print black and white at 100% with at least 12-point
  type, thick directional arrows, written stage names in addition to pictures,
  no color-only sequence cues, and uncluttered leader lines. Offer 16-18-point
  sheets, raised/tactile stage order cards, magnifier turns, a teacher photo
  substitute, scribing, dictation, or pointing. Read `pupa/chrysalis`,
  `viable`, and the metaphor prompt aloud. Confirm on one sample that the return
  arrow, all four box numbers, and pale-detail leader lines remain visible.
- **Technology compliance:** No technology artifact applies.
