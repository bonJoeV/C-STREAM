# Weeks 29-31: Growing Things

## Before class
1. Check school seed/potting-mix allergy guidance. Select **one** untreated bean or radish variety; read packet depth/water guidance. No fertilizer, pesticides or edible harvest in class.
2. Prestart two teacher backup pots 7-10 days earlier. Draw three dated backup observations if live growing conditions are unavailable; clearly identify teacher/picture data.
3. Teacher makes drainage holes before class. Put pots in leakproof trays by a safe bright indoor window, not on a freezing sill/heater. No electrical grow light required.
4. Mark a height baseline at the soil surface. Assign a named adult to check moisture on school days and before weekends; water when needed, not automatically daily. Keep care at school, including school breaks or arrange approved adult care.
5. Make a three-column record: date / drawing / height. Leave "no shoot yet" distinct from a missing measurement.

## Vocabulary / teacher background
Seed = contains a young plant and stored food; germinate = begin growing; seedling = young plant; nutrient = substance needed for growth; data = recorded observations.
Many seeds need water, oxygen and suitable temperature to germinate. Seedlings need light for making food, plus water/air/nutrients and support. Soil is a useful growing medium, **not plant food** or a universal requirement; plants can grow without soil under appropriate conditions. This is a longitudinal observation, not a controlled experiment proving needs.
**Misconception:** an unsprouted seed means its caretaker was bad or lacked faith.
**If asked "Why did mine not grow?"** Seed viability, temperature, water and other factors may matter; our records do not isolate the cause.

## Meeting 1: plant and predict (30 minutes)
1. **0-4:** Show seed/seedling; state objective and care boundary. Optional original prayer asks for careful stewardship.
2. **4-8:** Explain seed food versus seedling needs; demonstrate packet-based planting depth and soil-surface ruler baseline.
3. **8-18:** Teams scoop mix, plant two seeds, lightly water according to packet and label pot/date. Each child draws what is visible and predicts a future change orally.
4. **18-22:** Make a care log and place pots in trays; choose an adult checker. Explain that not every seed will sprout.
5. **22-25:** Individual evidence: point to seed/seedling, name one care action and one observation.
6. **25-30:** Cleanup/handwashing.

## Meeting 2: observe and measure (30 minutes)
1. **0-4:** Retrieve pots; compare to first dated drawing, not another team's tallest plant.
2. **4-8:** Teacher models height from soil surface to top of shoot, same tool each time. Grade 1 may use equal cubes; Grade 2 nearest whole cm. Do not include pot height.
3. **8-19:** Each child measures or points to the baseline and records one observation; record "no visible shoot" truthfully. Compare own two dates. Teacher checks individuals while partners work.
4. **19-23:** Inspect moisture with teacher and update care log; discuss what we know versus what we cannot yet explain.
5. **23-25:** Optional Sower paraphrase (Matthew 13:3-9) and care reflection: plant growth is not a score for anyone's faith.
6. **25-30:** Cleanup/handwashing.

## Meeting 3: communicate changes (30 minutes)
1. **0-4:** Recall measurement baseline and previous record.
2. **4-13:** Final measurement/drawing using same units; each student compares two dated observations.
3. **13-19:** Display one current height per team on class chart; separate "no shoot" from "not recorded." Grade 2 explains why different heights do not identify one cause.
4. **19-23:** Pairs present change/no change and a question. Keep plants at school unless a family **chooses** a free take-home plant; provide teacher care instead where needed.
5. **23-25:** Individual exit: show two dated observations and one responsible care decision.
6. **25-30:** Cleanup/handwashing.

## Questions / success
What changed since that date? Where does the ruler start? What if a seed does not sprout? Does our evidence prove why plants differ?
- **Grade 1:** two dated drawings, correct baseline demonstration, longer/shorter/same comparison and one care action, all oral/drawn.
- **Grade 2:** at least two dated measures in the same units and explanation of change; distinguish observation from an untested cause.
- Score **not yet / prompted / independent** for observation, measurement, comparison and care; never grade germination success or home participation.

## If things go wrong / indoor fallback
No sprouts: retain genuine record and measure backup plant; do not relabel teacher data as student's. Mold: teacher removes affected pot sealed, cleans tray per school policy, supplies pictures. No bright window/no live growing allowed: use the three dated teacher drawings and equal-length paper plant models; assess reading/comparing observations, **not live plant care mastery**. Indoor work remains available all Minnesota winter; no outdoor planting required.

## SAFETY / support / challenge
No tasting seeds/plants/mix, unknown soil, spraying faces, standing water near electronics, student puncturing or glass pots. Wash hands. Teacher handles spill/mold and packet safety.
Support: prefilled pots, picture steps, accessible table, adult scribing, thick equal blocks; nonreaders point/draw/tell. Challenge: Grade 2 use differences between two heights or propose a future fair test without claiming this one proves it.

## Cleanup / family / sources
Return tools, wipe trays/tables, put labeled pots safely back, wash hands and store records; adult checks care outside meetings.
**Family snippet:** We kept dated plant records and compared change honestly, including no growth. Our Catholic connection was stewardship. Ask: "What did your evidence show, and what are you still wondering?" Optional: notice a plant or picture at home; no seed purchase, watering homework or device required.
[CCC 2415](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P8B.HTM); optional Matthew 13 and 1 Corinthians 3:6 **references** use school-approved Bible (wording VERIFICATION REQUIRED). Seed-specific timing/depth must be verified against the selected packet; no universal sprouting deadline is promised.
