## Assessment and family copy

Use the [assessment template](./Assessment_Template.md) and
[student project rubric](../Rubrics/Student_Project_Rubric.md). Share 1-3
objective-linked criteria before launch. No universal 100-point or faith/
presentation weighting. Technical evidence and faith reasoning remain
distinguishable; neither team success nor enthusiasm proves individual mastery.

Use the [newsletter template](./Family_Newsletter_Template.md) at launch and
completion. No routine homework, home research, donation, purchased board,
family attendance or personal device required. Optional no-purchase family
conversation: "[One actual question a child can answer orally.]"

## Before reuse

Record observed prep/cleanup minutes, consumables per cohort, individual
evidence still missing, unsafe/missing parts, verified sources, objective changes
and revisions. Use [Standards Mapping](./Standards_Mapping_Template.md) to
connect only practiced standards from [Local Standards](../Review/Local_Standards.md).
