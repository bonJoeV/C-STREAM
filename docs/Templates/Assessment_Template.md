Use [Rubric Template](./Rubric_Template.md). **NE** means not yet evidenced;
absence, missed observation or equipment failure does not prove low mastery.
Ordinary access accommodations do not cap a score. Distinguish a conceptual
hint from read-aloud, translation, scribing or motor assistance.

Collect a response from every child; a group prototype is supporting evidence,
not an individual score. At 25, observe nine teams during hands-on work plus
simultaneous exit responses. Mark any unobserved child NE and arrange a brief
next-meeting check. Give one specific feedback statement and one next action.
Do not average unrelated objectives or replace learning evidence with
participation, prayer behavior, confidence or attractive displays.

**Expected-mastery checkpoints:** Follow
[Local Standards](../Review/Local_Standards.md): at least two dated observations
on separate occasions, including an individually attributable demonstration
and a changed context or follow-up explanation; both meet the grade target.
A single lesson's level 3 is that occasion's evidence, not end-of-grade mastery.
Record NE/not yet assessed when evidence is missing and schedule the next check.
