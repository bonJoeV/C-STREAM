# Objective-linked rubric template

**Grade/unit/lesson:** [ ] **Objective/code:** [ ] **Prompt:** [ ]
**Modes/support:** [Show/tell/draw/record, read-aloud/scribe/larger materials.]
Select 1-3 criteria genuinely taught. State the Meets condition first.

| Criterion linked to objective | 1 Beginning | 2 Developing | 3 Meets Expectation | 4 Extends Learning (optional) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Observable concept/action] | [Evidence reveals named misconception or missing action] | [Partial evidence; specify missing part] | [Exact success condition] | [Transfers/explains using additional evidence; not beyond-grade content by default] |

For a three-level rubric omit column 4; do not relabel NE as Beginning.
**NE: not yet evidenced** sits outside the scale.

## Completed example

O1: Compare flat and folded paper using an equal span and load.

| Criterion | 1 Beginning | 2 Developing | 3 Meets Expectation | 4 Extends Learning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence-based comparison | Names a result different from the observed test | Names the observed stronger design but cannot identify the common test condition yet | Names the observed result and shows equal span and load placement | Repeats the fair test and explains whether the second result supports the first |

A collapsed bridge can still meet this objective. Decorative skill, participation,
handwriting, family help and confident speech are not this criterion.
If the objective is a rhythmic pattern or visual composition, write a separate
arts criterion measuring that named artistic decision, not "looks beautiful."
Assess Catholic reasoning from taught content, not a child's interior faith.

**Scoring/evidence record:** use [Assessment Template](./Assessment_Template.md).
One Meets rating is evidence for that occasion. Expected-mastery checkpoints
require two dated observations on separate occasions under
[Local Standards](../Review/Local_Standards.md), not one polished project.

verification in [Standards Mapping](./Standards_Mapping_Template.md).
