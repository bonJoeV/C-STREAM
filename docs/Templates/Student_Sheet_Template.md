## Nonreader and substitute route

Use the actual [Show, Try, Tell card](../Student_Resources/Show_Try_Tell_Card.md).
The adult shows the object or picture, says one instruction, models it, then
lets each child act. Child points/builds/sorts/gestures/tells; adult records the
child's words or action. Do not hand a nonreader this text and expect independent
reading. Use a larger object or scribe without changing the conceptual criterion.
