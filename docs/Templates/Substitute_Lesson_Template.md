## Twenty minutes before class

| Prep window | Action | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | Read objective, sequence, background and individual success condition | Instructions or criterion missing |
| 4-8 | Locate/count student/team/class/teacher materials; verify 10/15/20/25 quantities | Required kit absent/short |
| 8-12 | Read safety; review accommodations and school emergency contact; inspect equipment | Unverified hazard or damage |
| 12-16 | Perform the named model/test; set out counted trays and group roles | Test fails or cannot be supervised |
| 16-20 | Draw/read aloud steps; rehearse assessment, cleanup and fallback | No feasible native-period plan |

If a stop condition occurs, tell the office/regular teacher and use the
[safe fallback](../Resources/Substitute_Teacher_Guide.md), recording changed
objectives. No repairing electrical gear, hunting personal accounts or going
outdoors in unsafe weather.

## Complete classroom plan

Attach native timed steps from the lesson, exact questions, nonreader
model/show/act directions, support/challenge, SAFETY/teacher-only tasks,
troubleshooting, cleanup and family copy. Include an individual evidence roster.
