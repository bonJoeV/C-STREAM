    Week 13: Advent Technology¶

    LESSON AT A GLANCE¶

    Field Reference
    Grade / schedule / rotation / unit 5-6 / Weekly / B / Communication
    Time 1 meeting of 45 minutes
    Objective / why / big idea I can design a readable four-panel Advent resource and justify its time/privacy choices. Technology should serve purpose, not demand attention/data.
    Domains / Catholic connection T, A, C; Advent prepares for Christ; responsible time/resources and voluntary reflection. Four activity panels are not official weekly theme assignments.
    Local standards CST-T3: intentional use/privacy; CST-A2: layout; CST-C3: time/material reason. Official benchmarks VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
    Technology Requirement Optional; paper primary preserves communication/ethics; approved slides may produce an actual digital version.
    Difficulty / entry Introductory; teach visual hierarchy and equal panels.
    Prep / cleanup Light: first 15 min, repeat 10 min; device setup extra if chosen; cleanup 4 min.

    BEFORE CLASS / MATERIALS¶

    Review contact/accommodations. Religion teacher confirms local seasonal timing; core content below needs no quotation, song or stock image. Seat tool teams ⅘/7/9. Copy four panels: Pause (quiet reflection may stay private), Notice (observe a useful classroom tool), Share (offer a turn), Care (reuse paper). Each is an optional classroom invitation, not fasting requirement or faith score. Pilot fold into four equal panels. Optional IT-pretested school slides: four slides, title/text/original drawing only, save school-only; no personal accounts.

    Item Allocation 10 15 20 25
    Paper panel sheet; evidence sheet; pencil 1 each/student 10 15 20 25
    Ruler; marker set; content sheet 1 each/team 4 5 7 9
    Computer with approved slide editor, optional 1/team 4 5 7 9
    Board; clock; roster 1 each/teacher 1 1 1 1

    Paper consumable; tools reusable; software is access, not material.

    VOCABULARY / TEACHER BACKGROUND¶

    Intentional use: use for a chosen purpose. Hierarchy: visual priority. Privacy: control of personal information. Stewardship: responsible care. Common misconception: more notifications mean more spiritual growth. If asked, "Are these official weekly Advent themes?" No; devotional resource choices, not universal liturgical assignments. Four two-minute invitations = 8 minutes, not a collected personal screen-time survey.

    EXACT LESSON SEQUENCE¶

    1. 0-4 (4 min): Ask "Could a tool support reflection without collecting it?"
    2. 4-11 (7 min): Model four panels/title/body contrast and eight-minute example.
    3. 11-25 (14 min): Each makes four-panel layout, original image and one brief invitation/panel; approved digital teams actually create/test slides if chosen.
    4. 25-35 (10 min): Peer finds Pause/Share without coaching, reads one instruction; revise visual order/text, retest and record.
    5. 35-41 (6 min): Individual layout purpose, privacy exclusion and time/resource tradeoff; name actual paper/digital route.
    6. 41-45 (4 min): Save school-only if digital, cap/count tools, save panels and tidy.

    QUESTIONS TO ASK STUDENTS / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE¶

    "What does the user need first? Why no personal journal input?" Each supplies four coherent panels, reader-backed visual revision and privacy/ time rationale. 1 unsupported; 2 prompted; 3 independent; 4 compares tool tradeoff; NE for missing; paper never called digital production.

    IF THINGS GO WRONG / SAFETY¶

    No devices -> paper route unchanged objective. Too much text -> one action per panel. Unapproved content -> use supplied invitations, no fabricated quote. No private prayers, personal usage tracking, compulsory fast, public posts, unlicensed music/images or flashing effects. Stop/report unsafe data use.

    SUPPORT / CHALLENGE / INDOOR FALLBACK¶

    Grade 5/support: predivided panels, word bank, dictation/large text. Grade 6/challenge: defend paper versus digital accessibility and time cost. Entire indoor resource lesson; no home device/activity required.

    CLEANUP / FAMILY NEWSLETTER¶

    Retain tests and actual production route, return devices/tools. Explored: intentional tools. Did: revised an Advent invitation resource. Learned: layout/privacy matter. Catholic connection: preparation and stewardship. Ask: "Why did your resource avoid collecting prayers?" Optional conversation; no routine homework.

    TEACHER ARTIFACTS¶

    Exact supplies and class-size allocation / required lesson-specific packet¶

    • Prompt/evidence: Four panels: Pause/private reflection, Notice/useful tool, Share/offer turn, Care/reuse paper. Record whether peer finds Pause/Share, revision/retest, actual paper/digital route, privacy exclusion, and 4×2=8 minutes.
    • Visual example / worked/finished example: Reader misses small PAUSE; enlarge heading/shorten action, retest found. Finished fold or four actually created approved slides contains all invitations, original image, reader evidence, and time/privacy rationale—not official weekly themes.
    • Board setup / expected discussion responses: Draw four-panel grid and TITLE>ACTION>IMAGE; ask need first/no journal input. Correct “notifications grow faith,” “digital automatically accessible,” and “paper is digital production.”
    • Kit contents / Kit label / reset / unfinished: Per team: one ruler, one marker set, and one four-panel content sheet; per student: one paper panel sheet, one evidence sheet, and one pencil; optional route: one school-managed computer with the approved slide editor. Kit label: ADVENT PANELS—W13—PAUSE/NOTICE/SHARE/CARE—PAPER/SLIDES ___—team ___. File evidence, cap/count every marker, return the ruler/content sheet, and, when used, save and close the four-slide file on the school-managed computer. Mark the missing panel or reader action; do not assert a retest.
    • Annotated exemplar / common error interpretation / reteach: Box reader result, circle hierarchy revision, star no-data choice. Private prayer field=data-minimization error; copied image=compliance error. Remodel one heading/action panel.
    • Devices/external-service compliance and deferred evidence: The optional service is the school's approved slide editor on a school-managed computer. Evidence is an actually created four-slide Pause/Notice/Share/Care file that is saved, reopened, displayed, and reader-tested. If the approved editor or computer is unavailable, defer slide creation, save/reopen, display, and digital reader-test evidence; paper panels and paper reasoning do not count as editor runtime/device evidence.

    SAFETY¶

    • Safety severity and likelihood / Adult supervision ratio / Print/accessibility check: Paper low/unlikely; computer/data moderate/possible. Adult supervision ratio: one adult/class with direct school-computer/charging oversight, teams ≤3. Print ≥12-point with high contrast and no color-only order; offer large print, predivided panels, scribe, or keyboard. Use only school-managed access/storage and original drawings; do not add tracking, public posts/links, personal accounts, unlicensed media, or flashing effects.
    • SDS/product gate: HOLD — school-approved marker. The exact marker product/manufacturer is not specified, and the Safety Data Sheet Register CONTROL-ROW does not approve a marker product. Do not issue or substitute any marker until the school safety lead approves the exact product and its current SDS.
