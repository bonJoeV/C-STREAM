    Session 05: Garden Engineers¶

    Lesson at a glance¶

    Field Teacher information
    Grade / unit / title Grades 1-2 / Growth and design / Garden Engineers
    Time / domains One 30-minute meeting, session 5 of 17 / C, S, E, A
    Big idea A garden needs light, safe space and responsible care; design has constraints.
    Student objective "I can plant a seed and show where our pots and care path fit safely."
    Why Designers consider living needs and people who care for plants. Growth will not happen during today's meeting.
    Catholic connection Stewardship of plants/resources, CCC 2415; service through a shared accessible garden.
    Standards Local CST-C3: care plan/resource use; CST-S3: seed/seedling needs; CST-E1: light/access constraints; CST-E2: test/revise layout; CST-A1: garden plan. Official benchmarks: VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
    Technology None primary path: physical pot-layout model and planting suffice.
    Difficulty / prep / cleanup Moderate; 20 minutes initial setup plus 2-3 minute adult school-day care checks; 5 minutes cleanup included.

    Before class¶

    1. Verify one untreated seed variety's packet, allergy/school potting-mix guidance and suitable bright indoor space. No outdoor planting or grow light required.
    2. Teacher makes drainage holes in plastic pots and places them in leakproof trays. Premeasure about 200 mL mix/team.
    3. Draw a 30 x 40 cm tray model with light-side mark and a 10 cm-wide care access strip at its front. Use three paper pot circles/team; do not block an exit or window route.
    4. Name an adult to check moisture on school days, weekends/break plan and watering when needed. No daily automatic watering or required home care.
    5. Prepare a prestarted seedling/photo for showing future growth. Explicitly identify it as earlier/teacher evidence.

    Supplies / scaling¶

    • Per student: half-sheet garden sketch, crayon.
    • Per team of 2-3: one pot, two seeds, 200 mL commercial potting mix, tray space, three paper pot circles and 30 x 40 cm paper mat.
    • Class: water jug, scoop, spill cloth, seedling/photo, whiteboard care log, handwashing.
    • Teacher-only: hole tool, seed packet, water distribution, care responsibility.
    Students Teams / pots Seeds plus 4 backups Mix Student sheets
    10 4 12 0.8 L 10
    15 5 14 1.0 L 15
    20 7 18 1.4 L 20
    25 9 22 1.8 L 25

    At 25 trays stay on tables; one teacher distributes water and collects pots. A small school garden can use team pots; no need for 25 individual live plants.

    Vocabulary / background¶

    Seed = young plant with stored food; seedling = growing young plant; constraint = limit; access = reaching safely; care plan = who checks needs and when. Seeds may germinate without light depending on variety; green seedlings need light to make food. Soil provides support and nutrients, not the plant's food itself; suitable soilless growth is possible. No causal experiment or same-day growth is claimed. Misconception: a plant needs exactly the same water daily. Check moisture/packet guidance. If asked "Will mine sprout by next class?" Maybe; variety, conditions and viability matter. Record what actually happens.

    Exact numbered sequence (30 minutes)¶

    1. 0-4: Show seed versus earlier seedling and explain today's design/planting objective. Optional original prayer asks for responsible care.
    2. 4-8: Model pot circles on tray mat: three fit without overlap, face light-side, keep care strip clear. Deliberately block strip then revise.
    3. 8-14: Teams arrange three circles, partner checks constraints, revise one feature. Each child sketches/points to light and access path.
    4. 14-21: Demonstrate packet-based planting depth; teams fill pot, plant two seeds, lightly water and label date/team. Each child participates safely.
    5. 21-25: Individual exit: show two seedling needs and one layout/care choice. Identify adult moisture checker and record a prediction as a prediction.
    6. 25-30: Cleanup/handwashing.

    Questions / success and later observation¶

    Where does light reach? Can the caretaker reach without knocking pots? How do we know when water is needed? Which result is still a prediction? Grade 1: oral/drawn two needs, point to clear access path and one care action. Grade 2: explain both layout constraints and one revision after checking; distinguish germination from later seedling growth. Record not yet / prompted / independent for needs, criteria, revision and care. One planted seed does not prove growth or experimental mastery. Before Session 06, the named adult adds one dated drawing/photo or height observation during normal plant care (about 2-3 minutes, not an extra C-STREAM meeting). Session 06 may spend its opening minute comparing it; if unavailable, use labeled teacher data and do not certify firsthand observation.

    If things go wrong / indoor fallback¶

    No safe growing space or allergy clearance: use empty pots/paper circles and teacher seedling pictures. Assess layout/needs, not live planting/care mastery. No germination: retain honest record and use the labeled prestarted example. Mold: adult removes sealed pot and cleans tray per policy. All work indoors even in Minnesota winter.

    SAFETY / support / challenge¶

    No seed/mix tasting, pesticides, student puncturing, glass pots, spraying faces or standing water near electronics. Wash hands, keep moisture in trays; teacher handles water/spills. Support: prefilled pot, large paper circles, visual step-by-step model, adult scribing and oral responses. Challenge: Grade 2 compare two layouts/tradeoffs while keeping both constraints.

    Cleanup / family / sources¶

    Return scoop, wipe trays/tables, move labeled pots to safe indoor location, wash hands and save sketches; adult owns care. Taking plants home is optional with an equally valid school-care option. Family snippet: We designed a light-accessible garden layout and planted seeds, but did not expect instant growth. Catholic connection: stewardship. Ask: "How can someone reach your pots safely?" Optional: notice a plant or garden picture; no seed purchase or home watering requirement. No routine homework. CCC 2415. Genesis 2:15 is an optional school-approved Bible reference; no unverified quotation. Fiacre patronage/biography and seed-specific sprouting dates are VERIFICATION REQUIRED before optional use.
