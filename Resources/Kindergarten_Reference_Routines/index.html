    Kindergarten reference routines¶

    Read this page with the selected lesson. It supplies reusable routines, not permission to omit that lesson's exact materials, test or safety instructions. Every minimum pathway works indoors, without independent reading or a device.

    Twenty-minute substitute gate¶

    1. 0-4 minutes: read the objective, background, test and individual check.
    2. 4-8: locate actual supplies; count the selected enrollment column.
    3. 8-12: inspect materials and confirm accommodations, school contact and any adult-only preparation. A supply estimate is not an owned kit.
    4. 12-16: perform the lesson's demonstration/pretest and distribute kits.
    5. 16-20: draw directions and rehearse the check and cleanup.

    If a safety condition or exact material is missing, use only the lesson's named alternative. Record the different/deferred objective; do not improvise unknown substances, electrical connections, outdoor exposure or a human trial.

    Common teacher kit: one classroom timer/clock, paper roster and school pencil or school-approved equivalent writing tool, for any enrollment. No pupil device is implied. The audit indexes this common kit separately from student supplies. Adult cutting, secured AA cells and Winter Science tongs are indexed where the selected lesson needs them. Prepared-resource prices do not include these tools or imply verified school stock.

    Teams and quantities¶

    Pupils Active pair/trio teams Kit budget Additional reserve
    10 5 pairs 5 0
    15 6 pairs + 1 trio 8 1
    20 10 pairs 10 0
    25 11 pairs + 1 trio 13 1

    Lesson tables use the kit budget, not a lone child working in a "pair." Unless a lesson explicitly adds teacher supplies, demonstrate with one budgeted kit and return it before practice. Never count a reserve twice.

    With fewer kits, use groups no larger than three: ⅘/7/9 groups respectively (2 trios + 2 pairs; 5 trios; 6 trios + 1 pair; 7 trios + 2 pairs). Recalculate the per-kit issue quantities. Preserve the test and every child's turn; do not change a learning target merely to fit a materials shortage. If individual turns cannot fit, record missing evidence and schedule follow-up.

    Nonreader directions and access¶

    Demonstrate one action, say it, let children show it, then add the next action. Use large drawings of an eye, hands, two arrows and a return box. No printout or icon-reading skill is assumed. A child can point, describe, dictate, build or direct an adult to act. Ask permission before physical assistance.

    Swap chooser/builder, tester and reporter roles during work. A third child gets a full choice and test turn, not permanent observation duty. Provide a seated route, large parts and a quieter/visual alternative as needed.

    Individual evidence without a serial exit line¶

    Prepare a roster with the lesson's two or three observable checks. Begin checking during 8-17 minutes, then finish during 17-21 while partners work. Thirteen combined minutes provide an average of about 31 seconds per child at 25 enrollment; actual processing needs may require later follow-up. Record not yet / prompted / independent separately for each check. Do not convert an unobserved response into a pass.

    Show what was tested, distinguish prediction from result, and ask one real question. Group products, compliance, handwriting and artistic polish are not mastery. A failed prototype can still produce excellent evidence. The local progression's mastery checkpoints need two dated individual observations; one lesson or a certificate cannot establish grade readiness.

    Common safety and cleanup¶

    Use teacher-inspected large paper pieces and sound blocks, seated desk tests, no student weight on models and no food/unknown nature materials. Do not taste, sniff closely, collect nests or view the Sun. No small magnets, button cells, lasers, flames, latex balloons, projectiles or mouth-contact instruments. Adults control scissors, batteries and wet setup. Keep paths/exits clear. Follow actual school allergy, hygiene, emergency and accommodation procedures.

    At 21-25 minutes, stop the test; count reusable pieces into their labeled kit; cap school-approved glue; retain dated evidence; flatten dry scrap for reuse/recycling; clear tables. Store models by kit number rather than publishing student names. Wet lessons use their own hygiene/cleanup plan.

    Catholic connection and source use¶

    Teach honest evidence, care of creation, dignity, inclusion and service. Prayer is optional according to the school's practice and is not a mastery check. Classroom applications are local, not official religious standards. Use the verified Church teaching register: CCC 159 (faith/reason), 1702/1704 (dignity/reason), 2415-2418 (creation care), 2427-2428 (work), 2468-2469 (truth). Do not attribute original classroom language to a saint or Scripture. An approved religion reading is an optional addition, not a scientific explanation.

    Continuity and family communication¶

    Keep one dated sheet per child when a lesson specifies it. Use an envelope by class/meeting; no expensive journal is required. Multi-meeting models stay at school. If prior work is unavailable, use the lesson's fresh-evidence route, never invent a past result.

    Newsletter statements describe what was actually taught and observed. Family questions are optional and ungraded. No home device, purchase, donation, collection, adult teaching or homework supplies an essential assessment.
