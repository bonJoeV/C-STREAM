    Kindergarten curriculum review¶

    Current result: all 37 Kindergarten lesson documents are complete instructional references. The autonomous second pass replaced the remaining 29 outlines, without agents, and verified their directions, timings and quantity tables. Document-complete is not classroom-certified. Physical supplies, school approvals, individual accommodations and actual trials remain required.

    Download the current 37-row audit | Teacher/substitute routines | Source-verified external candidates

    1. Baseline and change record¶

    All 37 lessons and both track READMEs were inspected in the first pass. Original baseline dispositions remain 23 KEEP + IMPROVE, 13 REBUILD, 1 REPLACE; priorities remain 22 P0, 10 P1, 5 P2. No baseline severity or status was downgraded merely because a document changed.

    Stage Rebuilt Targeted improvement only Unchanged
    First review pass 8 23 6
    After autonomous closure 37 0 0

    The 29 second-pass files are version 3.0. The original eight complete references remain in the version-2 series; ice/water teacher-tool and collection-capacity clarifications are version 2.1. Their primary material terms were normalized without claiming optional equipment is mandatory or rewriting their core objectives. The current audit preserves first-pass findings as history and states the subsequent closure separately in rationale/revision.

    A session-local copy of all 37 documents and the audit was saved before the second pass. Checks confirm the original paths, status, priority, meeting counts and native minutes are unchanged. Existing lesson URLs and the reported eight-kit school holding were preserved.

    What the autonomous pass closed¶

    • Removed conflicting legacy procedures rather than adding another override. Withdrawn high-shelf/pet/infant challenges, height races, unsafe sound options, glitter/small parts, live-release suggestions and unverified quotations are absent from the rewritten minimum pathways.
    • Supplied each reference's objective, purpose, quantities, setup, background, misconception/answer, timed sequence, individual evidence, support/challenge, safety, troubleshooting, indoor route, cleanup and family copy.
    • Implemented the six formerly audit-only checks: fall reflection, goals, school display, Thanksgiving, school-community art and weather.
    • Added two labeled weather records to the weekly practice arc and bi-weekly weather lesson; actual/teacher-sourced/model observations stay distinct.
    • Added a requested paper-book stand with two criteria and a tradeoff, not another unrestricted helper craft.
    • Added hypothetical private-information/adult-help checks in both schedules' school-community lessons. No real personal data are collected.
    • Made practice, service algorithms and privacy/community lessons CORE. Current scope is 30 CORE, 6 RECOMMENDED, 1 OPTIONAL across alternatives.
    • Normalized every K primary-material term to an explicit catalog entry; neither a name match nor a zero additional assembly price proves ownership.

    End-of-K evidence and handoff¶

    Competency Evidence sought, not automatic mastery
    Observation/inquiry Detail versus guess; question; actual result including no change
    Algorithms Predict short pictured instructions and test a correction; no software/hardware equivalence
    Data/privacy Compare two labeled observations; distinguish hypothetical private information and ask an adult
    Engineering State criterion, show a tested change and explain an outcome; identify a simple tradeoff
    Art Represent a detail/idea; identify a purposeful visual/rhythm choice and clarify communication
    Mathematics Count selected objects; compare consistent references; represent a repeat or two results
    Catholic practice Honest evidence, care of resources, respectful recipient/user choice and inclusion

    Use the local progression and individual evidence on two dated occasions where an expected-mastery checkpoint applies. Access supports are allowed. Participation, portfolios and certificates alone are not Grade 1 readiness. Missing evidence remains unobserved/not yet.

    4. Signature design and reference readiness¶

    Engineering Helpers is now a complete three-meeting reference: plan a tabletop tray, move the same two empty cups over 30 cm, record and retest one change. No real assistive device, infant/pet or high-shelf trial. In spring, Helping Hands advances to requested book-standing/picture-visibility criteria and a tradeoff.

    All 37 references are document-complete, locally conditional. No document is asserted to have passed an actual classroom/substitute trial. The older eight references' first-pass desk scores are historical editorial judgments, not measured outcomes; the second pass uses explicit instruction/arithmetic checks rather than manufacturing new student-performance scores.

    5. Materials, grouping and reported holdings¶

    Use K reference routines. 10/15/20/25 pupils form 5/7/10/12 active pair/trio teams; budget ⅝/10/13 kits, including one reserve at odd enrollment. Use no singleton "pair." Limited-kit groups <=3 require ⅘/7/9 kits with every child receiving a full decision/test turn.

    Exact quantities live in each lesson; do not issue nine generic kits to all pair-based lessons. Examples: 260 blocks for a full structure allocation; 15 germination cups/36 seeds; 4.2 L contained water; 104 child/teacher life-cycle cards. Timings include individual checks and cleanup, but freezing, charging, ongoing plant care and actual school hygiene rules require advance arrangements.

    The expanded catalog and material-usage index now identify every K primary term. Optional robots/hosts remain explicit in the lessons and technology plan, not mandatory primary-material purchases. Paper resources are assembled from separately priced stock; zero additional item-purchase cost is not free paper or zero teacher preparation. Budgets do not guarantee every K pair-based kit or all annual consumables for a 32-meeting year.

    Reported OLP holdings¶

    Preserved school report: 8 Snap Circuits STEM Classroom Activity Kits, item OHM-135. See reported-holdings data and the shared holdings record. Count, completeness, condition, location, manufacturer age/power guidance and school safety permission remain unverified. K uses only an optional preflighted adult demonstration in the light unit. No K student-circuit objective or loan requirement is inferred. Eight reports are not nine ready student stations.

    6. Sources and official mapping¶

    Local CST codes are stable program competencies, not official benchmarks. The external-candidate matrix verifies four exact 2018 arts identifiers and twelve planned task/evidence links across eleven K lessons against the state-agency workbook. MDE-copy corroboration, school review and actual mastery remain separate. No guessed science/math code replaces inaccessible final tables.

    Directly inspected content sources include NASA stars, USDA monarch biology, CCC 638-645, and the verified teaching register. Inherited unverified biographies/quotations were omitted from the 29 new minimum paths. Devotional tradition, classroom interpretation and scientific evidence are not interchangeable. The original growing references retain their verified UMN/USDA sources and ongoing-care preflight.

    7. Family communication¶

    Each rewritten reference includes a copy-ready concept/action/question and optional family conversation. Use only the lessons actually taught. Weather copy now distinguishes actual versus model records; privacy copy uses hypothetical examples; art copy explains choices; portfolios identify demonstrated skills rather than attendance. No purchase, donation, home device, parent teaching, private family disclosure or routine homework is required.

    8. Validation and remaining release gates¶

    The autonomous validator checked 29 new references, 37 new 25-minute meeting sequences and 360 class-size quantity comparisons. All 37 audit records are complete; frozen baseline judgments/paths/meetings/minutes match. Global map regeneration and publication checks are recorded in Validation. Source verification is not institutional approval.
