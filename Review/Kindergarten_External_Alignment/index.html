    Kindergarten external standards: source-verified candidates¶

    Checked October 4, 2026 for 2026-27. This is progress beyond topic-level alignment, not a school approval, observed mastery or complete subject crosswalk.

    Download the source-specific candidate links. The initial twelve arts links use four exact arts identifiers; the completion pass adds four national-CS links using two directly verified CSTA 2026 codes. Each record states the actual task, individual planned evidence and source/ authority boundary rather than claiming full subject coverage.

    What was actually verified¶

    The workbook was downloaded directly from Perpich, Minnesota's state arts education agency. Its key identifies grade 0 as Kindergarten and the identifier positions as arts area, grade, strand, anchor standard and benchmark. Visual arts is area 5; music is area 3. The VisualArts sheet's Kindergarten rows 7-19 and the Music sheet's Kindergarten rows were inspected directly.

    Exact identifier Worksheet / row Concise paraphrase of the inspected requirement
    5.0.2.3.1 MN2018ArtsEdStandardsVisualArts / 9 Make art that communicates an idea through artistic foundations.
    5.0.2.4.1 MN2018ArtsEdStandardsVisualArts / 11 Identify choices in one's own original artwork.
    5.0.4.8.1 MN2018ArtsEdStandardsVisualArts / 16 Describe distinctions between images and real objects.
    3.0.2.3.1 MN2018ArtsEdStandardsMusic / 8 Choose a musical sound to share.

    These identifiers and their Kindergarten placement were seen in the source, not manufactured from another grade or inferred from a topic. Use the source register for the verified 2018 edition's implementation status and source classification.

    What remains conditional¶

    The workbook itself says the MDE-hosted Excel is the official version. The directly inspected state-agency copy establishes its displayed codes and content; a final MDE-copy corroboration and school curriculum review remain required before a formal program-wide official-alignment claim.

    Each proposed link was matched to the rewritten lesson's actual directions and evidence check. It is not a claim that pupils already demonstrated the benchmark. A table row must meet its stated scope:

    • Original art requires a child-created or child-directed representation. Merely selecting a supplied picture does not prove art creation.
    • A stated choice must concern the child's own artwork. Counting points or watching a teacher is not sufficient.
    • Image/object distinctions are visual-arts evidence, not proof of full science-standard coverage.
    • The music link requires a student-directed choice and sharing of an actual musical sound. Silent gestures or only rests can satisfy the local pattern route but do not establish that external sound-sharing benchmark.

    The CSV status deliberately separates source verification from institutional review and classroom outcomes. The generated local-standard map retains its general external-alignment verification hold and links here for these narrowly checked candidates.

    National CS follow-up¶

    The current CSTA viewer directly supplied EK-ALG-IM-03 and EK-DAT-DC-08 in its PK/K placement. The first concerns the human process of naming a goal, breaking it into smaller steps and ordering them; the gratitude/service algorithm references now model and individually check that process explicitly. The second concerns collecting data to answer an age-appropriate question with two or three variables/attributes. Actual cloud-category/time records can evidence this target with teacher help; invented model records cannot. Neither code is a Minnesota adoption claim, a requirement for an iPad or evidence of executing computer code.

    Science and mathematics: no guessed identifiers¶

    Normal retrieval of the final MDE science and 2007 mathematics documents on October 4 returned browser-verification pages, not readable benchmark tables. A normal integrated-browser attempt did not provide readable PDF text; its request API was unavailable. No challenge bypass or invented code was used. The attempted Revisor 3501.0710 is marked repealed, so it was not substituted for a current Kindergarten table; the attempted science section 3501.0970 returned 404.

    Exact science/math identifiers therefore remain VERIFICATION REQUIRED. Obtain the final applicable documents through normal authorized access and match the exact task/evidence. The 2007 mathematics planning baseline and 2027-28 transition date remain as documented in the source register.

    Factual content checks beyond standards¶

    The autonomous pass also directly inspected NASA stars, USDA monarch biology and CCC 638-645. New lesson language paraphrases rather than reproduces them. Stars/models, four living biological stages and Resurrection distinctions are kept separate. Unverified inherited quotations/biographies were omitted from the 29 rewritten minimum pathways rather than left beside contradictory source gates.
