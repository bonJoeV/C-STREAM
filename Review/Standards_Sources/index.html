    Standards sources and implementation status¶

    Planning date and source access date: 2026-10-04. Target school year: 2026-27. All source entries record research on that date; the register distinguishes directly inspected text, indexed document references and failed retrievals. These are concise paraphrases, not reproductions of standards. Source years are edition labels; they are not necessarily adoption or implementation years.

    Classification and claim limits¶

    • Official standard: Minnesota's adopted subject standards. Their public-school implementation dates are a reference for this Catholic school's planning, not a finding that every public-school accountability requirement applies here.
    • Official guidance/teaching: MDE implementation guidance; Archdiocesan guidance or policy; Vatican/USCCB teaching. These are not interchangeable with numbered academic benchmarks.
    • National standard/framework: CSTA, NGSS, National Core Arts Standards and ISTE. Use selectively; no Minnesota or Archdiocesan adoption is inferred.
    • Local program standard: The eighteen CST-* competencies in Local Standards. Not MDE-approved, not Archdiocese-approved, and not a complete subject curriculum.

    Source verification does not establish lesson alignment. No external benchmark number is assigned to a lesson by this research. A claim requires the exact edition, applicable grade, verified benchmark, matching student task and assessment evidence. A topic resemblance or a local code is insufficient. All generated external-alignment fields remain VERIFICATION REQUIRED until that benchmark + lesson + individual assessment/evidence mapping is reviewed. A verified source, implementation date or local-code entry does not remove this hold.

    Minnesota: the 2026-27 baseline¶

    The directly inspected MDE newsletters and enacted rules establish these dates. Older projected transition timelines must not override the later confirmations. Assessment rollout dates are not substitutes for instruction implementation dates. In particular, the October 2023 newsletter called the social studies and math dates anticipated; the enacted rules and April 2026 confirmation, not that earlier forecast alone, establish the transition years used below.

    Catholic teaching: verified paragraph references¶

    All paragraphs below were seen on the official Vatican site, including page HTML where the simplified reader omitted body text. Classroom applications are local interpretations of teaching, not official academic standards.

    ID / classification Official URL and exact references, accessed 2026-10-04 Paraphrase / classroom application
    FAITH-01 / official Church teaching CCC: characteristics of faith, 159 Faith and reason are not rival sources of truth; legitimate scientific investigation need not contradict faith. Keep observations and explanations honest; do not use theology as an experimental result.
    FAITH-02 / official Church teaching CCC: the human person as God's image, 1702, 1704 Every person bears God's image; reason participates in understanding creation and seeking the good. Design for people's dignity and access, not their productivity or presumed ability.
    FAITH-03 / official Church teaching CCC: respect for personal dignity, 2293-2295 Science and technology serve human flourishing and are not morally neutral merely because they are efficient. Human experimentation has moral and consent limits. Classroom implication: safe models, privacy, no harmful experiments on classmates.
    FAITH-04 / official Church teaching CCC: persons and goods, 2415-2418 Respect creation and its resources, consider future generations, and avoid needless animal suffering. Reuse materials, minimize waste and observe living things responsibly.
    FAITH-05 / official Church teaching CCC: work and social justice, 2427-2428 Work proceeds from persons and can develop their gifts; people are not instruments of production. Value careful making, every honest vocation and service rather than speed or prestige.
    FAITH-06 / official Church teaching CCC: living in truth, 2468-2469 Truthfulness in actions and words sustains trust. Record actual results, acknowledge uncertainty and credit another person's idea.

    Legacy public-page claims: verification and safe wording¶

    This ledger supports qualification of shared home/README pages and related resources. It does not approve their legacy attributions or change external lesson-alignment fields. The local eighteen-code framework and twelve design principles are not an NCEA standards set, an empirically validated intervention or a Cognia certification.

The directly inspected NCEA Talk, STREAM in Action: Easy to Find is a contributed practitioner article about faith, service and STREAM, not the exact ten-item publication or a review of this curriculum.
