    School release checklist¶

    Repository artifact completion means a written pathway, traceable planned evidence and passing document checks. It does not establish physical kit condition, the school's permissions, individual access needs or actual learning. These are concrete checks the school can complete, not more curriculum reports to commission.

    Before the selected lesson¶

    Check Responsible role to assign Record before release
    Calendar and objective Grade teacher One schedule/rotation; actual date, native period, core objective and chosen physical/device/paper pathway.
    Counted kit Teacher/materials coordinator Actual location, issue/return quantities for the enrolled class, missing/damaged items and teacher-only tools. A catalogue name or report is not a count.
    Safe materials School safety lead/teacher Condition, allergies, ingredients, age guidance, water/hygiene, tool restrictions and activity-specific stop rules.
    Electrical pretest, if used Qualified school adult Matching model/manual/project, safe supply/covers/protection, correct approved diagram and successful inspection/test. No invented Snap component or battery allocation.
    Device/access pretest, if used School technology lead Approved actual host/app/version, privacy/account/accessibility settings, executable test and working turn plan.
    Individual access Teacher/support staff Reading, motor, sensory, language and physical accommodations preserving the target; consent for physical assistance.
    Prepared content Teacher Exact source/model-data/picture content, no unverified biography/quotation and no personal student data in fictional scenarios.
    Handoff Teacher/substitute Kit and lesson together, actual school contact, objective check, cleanup and fallback. First-use kit construction/charging/freezing happens beforehand.

    If an essential condition fails, hold that pathway. Use only the named alternative, state its changed objective and record deferred evidence. Paper reasoning cannot release actual software execution, robot operation, circuit construction, live observations or measured weather.

    Pilot evidence: collect, do not assume¶

    Pilot at least one younger-grade and one older-grade complete reference, then the next scheduled unit. Record:

    1. Actual first/repeat preparation and in-class transition/cleanup minutes.
    2. Correct material allocation and actual consumption/returns.
    3. Each child's objective-linked demonstration and support used.
    4. Whether every child received a meaningful choice/tool/test turn.
    5. Unexpected safety, accessibility, space or timing constraints.
    6. The one change needed before the next use.

    Compare the written threshold with the actual result: for example, a structure held ten seconds unaided, a two-command route was correctly predicted and debugged, or a child explained a recorded change using evidence. Do not replace a missing result with participation, photographs or a certificate. Use two dated individual observations where the local progression specifies an expected-mastery checkpoint.

    Source and institutional review¶

    • Confirm the school's 2026-27 mathematics baseline/transition decision.
    • Obtain school-issued OMCE religion/culture policies and applicable curriculum expectations through authorized school channels.
    • Obtain inaccessible final MDE benchmark tables before restoring exact science/mathematics/ELA/social-studies claims.
    • Corroborate state-agency arts copies against the MDE official edition for a formal institutional alignment claim.
    • Treat CSTA 2026 as a verified national framework, not automatically adopted Minnesota or Archdiocesan standards.
    • Use only the precise task/evidence scope of an external-candidate mapping. A source-verified identifier is not proof the pupil mastered it.

    The local C-STREAM competencies can guide teaching while these formal external claims remain withheld. They complement, not replace, primary subject curricula.

    Reported eight Snap Circuits kits¶

    The holding reports eight individual OHM-135 kits. Record actual manufacturer model, matching instructions, completeness, condition and age/power approval for each SC01-SC08. The classroom guide gives the bounded wave plan and evidence checks. An out-of-service kit is not a station; one wave cannot serve 25 pupils in eight groups of at most three. K remains optional adult-demonstration only.

    Release record to copy¶

    The machine-readable Teacher Release Record is a blank controlled template with the same fields. Do not put student names or identifying evidence in the public repository.

    Lesson / schedule / rotation:
Date / enrollment / native minutes:
Selected pathway / objective / individual evidence threshold:
Actual kit location / checker / check date:
Required items and teacher tools counted:
Safety, access and policy checks completed:
Pretest result and named fallback:
Objectives/evidence deferred if the pathway changes:
Pilot timing, individual evidence and next revision:
Teacher / school approver where required:

    Do not mark a blank release record approved. Record real school checks and revise the relevant lesson/kit after a failure. No commit, deployment, generic document score or schema pass substitutes for this evidence.
